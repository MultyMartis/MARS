    Our handy app:

    Freight transportation up to 1.5 t. is a perfect option if you plan to relocate, buy household appliances to your new apartment, or transport your sofa and bedside tables to your country house. Order a suitable vehicle in the Maxim service, and the driver will transport your things carefully.

    Available vehicles

    In our service, you can choose a vehicle for different kinds of freight: for example, we have a "Small body" rate in most cities. You can choose it if you need to pick up a microwave oven from the store. It will be cheaper than ordering a GAZelle.

    A "Standard body" rate involves freight transportation up to 1.5 tons. It will be a great help when you transport large furniture or a lot of stuff. These vehicles are 3 meters long, 1.9 meters wide and 2 meters high.

    In such a vehicle, you'll be able to transport a wardrobe, a sofa, a lot of bags with your stuff, or construction materials: laminate flooring, plasterboard, interior or entrance doors.

    Ordering freight transportation is cheap and easy

    When you browse through classifieds websites, you see the price difference: some people charge three times as much as the others do for the same work. It is often an hourly paid service, but it's not very convenient to use the timer on your smartphone, especially when you try to load your belongings as fast as you can.

    In the Maxim service, all the processes are automated, and the payment is hourly. When the driver arrives to the address, a timer starts in their app. You can order freight transportation at an affordable price. You pay only for the first hour of work, then the payment is per minute. You don't have to pay for the second hour if, for example, your ride took 75 minutes. When the driver completes the order, the final price will appear on the app screen.

    You don't have to agree upon the time for relocating in advance to make it convenient for the performer. Just specify the time you need, and the driver will be waiting for you at the appointed time. You can also place an urgent order since you may need freight transportation at any time — that's why we work 24/7.

    All the drivers undergo a check as well: we check their documents and vehicle condition. Non-runner vehicles in bad condition are not allowed to work.
