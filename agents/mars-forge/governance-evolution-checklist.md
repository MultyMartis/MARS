# Governance Evolution Checklist - MARS Forge

**Status:** **documented** - Forge overlay checklist for Website Factory governance evolution and self-refinement QA.  
**Not:** autonomous self-improving AI, runtime governance mutation, automatic process rewriting, universal evolution law, or perfect adaptability.

**Parent governance:** [`../../projects/mars-website-factory/governance-evolution-governance.md`](../../projects/mars-website-factory/governance-evolution-governance.md).  
**Self-refinement model:** [`../../projects/mars-website-factory/self-refinement-model.md`](../../projects/mars-website-factory/self-refinement-model.md).  
**Drift taxonomy:** [`../../projects/mars-website-factory/evolutionary-drift-taxonomy.md`](../../projects/mars-website-factory/evolutionary-drift-taxonomy.md).

## 1. When To Run

Run this checklist before report closure, handoff, governance review, continuity checkpoint, or methodology update when any of the following affect the scope:

- a governance method appears stale, rigid, duplicated, excessive, or under-protective;
- repeated findings suggest that the method itself needs refinement;
- old rules, legacy assumptions, or previous process decisions influence current work;
- a new governance layer, rule, checklist, report block, or cross-link is proposed;
- a rule is being clarified, narrowed, expanded, merged, split, deprecated, quarantined, or made optional;
- continuity, old report readability, or historical governance rationale may be affected;
- governance stagnation, fossilization, ossification, or uncontrolled mutation is suspected.

Record results as **GOVERNANCE EVOLUTION FINDINGS**.

---

## 2. Governance Evolution QA

- [ ] The governance issue is named: stagnation, bloat, ambiguity, under-protection, over-protection, legacy-rule pressure, or mutation risk.
- [ ] The evidence motivating refinement is identified: lesson, failure, repeated drift, operator friction, contradiction, or SAFE UNKNOWN.
- [ ] The current governance method's historical rationale is preserved or marked **SAFE UNKNOWN**.
- [ ] The proposed evolution is classified: preserve, clarify, refine, narrow, expand, split, merge, deprecate, quarantine, experiment, or SAFE UNKNOWN.
- [ ] The change is scoped to Website Factory, Forge overlay, project pack, report format, checklist item, or local experiment.
- [ ] The refinement does not claim autonomous self-improvement, runtime mutation, or universal evolution law.

---

## 3. Refinement Traceability QA

- [ ] The refinement states what it preserves, supersedes, narrows, expands, retires, or leaves unresolved.
- [ ] Source evidence and operational lesson are traceable enough for future review.
- [ ] The refinement explains why now, not only what changed.
- [ ] Future operators can understand the change without private chat memory.
- [ ] Old report/readme/checklist references remain understandable or receive minimal cross-links.
- [ ] Deprecation or quarantine is explicit when old governance should not remain active authority.

---

## 4. Continuity-Safe Change QA

- [ ] Historical continuity is preserved across old rule, rationale, new method, and future use.
- [ ] Older reports and findings remain interpretable after the refinement.
- [ ] The change does not silently break source-lineage, organizational-memory, temporal-evolution, or workflow continuity.
- [ ] Governance renewal does not erase prior lessons.
- [ ] Continuity-breaking redesign is avoided unless explicitly human-approved and documented.
- [ ] SAFE UNKNOWN is recorded when continuity impact cannot be proven.

---

## 5. Methodology Review QA

- [ ] The rule still solves a real risk, or deprecation/quarantine is considered.
- [ ] The rule does not duplicate another governance layer without adding distinct evidence.
- [ ] The method remains proportionate to risk and operator capacity.
- [ ] Optional-depth, escalation-only, or adaptive-review treatment is considered before adding mandatory process.
- [ ] Governance minimalism, adaptive governance, organizational memory, and temporal evolution findings remain separate when relevant.
- [ ] "Old process therefore correct" and "new process therefore better" are both rejected.

---

## 6. Adaptive Survivability QA

- [ ] Governance remains stable enough to preserve trust and flexible enough to improve.
- [ ] Frozen methodology is not protected when evidence shows fragility or obsolescence.
- [ ] Methodological change is not allowed to become uncontrolled churn.
- [ ] Operator readability improves or remains stable after refinement.
- [ ] The governance-to-value ratio remains healthy.
- [ ] The refinement can survive handoff, compression, future review, and long-term use.

---

## 7. Historical-Lineage QA

- [ ] The historical source of the rule, lesson, or method is identified when material.
- [ ] Active, stale, superseded, deprecated, experimental, and unknown governance states are not blended.
- [ ] Prior rationale remains findable when the method changes.
- [ ] Legacy rules are not inherited as current authority without review.
- [ ] Historical-lineage disruption is named if old governance context cannot be reconstructed.

---

## 8. Drift Classification

Classify any issue using [`evolutionary-drift-taxonomy.md`](../../projects/mars-website-factory/evolutionary-drift-taxonomy.md):

- [ ] Governance stagnation.
- [ ] Methodology fossilization.
- [ ] Legacy-rule accumulation.
- [ ] Anti-evolution drift.
- [ ] Frozen-process decay.
- [ ] Institutional rigidity.
- [ ] Self-protection governance.
- [ ] Uncontrolled governance mutation.
- [ ] Continuity-breaking evolution.
- [ ] Refinement opacity.
- [ ] Governance ossification.
- [ ] Adaptive survivability erosion.
- [ ] Historical-lineage disruption.

## 9. Reporting Block

Use this block in Forge reports when governance evolution is in scope:

```text
GOVERNANCE EVOLUTION FINDINGS - <scope / method / rule>

Evolution trigger:
- <lesson / repeated drift / friction / stale rule / method change / SAFE UNKNOWN>

Refinement classification:
- preserve | clarify | refine | narrow | expand | split | merge | deprecate | quarantine | experiment | SAFE UNKNOWN

Continuity-safe change:
- Preserves:
- Supersedes / changes:
- Historical rationale:
- Old-report compatibility:

Self-refinement model layer:
- stable-foundation | adaptive-refinement | lesson-driven-evolution | governance-review | historical-survivability | controlled-change | continuity-preserving

Evolutionary drift taxonomy:
- Patterns:
- Severity:
- Continuity / methodology / survivability impact:

Disposition:
- PASS | PARTIAL | FAIL | SAFE UNKNOWN | HITL REQUIRED | BLOCKED
- Action: preserve | clarify | refine | deprecate | quarantine | experiment | escalate | stop
- Evidence / unknowns:
```

Keep this separate from `GOVERNANCE MINIMALISM FINDINGS`, `ADAPTIVE GOVERNANCE FINDINGS`, `ORGANIZATIONAL MEMORY FINDINGS`, `TEMPORAL EVOLUTION FINDINGS`, `WORKFLOW DISCIPLINE FINDINGS`, and `REASONING VISIBILITY FINDINGS`.

---

## 10. SAFE UNKNOWN

Record **SAFE UNKNOWN** when:

- the refinement source cannot be named;
- historical rationale is missing;
- governance authority cannot be established;
- continuity impact is unreviewed;
- old rule validity is unclear;
- mutation risk cannot be assessed;
- proportionality is unproven;
- future report compatibility is unknown.

**Action:** state what evolution evidence is missing, what would restore traceability, and whether the method should be preserved, clarified, refined, deprecated, quarantined, treated as an experiment, escalated, blocked, or marked SAFE UNKNOWN.

---

## 11. Non-Goals

- Do not redesign Triumph or any other project.
- Do not invent autonomous self-improving AI.
- Do not create runtime governance mutation.
- Do not define universal evolution laws.
- Do not claim perfect adaptability.
- Do not treat governance growth as proof of maturity.
- Do not treat old methodology as automatically correct.
