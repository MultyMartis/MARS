# Operator Decision Model v1

## Status

Manual decision-support model. Not autonomous decision-making, automation, strategic certainty, or pseudo-intelligence.

## Purpose

Help a PPC operator decide what to review, what to act on, what to defer, and when to escalate.

## Decision Priorities

1. High-impact risks first.
2. Semantic cleanliness.
3. Landing clarity.
4. Trust alignment.
5. Mobile usability.
6. Operational simplicity.
7. Realistic review scope.

## Decision Inputs

- evidence strength;
- contradiction level;
- commercial impact;
- operator capacity;
- time sensitivity;
- uncertainty level;
- escalation need.

## Required Decision Discipline

- ORCA does not make business decisions.
- Evidence can be incomplete.
- Uncertainty is normal.
- Operator judgment matters.
- Operational tradeoffs exist.
- Perfect certainty is impossible.
- Escalation is healthy.
- Simplicity often beats theoretical perfection.

## Boundary

This model rejects autonomous decision-making, fake confidence, unsupported strategic certainty, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
