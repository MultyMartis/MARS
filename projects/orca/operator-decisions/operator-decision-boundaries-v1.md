# Operator Decision Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA Operator Decision Layer may help a human operator structure PPC review decisions.

It does not make business decisions, approve strategy, allocate budget, diagnose root causes, or optimize campaigns autonomously.

## Explicitly Rejected

- autonomous decision-making;
- fake confidence;
- unsupported strategic certainty;
- pseudo-intelligence narratives;
- AI reasoning engine claims;
- automation, orchestration, telemetry, dashboards, or runtime systems;
- decisions disguised as documentation.

## Allowed Scope

- PPC operator decisions;
- low-evidence decision handling;
- uncertainty handling;
- operational tradeoffs;
- evidence weighting;
- escalation logic;
- decision fatigue awareness;
- practical prioritization.

## Required Decision Discipline

- ORCA does not make business decisions.
- Evidence can be incomplete.
- Uncertainty is normal.
- Operator judgment matters.
- Operational tradeoffs exist.
- Perfect certainty is impossible.
- Escalation is healthy.
- Simplicity often beats theoretical perfection.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know business priority, acceptable risk, legal tolerance, operational capacity, true conversion impact, lead quality, or budget constraints unless verified by responsible humans.
