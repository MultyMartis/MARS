# Decision Fatigue Rules v1

## Status

Manual fatigue rules. Not productivity monitoring, telemetry, or workflow automation.

## Fatigue Signals

- operator cannot name the top risk;
- notes grow but decisions do not improve;
- weak evidence is treated as strong;
- contradictions are ignored;
- escalation is delayed;
- review scope keeps expanding;
- theory replaces practical action.

## Response

- stop the review;
- state the top risk;
- state the evidence quality;
- mark uncertainty;
- escalate if needed;
- defer low-impact issues;
- simplify the next action.

## Decision Safety

Do not make high-impact decisions while fatigued unless the risk is urgent and escalation is unavailable. Record uncertainty clearly.

## Required Decision Discipline

ORCA does not make business decisions. Evidence can be incomplete, uncertainty is normal, operator judgment matters, and escalation is healthy.

## Boundary

These rules reject fake confidence, theoretical perfection, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
