# Decision Quality Checklist v1

## Status

Manual checklist. Not autonomous approval or decision scoring.

## Checklist

- high-impact risks reviewed first;
- semantic cleanliness considered;
- landing clarity considered;
- trust alignment considered;
- mobile usability considered;
- operational simplicity considered;
- review scope stayed realistic;
- evidence quality stated;
- uncertainty stated;
- contradictions stated;
- escalation considered.

## Decision Quality Questions

- Does the action match the evidence?
- Are assumptions labeled?
- Could weak evidence mislead?
- Is the decision practical?
- Would more review change the action?

## Required Decision Discipline

ORCA does not make business decisions. Evidence can be incomplete, uncertainty is normal, operator judgment matters, and escalation is healthy.

## Boundary

This checklist rejects fake confidence, unsupported strategic certainty, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
