# Low-Evidence Checklist v1

## Status

Manual checklist. Not confidence scoring, automation, or decision approval.

## Low-Evidence Checks

- source data is incomplete;
- sample is thin;
- observation is isolated;
- assumptions are doing the work;
- contradictions reduce confidence;
- business constraints are unknown;
- action would be hard to reverse;
- escalation may be needed.

## Allowed Response

- mark SAFE UNKNOWN;
- verify evidence;
- stop review;
- escalate;
- take only reversible low-risk action;
- defer low-impact decisions.

## Avoid

- fake confidence;
- unsupported attribution;
- major strategic changes;
- autonomous decision-making;
- pseudo-intelligence narratives.

## Required Decision Discipline

ORCA does not make business decisions. Evidence can be incomplete, uncertainty is normal, operator judgment matters, and perfect certainty is impossible.

## Boundary

Low evidence should reduce confidence, not inspire sophisticated-sounding conclusions.
