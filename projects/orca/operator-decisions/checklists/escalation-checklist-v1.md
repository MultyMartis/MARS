# Escalation Checklist v1

## Status

Manual checklist. Not automated routing, approval, or business decision-making.

## Escalate If

- high-impact risk exists;
- evidence is incomplete;
- contradictions reduce confidence;
- assumptions are dangerous;
- spend, brand, policy, legal, or operational capacity is affected;
- operator authority is insufficient;
- decision fatigue is visible;
- action risk and delay risk both matter.

## Escalation Note Must Include

- decision needed;
- evidence;
- uncertainty;
- contradictions;
- risk of action;
- risk of delay;
- recommended human owner;
- SAFE_UNKNOWN.

## Do Not Escalate For

- cosmetic observations;
- low-impact noise;
- methodology completeness;
- theory-only improvements.

## Required Decision Discipline

ORCA does not make business decisions. Evidence can be incomplete, uncertainty is normal, operator judgment matters, and escalation is healthy.

## Boundary

This checklist rejects autonomous decision-making, fake confidence, unsupported strategic certainty, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
