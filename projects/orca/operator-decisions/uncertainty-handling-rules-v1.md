# Uncertainty Handling Rules v1

## Status

Manual uncertainty rules. Not automated confidence scoring or autonomous decision-making.

## When Evidence Is Weak

Evidence is weak when:

- the source is incomplete;
- the observation is isolated;
- assumptions drive the conclusion;
- contradictions are unresolved;
- commercial impact is unclear;
- the operator cannot explain the action.

## When Review Should Stop

Stop when:

- more review will not change action;
- evidence requires external verification;
- the operator is repeating uncertainty;
- decision fatigue is visible;
- theoretical perfection is replacing practical judgment.

## When Escalation Is Needed

Escalate when:

- risk is high-impact and evidence is incomplete;
- contradictions reduce confidence;
- legal, policy, brand, budget, or operational capacity questions appear;
- the decision exceeds operator authority.

## Required Decision Discipline

ORCA does not make business decisions. Evidence can be incomplete. Uncertainty is normal. Operator judgment matters, escalation is healthy, and perfect certainty is impossible.

## Boundary

These rules reject fake confidence, unsupported strategic certainty, and assumptions disguised as evidence.
