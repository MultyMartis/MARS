# Escalation Rules v1

## Status

Manual escalation rules. Not automated approval, routing, or decision-making.

## Escalate When

- high-impact risk has incomplete evidence;
- contradictions reduce confidence;
- assumptions are dangerous;
- spend, policy, brand, legal, or operational capacity is affected;
- business authority is required;
- operator fatigue reduces decision quality;
- perfect certainty is impossible but action still matters.

## Do Not Escalate For

- low-impact cosmetic observations;
- theory-only improvements;
- unsupported speculation;
- questions that can be answered by a short human review;
- methodology completeness.

## Required Decision Discipline

ORCA does not make business decisions. Evidence can be incomplete, uncertainty is normal, operator judgment matters, and escalation is healthy.

## Boundary

These rules reject autonomous decision-making, fake confidence, unsupported strategic certainty, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
