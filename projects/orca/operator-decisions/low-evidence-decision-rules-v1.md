# Low-Evidence Decision Rules v1

## Status

Manual low-evidence rules. Not automated confidence scoring or autonomous decision-making.

## Low-Evidence Conditions

- source data is thin;
- SERP review is incomplete;
- search terms are too few;
- landing review is only partial;
- business constraints are unknown;
- contradictions reduce confidence;
- operator assumptions are doing most of the work.

## Allowed Low-Evidence Decisions

- mark SAFE UNKNOWN;
- request human verification;
- make a reversible low-risk adjustment;
- defer the decision;
- escalate high-impact risk.

## Avoid Low-Evidence Decisions

- major structural changes;
- strategic claims;
- root-cause certainty;
- unsupported attribution;
- budget or business conclusions;
- fake confidence.

## Required Decision Discipline

ORCA does not make business decisions. Evidence can be incomplete. Uncertainty is normal. Perfect certainty is impossible, but assumptions are dangerous when unlabeled.

## Boundary

These rules reject unsupported strategic certainty, pseudo-intelligence narratives, and fake confidence under weak evidence.
