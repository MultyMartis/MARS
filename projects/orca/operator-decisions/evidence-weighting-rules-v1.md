# Evidence Weighting Rules v1

## Status

Manual evidence rules. Not automated scoring, telemetry, or intelligence ranking.

## Stronger Evidence

- repeated search-term mismatch;
- visible SERP dominance or pressure;
- clear ad-to-landing contradiction;
- obvious mobile friction;
- verified business constraint;
- direct operator observation;
- human-reviewed performance or lead-quality evidence.

## Weaker Evidence

- one-off observations;
- cosmetic differences;
- unsupported assumptions;
- speculative competitor narratives;
- incomplete screenshots;
- low-impact wording differences;
- contradictions not yet resolved.

## Weighting Rules

- high-impact risk with strong evidence can justify action;
- high-impact risk with weak evidence should usually escalate or verify;
- low-impact risk with weak evidence should usually stop;
- contradictions reduce confidence;
- assumptions must be labeled.

## Required Decision Discipline

ORCA does not make business decisions. Evidence can be incomplete, uncertainty is normal, operator judgment matters, and operational tradeoffs exist.

## Boundary

These rules reject fake confidence, unsupported strategic certainty, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
