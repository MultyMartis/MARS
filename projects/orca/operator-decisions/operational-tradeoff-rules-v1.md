# Operational Tradeoff Rules v1

## Status

Manual tradeoff rules. Not autonomous strategy, optimization engine, or business decision system.

## Core Tradeoffs

- speed vs depth;
- segmentation vs simplicity;
- trust vs pricing;
- breadth vs cleanliness;
- evidence quality vs action speed;
- operational realism vs theory.

## Tradeoff Discipline

- choose speed when the risk is low and action is reversible;
- choose depth when risk, spend, brand, policy, or lead quality impact is high;
- choose simplicity when segmentation creates operator overload;
- choose cleanliness when broad reach creates semantic pollution;
- choose escalation when authority or evidence is insufficient.

## Evidence Questions

- What evidence supports this tradeoff?
- What evidence is missing?
- What contradiction reduces confidence?
- What happens if action is delayed?
- What happens if action is wrong?

## Required Decision Discipline

ORCA does not make business decisions. Evidence can be incomplete, operator judgment matters, operational tradeoffs exist, and simplicity often beats theoretical perfection.

## Boundary

These rules reject unsupported strategic certainty, fake optimization certainty, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
