# ORCA Operator Decision Layer v1

## Purpose

This layer helps a human PPC operator make practical review decisions under uncertainty.

It supports prioritization, evidence weighting, low-evidence handling, tradeoff review, escalation, and decision fatigue awareness.

## What This Layer Is

- Practical PPC decision support.
- Human-supervised evidence discipline.
- Operational tradeoff framing.
- Lightweight escalation aid.
- Decision-quality checklist layer.

## What This Layer Is Not

- Not autonomous decision-making.
- Not an AI reasoning engine.
- Not automation, orchestration, telemetry, dashboards, or runtime systems.
- Not strategic certainty.
- Not pseudo-intelligence.

## Required Decision Discipline

- ORCA does not make business decisions.
- Evidence can be incomplete.
- Uncertainty is normal.
- Operator judgment matters.
- Operational tradeoffs exist.
- Perfect certainty is impossible.
- Escalation is healthy.
- Simplicity often beats theoretical perfection.
