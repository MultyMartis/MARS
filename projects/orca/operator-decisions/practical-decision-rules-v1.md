# Practical Decision Rules v1

## Status

Manual practical rules. Not autonomous business decision-making, automation, or optimization certainty.

## Practical Decision Tests

- Is the issue commercially relevant?
- Is there enough evidence for action or escalation?
- Does the action reduce PPC waste or improve alignment?
- Is the scope realistic for the operator?
- Is the decision simpler than the theory around it?

## Prefer Practical Actions

- clean obvious semantic pollution;
- clarify landing mismatch;
- align CTA with user intent;
- support trust claims;
- reduce mobile friction;
- simplify excessive segmentation;
- escalate when evidence or authority is insufficient.

## Avoid

- theoretical perfection;
- unsupported strategic certainty;
- over-segmentation for its own sake;
- long reviews that do not change action;
- decisions made to satisfy methodology instead of PPC reality.

## Required Decision Discipline

ORCA does not make business decisions. Evidence can be incomplete, uncertainty is normal, operator judgment matters, and simplicity often beats theoretical perfection.

## Boundary

These rules reject autonomous decision-making, fake confidence, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
