# Decision Priority Rules v1

## Status

Manual priority rules. Not automated ranking, optimization, or business decision-making.

## Prioritize First

- high-impact risks;
- semantic cleanliness;
- landing clarity;
- trust alignment;
- mobile usability;
- operational simplicity;
- realistic review scope.

## Prioritization Test

Ask:

- Could this risk waste spend or degrade lead quality?
- Is evidence strong enough to act or escalate?
- Does the issue affect commercial intent?
- Will more review improve the decision?
- Is the operator drifting into theoretical perfection?

## Lower Priority

- cosmetic issues;
- weak unsupported assumptions;
- low-impact differences;
- theoretical structure improvements;
- review work that increases complexity without improving action.

## Required Decision Discipline

ORCA does not make business decisions. Evidence can be incomplete, uncertainty is normal, operator judgment matters, and simplicity often beats theoretical perfection.

## Boundary

These rules reject fake confidence, unsupported strategic certainty, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
