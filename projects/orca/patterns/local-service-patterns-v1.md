# Local Service Patterns v1

## Purpose

Documents recurring local service PPC patterns for ORCA research, semantics, and QA.

## Common Niches

- Cargo and moving.
- Home repair and installation.
- Emergency plumbing, locksmith, electrical, and appliance repair.
- Clinics and diagnostics.
- Beauty and wellness appointments.
- Legal and accounting consultations.
- Cleaning and maintenance.

## Recurring Search Patterns

- service + city.
- service + district or metro.
- service + near me.
- urgent + service.
- service + price.
- service + reviews.
- service + home visit.

## PPC Architecture Notes

- Separate city and district clusters when SERP or landing differs.
- Keep emergency clusters separate.
- Use local proof in ads and landing pages.
- Watch aggregator and map pack pressure.
- Do not mix B2B facility services with household service demand.

## Boundary

These patterns support manual PPC architecture. They do not automate targeting, bidding, local ranking, campaign publishing, or optimization.
