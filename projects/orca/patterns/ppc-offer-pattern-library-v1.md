# PPC Offer Pattern Library v1

## Purpose

Documents recurring PPC offer patterns for service-business campaign planning.

## Offer Patterns

- Speed-first - urgent, same-day, 24/7, emergency response.
- Price-first - from-price, calculator, fixed price, free estimate.
- Trust-first - reviews, licenses, guarantee, cases, years in market.
- Local-first - nearby, city-specific, district-specific, local team.
- Convenience-first - online booking, messenger, home visit, pickup.
- Expertise-first - certified specialist, complex cases, premium service.
- Aggregator-alternative - direct provider, no middleman, transparent team.

## QA Use

- Match offer pattern to query intent.
- Check whether landing page proves the promise.
- Avoid unsupported claims in ad copy.
- Separate clusters when different offer patterns require different messages.

## Risks

- Speed claims without operational capacity.
- Price claims without transparent details.
- Trust claims without proof.
- Local claims without local presence.
- Convenience claims with high lead form friction.

## Boundary

Offer patterns guide human-reviewed PPC messaging. They do not generate autonomous ads, optimize campaigns, or manage bids.
