# SERP Pattern Library v1

## Purpose

Documents recurring SERP patterns used by ORCA for PPC research and QA. Patterns guide human analysis; they are not automated detection or optimization rules.

## Patterns

### Local Pack Domination

Maps or local cards take major attention. Common in repairs, clinics, moving, emergency, beauty, and home services.

PPC implication: landing needs local trust, phone visibility, reviews, and service area proof.

### Aggregator Domination

Aggregators, directories, marketplaces, or Yandex ecosystem surfaces occupy multiple results.

PPC implication: direct advertiser needs stronger offer clarity, trust, and specificity.

### Price-Sensitive SERP

Ads and snippets emphasize price, calculator, discount, or quote.

PPC implication: price queries need transparent pricing or quote logic.

### Emergency SERP

Urgent wording, 24/7 claims, same-day promises, and call CTAs dominate.

PPC implication: only use urgent clusters if operations can fulfill urgency.

### Trust-Heavy SERP

Reviews, certifications, guarantees, years of experience, and proof dominate.

PPC implication: weak landing trust is a blocker.

## Boundary

Pattern use remains human-supervised. ORCA does not automatically classify, bid, publish, or optimize campaigns from patterns.
