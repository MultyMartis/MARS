# Aggregator Detection Patterns v1

## Purpose

Documents signals that a SERP is dominated by aggregators, directories, marketplaces, or platform surfaces. This helps human PPC strategy review.

## Detection Signals

- Multiple directory or marketplace domains above direct providers.
- Yandex Services, Maps, Market, or similar platform blocks dominate attention.
- Organic results list provider rankings, catalog pages, or comparison pages.
- Ads emphasize provider choice rather than direct service delivery.
- Review and rating widgets are more visible than individual business pages.

## PPC Implications

- Direct advertisers need strong differentiation and trust proof.
- Price and review expectations may be higher.
- Landing pages must quickly explain why direct contact is better.
- Generic service keywords may be expensive or low-quality.
- Long-tail local and urgent queries may be cleaner.

## QA Questions

- Can a direct service landing compete with aggregator trust?
- Is the offer specific enough?
- Should aggregator-style research queries be excluded?
- Does the SERP suggest separate comparison intent clusters?

## Boundary

Aggregator detection is manual research intelligence. It is not automated scraping, ranking monitoring, bidding logic, or campaign optimization.
