# ORCA Doc Map v1

## Core

- `README.md` - main ORCA entry point.
- `system-overview.md` - purpose, layers, supervision, and boundaries.
- `phase-1-architecture.md` - Phase 1 architecture and exclusions.
- `current-state-v1.md` - concise current-state map.
- `operator-entrypoints-v1.md` - practical operator starting points.

## Agents

- `agents/` - documented role definitions for ORCA support agents.

## Workflows

- `workflows/` - documented workflows for SERP research, semantics, campaign generation, export, and QA.

## Contracts

- `contracts/` - handoff contracts for project input, SERP output, semantic clusters, campaign structure, and export packages.

## Research

- `research/` - SERP, local SERP, competitor intelligence, review analysis, and snapshot methodology.

## Standards

- `standards/` - ORCA normative standards (ORCA-RS-*).
- `orca-standards-register-v1.md` - standards index.
- **ORCA-RS-001** - Executive Research Publication Standard (ACTIVE).

## Semantic

- `semantic/` - intent classification, clustering, geo modifiers, negative keywords, and semantic quality.

## QA

- `qa/` - semantic, campaign structure, landing match, and ad relevance QA.

## Methodology

- `methodology/` - PPC research flow, campaign architecture, commercial intent, offer, and landing analysis.

## Patterns

- `patterns/` - local-service, SERP, offer, and aggregator pattern libraries.

## Pilot Cases

- `pilot-cases/` - pilot templates, execution flow, reports, and bounded pilot records.

## Observations

- `observations/` - observation model, normalization, SERP, semantic, landing, regional, and confidence rules.

## Evidence

- `evidence/` - evidence discipline, strength, source reliability, traceability, and human validation rules.
- SAFE UNKNOWN: no separate `evidence-records/` directory is present.

## Live Observations

- `live-observations/` - manual live observation workflow, SERP/local-pack/landing methods, checklists, templates, and examples.

## Heuristics

- `heuristics/` - strategic heuristic model, evidence, validation, confidence, and expiration rules.
- `heuristic-mapping/` - evidence-to-heuristic links, contradiction-aware mapping, revision, expiration, and stability.

## Review

- `review/` - operational, methodology, heuristic, evidence, pilot, usefulness, anti-mythology, drift, and commercial reality review.

## Boundary

This map links documentation only. It does not imply production readiness, runtime orchestration, autonomous intelligence, live market validation, automation, scraping, or telemetry.
