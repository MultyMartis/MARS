# PPC Failure Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA PPC Failure Patterns may help a human operator notice possible failure patterns for review.

It does not diagnose root causes, prove attribution, optimize campaigns, or make autonomous decisions.

## Explicitly Rejected

- autonomous diagnosis;
- fake root-cause certainty;
- unsupported attribution claims;
- fake optimization certainty;
- AI reasoning engine claims;
- automation, orchestration, telemetry, dashboards, or runtime systems;
- fake intelligence narratives.

## Allowed Scope

- PPC failure detection support;
- semantic failure awareness;
- landing mismatch awareness;
- trust-collapse awareness;
- operator overload awareness;
- mobile-friction awareness;
- low-quality-lead awareness;
- commercial realism review.

## Required Reality Discipline

- PPC failure is contextual.
- Evidence quality matters.
- Human review is mandatory.
- Failures can have multiple causes.
- Weak evidence can mislead.
- Fake certainty is dangerous.
- Operational simplicity matters.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know true root cause, conversion impact, lead quality, customer intent, auction dynamics, or competitor performance unless verified through reliable human-reviewed evidence.
