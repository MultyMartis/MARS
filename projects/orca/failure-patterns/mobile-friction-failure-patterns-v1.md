# Mobile Friction Failure Patterns v1

## Status

Manual mobile friction guide. Not automated UX scoring, device telemetry, or conversion proof.

## Purpose

Identify mobile experiences that may block practical PPC action, especially for urgent or local services.

## Failure Patterns

- major mobile friction;
- CTA hidden below high-friction content;
- phone or form path hard to find;
- slow or cluttered above-the-fold experience;
- trust proof not visible before action;
- pricing or service clarity missing on mobile;
- landing mismatch amplified by small-screen layout.

## Evidence To Look For

- user cannot act quickly on mobile;
- tap targets or forms are hard to use;
- urgent-intent path is delayed;
- contact options disappear or compete with clutter;
- key service or geo information is not visible.

## Required Reality Discipline

- PPC failure is contextual.
- Evidence quality matters.
- Human review is mandatory.
- Mobile friction can overlap with trust, landing, and urgency issues.
- Weak evidence can mislead.
- Fake certainty is dangerous.
- Operational simplicity matters.

## Boundary

This guide rejects autonomous diagnosis, unsupported conversion claims, and fake mobile optimization certainty.
