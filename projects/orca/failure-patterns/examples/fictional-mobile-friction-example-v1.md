## Status

Fictional demonstration only. Non-production. Non-validated. Not UX proof or conversion proof.

## Context

- market: fictional emergency plumbing;
- query cluster: fictional same-day leak repair terms;
- review type: manual failure-pattern example.

## Candidate Failure Pattern

The fictional mobile page delays urgent action even though the query and ad imply fast response.

## Evidence

- phone CTA is not visible above the fold;
- form has too many required fields for urgent intent;
- trust proof appears below long generic content;
- service area is unclear on mobile.

## Possible Causes

- mobile friction;
- fake urgency;
- weak landing clarity;
- geo mismatch;
- trust collapse.

## Operator Action

Review mobile CTA visibility, shorten the urgent contact path, clarify service area, and verify with human mobile review.
