# Semantic Failure Patterns v1

## Status

Manual semantic failure guide. Not NLP automation, AI reasoning, or autonomous campaign diagnosis.

## Purpose

Identify semantic patterns that may send PPC traffic toward the wrong intent, service, or commercial expectation.

## Failure Patterns

- broad semantic pollution;
- low-commercial-intent contamination;
- wrong-service traffic;
- job, DIY, tutorial, or definition intent leakage;
- location ambiguity;
- urgent-intent mismatch;
- query meaning that conflicts with landing clarity.

## Evidence To Look For

- repeated search terms that do not match the paid service;
- SERP results showing a different dominant meaning;
- ad group language mixing unrelated intent;
- landing page copy that does not resolve the query meaning.

## Required Reality Discipline

- PPC failure is contextual.
- Evidence quality matters.
- Human review is mandatory.
- Semantic failure can combine with landing, offer, or geo issues.
- Weak evidence can mislead.
- Fake certainty is dangerous.
- Operational simplicity matters.

## Boundary

Do not claim semantic root cause automatically. This guide rejects unsupported attribution and fake optimization certainty.
