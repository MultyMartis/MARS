# Operator Overload Patterns v1

## Status

Manual operator overload guide. Not productivity telemetry, monitoring, or workflow automation.

## Purpose

Identify when PPC review complexity harms operator judgment, speed, and decision quality.

## Failure Patterns

- operator overload from excessive fragmentation;
- too many low-value observations;
- review scope grows without better decisions;
- weak evidence receives too much attention;
- contradictory notes are not resolved;
- fake sophistication hides simple risks;
- fatigue leads to unclear actions.

## Evidence To Look For

- operator cannot state the top risk quickly;
- documents grow but decisions do not improve;
- review repeats the same uncertainty;
- low-impact details dominate high-impact risks;
- escalation is avoided when evidence is weak.

## Required Reality Discipline

- PPC failure is contextual.
- Evidence quality matters.
- Human review is mandatory.
- Operator overload can amplify semantic, landing, and trust failures.
- Weak evidence can mislead.
- Fake certainty is dangerous.
- Operational simplicity matters.

## Boundary

This guide rejects fake methodology depth, unsupported diagnosis, and complexity as a substitute for judgment.
