# Trust Collapse Patterns v1

## Status

Manual trust failure guide. Not reputation intelligence, automated verification, or proof of user trust behavior.

## Purpose

Identify visible trust gaps that may weaken PPC response or make a landing page feel unsupported.

## Failure Patterns

- trust collapse between ad promise and landing proof;
- review or rating absence where competitors show strong proof;
- unsupported guarantee, license, or certification claims;
- weak local credibility;
- contact path looks risky or unclear;
- pricing mismatch reduces trust;
- fake urgency without proof.

## Evidence To Look For

- competitor SERP results show stronger trust mechanics;
- landing page makes claims without proof nearby;
- urgent CTA appears before basic credibility support;
- contact details are unclear or inconsistent;
- service claims exceed visible evidence.

## Required Reality Discipline

- PPC failure is contextual.
- Evidence quality matters.
- Human review is mandatory.
- Trust collapse can overlap with pricing, landing, or offer mismatch.
- Weak evidence can mislead.
- Fake certainty is dangerous.
- Operational simplicity matters.

## Boundary

Do not claim actual user distrust without evidence. This guide rejects unsupported attribution and fake optimization certainty.
