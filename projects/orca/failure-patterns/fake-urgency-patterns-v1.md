# Fake Urgency Patterns v1

## Status

Manual urgency failure guide. Not conversion prediction, persuasion scoring, or autonomous diagnosis.

## Purpose

Identify cases where urgency language is used without operational support, trust proof, or landing clarity.

## Failure Patterns

- fake urgency unsupported by response capability;
- urgent CTA without fast contact path;
- emergency language on a generic landing page;
- pricing mismatch against urgent promise;
- trust proof missing near urgent claims;
- mobile friction blocking urgent action;
- geo mismatch for urgent local intent.

## Evidence To Look For

- "call now" or emergency claims without visible phone path;
- response-time promise with no operational proof;
- urgent ad sends to slow or generic page;
- competitors support urgency more clearly;
- user must scroll or search to act.

## Required Reality Discipline

- PPC failure is contextual.
- Evidence quality matters.
- Human review is mandatory.
- Fake urgency can combine with mobile, trust, or geo failure.
- Weak evidence can mislead.
- Fake certainty is dangerous.
- Operational simplicity matters.

## Boundary

Do not claim urgency caused failure by itself. This guide rejects unsupported attribution and fake optimization certainty.
