# Low-Quality Lead Patterns v1

## Status

Manual low-quality lead awareness guide. Not lead scoring, CRM telemetry, or proof of lead quality.

## Purpose

Identify PPC patterns that may attract poor-fit leads, wrong-service requests, or low-commercial-intent traffic.

## Failure Patterns

- low-commercial-intent contamination;
- wrong-service traffic;
- broad semantic pollution;
- pricing mismatch;
- geo mismatch;
- weak landing clarity;
- fake urgency that attracts unqualified demand;
- trust gaps that push good-fit users away.

## Evidence To Look For

- search terms suggest research, jobs, DIY, or unrelated services;
- inquiries do not match the paid offer;
- pricing expectations conflict with landing or ad claims;
- service area is unclear;
- lead notes contradict campaign intent.

## Required Reality Discipline

- PPC failure is contextual.
- Evidence quality matters.
- Human review is mandatory.
- Low-quality leads can have multiple causes.
- Weak evidence can mislead.
- Fake certainty is dangerous.
- Operational simplicity matters.

## Boundary

Do not infer true lead quality without reliable human-reviewed evidence. This guide rejects unsupported attribution and fake optimization certainty.
