# Over-Segmentation Failure Patterns v1

## Status

Manual segmentation failure guide. Not automated account restructuring or optimization certainty.

## Purpose

Identify when PPC structure becomes too fragmented for practical operator control or evidence quality.

## Failure Patterns

- excessive fragmentation across campaigns or ad groups;
- thin evidence per segment;
- duplicated intent split into too many units;
- inconsistent CTA or landing alignment;
- operator overload from too many review surfaces;
- semantic cleanliness reduced by structural complexity;
- reporting noise mistaken for insight.

## Evidence To Look For

- many segments with unclear purpose;
- small data pockets driving large decisions;
- similar keywords pointing to different landings without reason;
- operator cannot quickly explain the structure;
- reviews become slower without better decisions.

## Required Reality Discipline

- PPC failure is contextual.
- Evidence quality matters.
- Human review is mandatory.
- Structural failure can combine with semantic or landing issues.
- Weak evidence can mislead.
- Fake certainty is dangerous.
- Operational simplicity matters.

## Boundary

This guide rejects fake sophistication, unsupported restructuring certainty, and autonomous optimization claims.
