# Landing Mismatch Failure Patterns v1

## Status

Manual landing mismatch guide. Not UX scoring, conversion prediction, telemetry, or autonomous diagnosis.

## Purpose

Identify cases where query, ad, offer, and landing page do not support the same commercial action.

## Failure Patterns

- weak landing clarity;
- weak CTA alignment;
- wrong-service landing content;
- pricing mismatch;
- geo mismatch;
- urgent-intent promise not supported on page;
- trust proof missing for the ad promise;
- mobile landing friction.

## Evidence To Look For

- ad promises a specific service but landing page is generic;
- query implies urgent action but landing page delays contact;
- landing page emphasizes a different offer or region;
- CTA is unclear, hidden, or inconsistent;
- trust proof appears after high-friction steps.

## Required Reality Discipline

- PPC failure is contextual.
- Evidence quality matters.
- Human review is mandatory.
- Landing mismatch may not be the only cause.
- Weak evidence can mislead.
- Fake certainty is dangerous.
- Operational simplicity matters.

## Boundary

This guide rejects autonomous diagnosis, unsupported conversion claims, and fake root-cause certainty.
