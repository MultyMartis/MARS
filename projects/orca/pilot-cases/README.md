# ORCA Pilot Cases

## Purpose

This folder defines the ORCA Pilot Case System v1: a human-supervised operating system for running real-world PPC research pilots and accumulating reusable intelligence.

Pilot cases turn contextual observations into structured findings that can be reviewed, compared, and reused across future ORCA work.

## Scope

This system supports:

- real pilot-case accumulation;
- reusable observations;
- normalized findings;
- structured PPC intelligence;
- confidence scoring;
- regional and local service intelligence;
- commercial pattern accumulation.

## What This Is Not

- Not automation runtime.
- Not autonomous campaign management.
- Not scraping infrastructure.
- Not browser automation.
- Not live optimization.
- Not autonomous advertising AI.
- Not automatic market conclusion generation.

## Operating Boundary

Every pilot case is interpreted by a human operator. SERP data is time-bound, region-bound, device-sensitive, and affected by personalization. Observations can inform PPC research and campaign architecture, but they must not automatically trigger strategic decisions, campaign changes, bidding changes, or market conclusions.

## Folder Map

- `pilot-case-template-v1.md` - standard case record.
- `pilot-execution-flow-v1.md` - repeatable research flow.
- `pilot-report-template-v1.md` - final report structure.
- `local-service-pilot-template-v1.md` - local service specific case template.

## Relationship To ORCA

Pilot cases extend ORCA research and QA documentation with accumulation discipline. They do not add runtime behavior, account access, scraping, optimization, deployment, or autonomous execution.
