# Contradiction Review v1

## Boundary

This review records possible contradictions in a fictionalized pilot. It does not resolve them and does not claim market truth.

## Pricing Importance Variance

Pricing may be decisive for simple city orders, but less decisive for heavy, urgent, risky, or B2B jobs where equipment fit and reliability matter more. A `price from` message may help comparison while still failing to quote complex work accurately.

## WhatsApp Preference Variance

WhatsApp may be preferred for sending cargo photos and route details. For urgent dispatch, users may still prefer a direct phone call. Some B2B users may prefer email or formal quote documents.

## Desktop vs Mobile Differences

Mobile users may be more call-ready and urgency-driven. Desktop users may compare providers, prepare B2B orders, or review service details more deeply. This distinction is unvalidated in the pilot.

## B2B vs B2C Behavior

B2C users may care about fast response and visible price. B2B users may care about documents, repeat reliability, cashless payment, equipment availability, and route planning. Blended messaging can weaken both paths.

## Seasonal Shifts

Construction season, weather, holidays, agricultural cycles, and regional logistics peaks may change demand, urgency, and provider availability. Seasonal effects can contradict a single snapshot.

## Aggregator Trust Contradiction

Aggregators may increase trust through review volume and choice, or reduce trust because users cannot verify the actual operator. Both can be true for different users.

## Local Pack Contradiction

Local pack visibility may help providers with reviews, but it can also distract from paid ads or route users into map comparison behavior.

## Review Requirement

Contradictions should be preserved until live evidence bounds them. Do not smooth contradictions into a stronger conclusion.
