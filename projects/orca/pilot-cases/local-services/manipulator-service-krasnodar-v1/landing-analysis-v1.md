# Landing Analysis v1

## Boundary

This landing analysis is a simulation for QA methodology. No real landing page was audited, and no conversion claims are validated.

## CTA Clarity

- Primary CTA should be visible immediately: phone call, WhatsApp, or quick price estimate.
- A strong first screen should answer: service type, city, availability, vehicle parameters, and how to order.
- Multiple CTAs are acceptable only if priority remains clear.

## Mobile Friction

- Tap-to-call phone must be prominent.
- WhatsApp link should support photo/address/cargo detail sharing.
- Long forms, small phone text, hidden price logic, and overloaded tables increase friction on mobile.

## Trust Architecture

- Useful trust blocks: real truck photos, crane capacity, payload, boom length, job examples, reviews, company details, and payment options.
- Generic badges without proof are weak.
- Local proof should appear before deep informational text.

## Urgency Handling

- `Available today` or `24/7` claims need supporting operational cues: dispatch phone, service area, and realistic callback promise.
- Emergency messaging should not hide constraints such as distance, load access, or truck availability.

## Pricing Transparency

- Recommended visible fields: hourly rate from, minimum order, out-of-city calculation note, loading/waiting fee note, and custom quote path.
- Too much pricing complexity can slow conversion; too little can reduce trust.

## WhatsApp Usage

- WhatsApp is likely useful for sending cargo photos, pickup/delivery addresses, loading constraints, and quick quote details.
- It should complement phone calls, not replace them for urgent jobs.

## Conversion Friction Risks

- No visible phone on first screen.
- Service page mixed with unrelated грузоперевозки.
- No equipment parameters.
- No local coverage details.
- No clear price estimate path.
- Review widgets without local relevance.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

Actual landing performance, scroll behavior, form completion, call tracking, and messenger conversion are unknown. Human review and live analytics would be required for production decisions.
