# Intent Analysis v1

## Boundary

This analysis is a fictionalized research simulation. Intent classifications are hypotheses, not validated query performance data.

## Intent Classes

| Intent class | Example query pattern | Commercial interpretation | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent local intent | `манипулятор срочно краснодар`, `заказать манипулятор сейчас` | High probability of call-first behavior | Availability claim may be unsupported |
| Pricing intent | `услуги манипулятора цена`, `манипулятор цена за час` | User compares hourly/minimum order cost | Price-only traffic may be low loyalty |
| Commercial service intent | `манипулятор краснодар`, `аренда манипулятора` | Core demand for service provider | Mixed provider/aggregator SERP |
| B2B possibility | `доставка стройматериалов манипулятор`, `перевозка оборудования манипулятором` | Planned job, company payment, repeat use possible | Longer decision path |
| Informational intent | `что такое манипулятор`, `сколько тонн берет манипулятор` | Research or learning | Low immediate conversion |

## Urgent Local Intent

Urgent intent should be isolated because ad text, CTA, landing section, and negative keywords differ from planned freight and B2B transport. The strongest simulated signals are city modifier, same-day phrasing, phone-ready behavior, and short quote path.

## Pricing Intent

Pricing intent is commercially meaningful but unstable. Some users need a quick minimum estimate; others require route, weight, dimensions, loading access, waiting time, and crane operation details before price can be credible.

## B2B Possibility

B2B demand may include construction suppliers, contractors, warehouses, small manufacturers, farms, and equipment owners. These users may prefer documents, cashless payment, repeated availability, and reliability over the lowest hourly rate.

## Mixed-Intent Risks

- `грузоперевозки` may include vans, movers, intercity freight, courier delivery, and non-crane transport.
- `аренда крана` may shift toward truck cranes or heavy construction equipment.
- `перевозка мебели` may attract mover traffic that does not need a manipulator truck.
- `доставка стройматериалов` may be supplier-side or delivery-service intent.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

Actual search volume, conversion rate, device split, and query profitability are unknown. Human review with current keyword data and SERP observation is required before campaign use.
