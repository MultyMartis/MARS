# QA Findings v1

## Boundary

This QA review tests ORCA methodology on a fictionalized pilot. It does not validate a live campaign, live landing page, or real market.

## Evidence QA

- Evidence is intentionally simulated and therefore LOW confidence.
- No screenshots, live SERP exports, competitor URLs, call tracking, or analytics are attached.
- Observations must not be reused as factual market claims.

## Semantic QA

- Broad cargo terms create high leakage risk.
- `манипулятор` has non-service meanings and needs negative keyword review.
- `аренда` may attract equipment rental expectations that differ from operated service.
- Geo scope must be reviewed before campaign structure.

## Landing QA

- The expected landing standard requires phone, WhatsApp, equipment details, pricing framework, local coverage, and trust proof.
- A generic logistics page would likely mismatch urgent manipulator intent.
- Mobile CTA hierarchy must be reviewed before ad decisions.

## Campaign QA

- Urgent and planned intents should not be blended without review.
- B2B and B2C messaging may require different trust and price framing.
- Aggregator-defense messaging must not make unsupported superiority claims.

## Contradiction QA

- Price transparency may help or hurt depending on job complexity.
- WhatsApp may be strong for photo-based quote but weaker for urgent calls.
- Desktop and mobile behavior may diverge.
- Seasonal construction demand can change urgency and availability.

## Human Review Required

Before reuse, a human operator should validate current SERPs, local pack, landing pages, competitor claims, query modifiers, negative keyword candidates, and business operations.
