## Regional Uncertainty

Krasnodar city behavior may differ from Krasnodar Krai, nearby settlements, resort routes, construction zones, industrial areas, and rural delivery needs. Regional transfer is not allowed without new evidence.

## Volatility Risks

- SERP layout can change by time, device, user location, and personalization.
- Ads and aggregators may rotate quickly.
- Construction season, weather, holidays, and logistics demand can shift urgency.
- Provider availability can change daily.

## Contradiction Possibilities

Contradictions are expected and should reduce confidence until reviewed. Examples include price importance variance, WhatsApp preference variance, desktop vs mobile behavior, and B2B vs B2C decision differences.

## Reuse Constraint

This confidence review supports QA and methodology testing only. It should not be reused as validated market intelligence.
