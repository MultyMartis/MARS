# Competitor Observations v1

## Boundary

These are simulated competitor observations for methodology testing. They are not live competitor intelligence and must not be used to infer budgets, bids, conversion rates, account quality, or real market share.

## Offer Structure

- Local providers likely frame the offer around truck capacity, crane reach, hourly rate, minimum order, and city/region availability.
- Strong offers may bundle loading, transport, unloading, and construction-site delivery.
- Weak offers may say only `услуги манипулятора` without payload, boom length, working radius, or service area.

## Pricing Visibility

- Expected pricing formats: `от X руб/час`, `минимальный заказ`, `расчет по маршруту`, and `звоните для расчета`.
- Pricing transparency may reduce call friction, but fixed prices can be risky when cargo weight, route, access, and waiting time vary.
- Hidden pricing may be acceptable for complex B2B orders but weaker for urgent B2C comparison.

## Urgency Messaging

- Likely claims: `подача сегодня`, `срочно`, `24/7`, `свободная машина`, `быстрый расчет`.
- High-risk claims: guaranteed arrival time without visible dispatch capacity or regional coverage detail.

## CTA Mechanics

- Call-first CTA likely dominates immediate orders.
- WhatsApp CTA may help quote requests with photos, addresses, cargo dimensions, and route details.
- Forms may work for planned B2B jobs but add friction for urgent local demand.

## Local Trust Patterns

- Real phone number visibility, equipment photos, driver/operator proof, local address or service-area wording, and reviews likely matter.
- Generic stock photos and no vehicle details weaken trust in a heavy-cargo service.

## Landing Maturity

- Mature competitors likely separate crane-truck service from broad грузоперевозки.
- Immature pages may mix movers, loaders, freight, crane rental, and equipment transport without semantic boundaries.

## Commercial Differentiation

- Useful differentiators: known vehicle parameters, short dispatch promise, documented geography, cashless payment, work with companies, photo-based estimate, and proof of completed jobs.
- Unsupported differentiators remain hypotheses until validated by visible evidence.
