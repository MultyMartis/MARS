# Pilot Summary v1

## Scope

This is a human-supervised ORCA pilot simulation for a local manipulator truck service in Krasnodar. The niche includes crane-truck loading, cargo movement, construction material delivery, equipment transportation, and local urgent orders.

The case is explicitly non-production and non-validated. It does not use live SERP collection, scraping, browser automation, analytics, ad account data, or real competitor performance data.

## Simulated Market Shape

- Local intent appears strong for phrases such as `манипулятор краснодар`, `услуги манипулятора`, `заказать манипулятор`, and `манипулятор с краном`.
- Urgent intent likely appears around same-day delivery, construction site needs, equipment movement, and emergency logistics.
- Mobile behavior is expected to be important because users may search while coordinating a job, comparing phone numbers, or requesting fast availability.
- Aggregator and classifieds pressure may distort trust because users can compare many providers without clear quality controls.
- Trust signals likely matter because the service involves heavy cargo, timing risk, vehicle availability, and driver/operator competence.

## Operational Findings

- Call-first and WhatsApp-first CTAs should be treated as central hypotheses, not proven preferences.
- Pricing visibility is commercially important but potentially unstable: users may want `price from`, hourly rate, minimum order, and crane capacity before calling.
- Landing pages need to explain vehicle capacity, crane reach, geography, availability, and real contact paths quickly.
- Broad cargo terms create semantic contamination from freight forwarding, movers, logistics companies, courier delivery, and equipment rental.

## Methodology Stress-Test Value

This case tests whether ORCA can keep fictionalized observations separate from evidence-backed conclusions. It also tests whether local SERP, competitor, semantic, landing, trust, and confidence notes can remain practical without becoming unsupported market intelligence.

## Boundary

All findings remain hypothetical until checked by a human operator against current local SERPs, competitor pages, landing pages, and client constraints.
