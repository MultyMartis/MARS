## Simulated Visible Patterns

- Top ad blocks likely emphasize `цена от`, `подача сегодня`, `работаем 24/7`, `наличный/безналичный расчет`, and direct phone calls.
- Local pack presence is expected for city-modified service queries, with phone, rating, distance, and map-card trust cues.
- Aggregators, classifieds, service boards, and directory pages may appear for `аренда`, `услуги`, and `цена` queries.
- Organic results likely mix local providers, aggregator listings, map services, and broad cargo/rental pages.
- Mobile snippets likely favor tap-to-call, messenger links, short availability claims, and fast estimate offers.

## Urgent-Intent Patterns

- Same-day availability.
- Fast vehicle dispatch.
- Operator and truck availability by phone.
- Loading/unloading plus transportation bundles.
- Direct contact instead of long forms.
