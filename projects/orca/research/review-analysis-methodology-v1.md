# Review Analysis Methodology v1

## Purpose

Uses visible review signals to improve PPC offer, trust, and landing quality analysis for service businesses. It does not automate review collection or reputation management.

## Signals To Capture

- Rating level and review count.
- Review recency.
- Repeated praise themes.
- Repeated complaint themes.
- Service speed, price, quality, staff, guarantee, and communication cues.
- Local trust cues: address, photos, verified profile, response behavior.
- Risk signals: fake-looking reviews, unresolved complaints, low review volume.

## PPC Use

- Align ad copy with real trust expectations.
- Detect offer gaps against local competitors.
- Improve landing page trust architecture.
- Identify objections that need landing page answers.
- Support QA for unsupported claims.

## Rules

- Reviews are qualitative evidence, not proof of conversion performance.
- Do not invent ratings or review themes.
- Do not treat one review as a market truth.
- Do not use review content for claims without human approval.
- Record SAFE UNKNOWN when reviews are sparse, old, or suspicious.

## Output

- Trust signal summary.
- Complaint and objection summary.
- Landing improvement notes.
- Ad claim risk notes.
- SAFE UNKNOWN list.

## Boundary

Review analysis is human-supervised PPC intelligence. It is not sentiment automation, hidden optimization, review scraping, or autonomous campaign control.
