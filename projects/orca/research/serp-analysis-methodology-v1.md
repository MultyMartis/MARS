# SERP Analysis Methodology v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA reviews search result pages for PPC planning, especially Yandex.Direct search-first campaigns for service businesses and local lead generation.

## Method

1. Start from commercial seed queries, not broad informational terms.
2. Capture SERP snapshots by region, device, localization, timestamp, and exact query.
3. Separate ads, maps/local pack, aggregators, marketplaces, organic service pages, and informational results.
4. Identify commercial pressure: number of ads, local pack dominance, aggregator density, price messaging, urgency cues, and CTA style.
5. Compare SERP intent against planned landing pages and semantic clusters.
6. Record SAFE UNKNOWN where evidence is weak, personalized, or unstable.

## Yandex.Direct Realities

- Yandex local results can strongly influence commercial intent.
- Aggregators and Yandex ecosystem surfaces may dominate service niches.
- Map reviews and local trust signals often shape lead quality.
- Mobile SERP review is critical for call-heavy services.
- Query wording can shift between direct service intent and research intent.

## Quality Rules

- One query can produce mixed intent; do not force it into a pure commercial cluster.
- SERP evidence beats keyword intuition when they conflict.
- High ad density does not prove profitability.
- Competitor copy is inspiration evidence, not a strategy to copy blindly.
- Human review decides whether a query is usable.

## Output

- SERP snapshot set.
- Intent notes.
- Competitive pressure notes.
- Aggregator and marketplace notes.
- Landing mismatch risks.
- SAFE UNKNOWN register.

## Boundary

This is a manual, human-supervised research methodology. It is not scraping infrastructure, browser automation, autonomous optimization, automatic bidding, or runtime orchestration.
