# Competitor Intelligence Model v1

## Purpose

Structures visible competitor observations for PPC planning without claiming inside knowledge, account access, or automated monitoring.

## Observation Areas

- Visible ad message and CTA.
- Offer type: price-first, speed-first, trust-first, emergency-first, premium, aggregator.
- Landing type: direct service page, multi-service site, marketplace, map card, lead form.
- Trust signals: reviews, certificates, guarantees, years in market, local address.
- Commercial pressure: repeated advertisers, dense ad blocks, local pack domination.
- Risk cues: unsupported claims, extreme discounts, misleading urgency.

## Practical Use

- Identify expected offer standards in a niche.
- Detect aggregator pressure and whether direct advertisers can compete.
- Support ad relevance and landing match QA.
- Inform negative keywords and audience separation.
- Highlight differentiation opportunities for the human operator.

## Non-Use

- Do not infer bids, budgets, conversion rates, or campaign performance.
- Do not treat competitor wording as legally safe.
- Do not automate competitor tracking.
- Do not copy competitor ads without human review.
- Do not claim optimization from observation alone.

## QA Checks

- Every competitor note is tied to a query, region, device, and timestamp.
- Observation is separated from interpretation.
- Claims are phrased as visible evidence, not verified internal facts.
- SAFE UNKNOWN is used for missing or unstable signals.

## Boundary

Competitor intelligence is research-first support for human strategy. It does not control campaigns, bids, budgets, targeting, or live account behavior.
