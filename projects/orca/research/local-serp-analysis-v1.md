# Local SERP Analysis v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA evaluates local commercial search results for service business PPC, with emphasis on Yandex.Direct and lead generation.

## Local Signals

- City and district modifiers.
- Map pack presence and dominance.
- Yandex Maps cards, ratings, review count, and proximity cues.
- Local phone, address, service area, and opening hours.
- Emergency and same-day service wording.
- Aggregator or marketplace control of local results.
- Mobile click-to-call and route behavior signals.

## Analysis Steps

1. Compare base query against geo-modified query.
2. Review desktop and mobile if the niche is call-heavy.
3. Mark whether map/local pack competes with ads for attention.
4. Identify local trust standards: reviews, address, local proof, warranty.
5. Check whether landing pages support the target location.
6. Flag semantic clusters that mix regions, cities, or service areas.

## PPC Implications

- Local intent often requires separate campaigns or ad groups.
- Geo purity matters more than raw keyword volume.
- Landing pages must prove local service availability.
- Generic national copy can underperform in local lead generation.
- Human review decides whether a region is worth separate structure.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- User location personalization.
- True service coverage.
- Real availability in a district.
- Map ranking stability.
- Review authenticity.

## Boundary

This document supports local PPC research and QA only. It does not automate local rankings, campaign publishing, bidding, or account optimization.
