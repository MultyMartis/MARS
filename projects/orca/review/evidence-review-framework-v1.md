# Evidence Review Framework v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA reviews evidence realism and evidence quality before using observations, pilot findings, or records to support strategic conclusions.

Evidence review does not make an observation absolute. It checks whether the evidence is useful, bounded, and honest.

## Review Areas

- source traceability;
- timestamp and freshness;
- region and niche context;
- device and query context;
- screenshot or manual review reference;
- separation of visible fact from interpretation;
- contradiction links;
- volatility notes;
- confidence impact;
- SAFE UNKNOWN.

## Evidence Realism Rules

- Real evidence can be weak, partial, or contradictory.
- Stale evidence degrades reliability.
- Missing context must cap confidence.
- Synthetic summaries cannot replace traceable records.
- A single observation can suggest a hypothesis, not establish a durable pattern.
- Commercial interpretation must not exceed visible support.

## Rejection or Limitation Signals

- missing timestamp;
- missing region;
- missing device;
- untraceable source;
- visual claim without screenshot or review note;
- unsupported market claim;
- conversion claim without conversion evidence;
- contradiction omitted;
- evidence older than the current review context allows.

## Boundary

This framework supports manual evidence quality review. It is not telemetry validation, scraping validation, autonomous QA, or self-learning intelligence.
