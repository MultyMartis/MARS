# Operational Review Model v1

## Purpose

Defines the categories used to review ORCA's operational quality. Review is human-supervised and evidence-first. It does not create automatic corrections or autonomous improvement loops.

## Review Categories

### Evidence Realism

Checks whether claims are backed by traceable records, current observations, clear context, and preserved contradictions.

### Methodology Usefulness

Checks whether methods help humans make better PPC and local-service decisions, or merely add process language.

### Heuristic Usefulness

Checks whether heuristics remain bounded, revisable, evidence-backed, and useful in real strategic review.

### Operational Complexity

Checks whether a layer, template, or workflow creates more burden than value for human reviewers.

### Contradiction Handling

Checks whether conflicting evidence is preserved, linked, and reflected in confidence rather than hidden.

### Confidence Realism

Checks whether confidence matches evidence strength, freshness, repeatability, volatility, and regional/niche limits.

### Stale Evidence Risk

Checks whether old observations, screenshots, pilots, or patterns are still being treated as current.

### Volatility Awareness

Checks whether SERP, market, seasonal, device, offer, and competitor volatility are visible in conclusions.

### Regional Realism

Checks whether findings remain limited to observed regions unless transfer has fresh human review.

### Niche Realism

Checks whether findings remain limited to comparable niches and commercial intent patterns.

### Anti-Abstraction Review

Checks whether concepts remain useful for operators or drift into impressive but low-value abstraction.

## Review Outputs

Every operational review should record:

- reviewed area;
- evidence used;
- contradictions found;
- stale or weak evidence risks;
- confidence and usefulness impact;
- operational burden;
- commercial reality notes;
- SAFE UNKNOWN;
- reviewer and date;
- next review trigger.

## Boundary

This model organizes human review. It is not automated governance, telemetry, runtime orchestration, autonomous QA, or self-learning strategy.
