# Anti-Mythology Checklist v1

- [ ] No self-learning or autonomous evolution is implied.
- [ ] No unsupported strategic certainty appears.
- [ ] No synthetic confidence replaces evidence.
- [ ] No thin evidence is inflated into market proof.
- [ ] No region or niche over-generalization appears.
- [ ] No fake intelligence narrative appears.
- [ ] No unsupported CPC, conversion, or lead-quality claim appears.
- [ ] Abstraction has practical operating value.
- [ ] Commercial reality is prioritized over theory.
- [ ] Human reviewer responsibility is explicit.
