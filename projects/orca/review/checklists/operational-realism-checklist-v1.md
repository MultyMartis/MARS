# Operational Realism Checklist v1

- [ ] The review output helps a real human operator.
- [ ] Commercial usefulness is clear.
- [ ] Evidence is traceable.
- [ ] Observations are not treated as absolute truth.
- [ ] Contradictions are preserved.
- [ ] Region and niche limits are visible.
- [ ] Stale evidence risk is checked.
- [ ] Reviewer workload is reasonable.
- [ ] No hidden automation is implied.
- [ ] SAFE UNKNOWN is used where support is missing.
