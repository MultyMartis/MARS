# Operational Usefulness Review v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA reviews whether a method, heuristic, evidence layer, or template remains useful to human operators.

Usefulness is judged by practical PPC and local-service work, not by conceptual elegance.

## Usefulness Criteria

An ORCA artifact is useful when it:

- improves evidence discipline;
- makes contradictions easier to see;
- helps reviewers avoid overconfidence;
- supports practical campaign or research decisions;
- reduces ambiguity;
- remains short enough to use repeatedly;
- preserves human responsibility.

## Low-Usefulness Signals

- fields are filled but do not affect decisions;
- review creates fatigue without clearer output;
- language becomes abstract or mythological;
- the artifact duplicates another layer;
- outputs are hard to apply to real PPC work;
- commercial reality is missing;
- conclusions could not change based on evidence.

## Review Actions

- keep;
- simplify;
- merge with an existing artifact;
- narrow scope;
- mark as experimental;
- retire from active use while preserving history.

## Boundary

Operational usefulness review is human judgment. It is not automated governance, autonomous review, telemetry analytics, or self-improvement.
