# Commercial Reality Review v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA reviews whether its findings, methods, and heuristics remain useful in real PPC and local-service work.

Commercial reality overrides theoretical elegance. A clean framework that does not help operators is not a successful framework.

## Priorities

Review must prioritize:

- real PPC usefulness;
- local-service reality;
- commercial practicality;
- operational survivability;
- human usability;
- campaign usefulness;
- strategic clarity;
- research efficiency.

## Commercial Review Questions

- Would this help a PPC specialist or strategist make a better review decision?
- Does it reflect local-service buying behavior and urgency?
- Does it avoid unsupported claims about conversions, CPC, or lead quality?
- Does it preserve uncertainty where commercial data is missing?
- Does it reduce wasted research time?
- Does it clarify campaign, landing, SERP, or competitor review?
- Can a human apply it without a hidden system?

## Failure Signals

- strategy language without campaign implications;
- elegant taxonomy with no operating value;
- unsupported assumptions about buyer behavior;
- local context ignored;
- evidence too stale for commercial use;
- contradiction ignored because the model sounds cleaner without it;
- confidence higher than commercial proof allows.

## Boundary

Commercial reality review is human-supervised operational judgment. It is not automatic optimization, telemetry analytics, bid management, market prediction, or autonomous strategy.
