# Heuristic Review Framework v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA reviews heuristic quality, usefulness, evidence support, contradictions, confidence, and stability.

Heuristics are revisable strategic prompts. They are not universal truth, automatic decisions, or self-learning outputs.

## Review Areas

- evidence traceability;
- repeated observation support;
- contradiction handling;
- confidence realism;
- stability and volatility;
- regional applicability;
- niche applicability;
- device and seasonal limits;
- stale evidence risk;
- practical PPC usefulness.

## Quality Rules

A heuristic is stronger when:

- evidence is current, traceable, and repeated;
- contradictions are bounded and preserved;
- confidence does not exceed evidence;
- region and niche limits are explicit;
- the heuristic helps a human choose a practical review focus.

A heuristic is weaker when:

- it depends on stale or synthetic-only evidence;
- contradictions are ignored;
- it generalizes across regions or niches without review;
- it sounds certain but has thin support;
- it creates abstraction without campaign usefulness.

## Review Actions

- keep with boundaries;
- downgrade confidence;
- narrow applicability;
- mark `needs_refresh`;
- mark `contradicted`;
- retire from active guidance;
- preserve as historical note.

## Boundary

This framework is human heuristic review discipline. ORCA does not autonomously evolve, promote, retire, or apply heuristics.
