# Anti-Mythology Review Rules v1

## Purpose

Defines rules for detecting language, claims, or structures that make ORCA sound more intelligent, autonomous, certain, or complete than the evidence supports.

ORCA is human-supervised commercial intelligence discipline. It does not autonomously evolve or know markets.

## Detect These Risks

### Fake Intelligence Narratives

Flag claims that imply ORCA understands, learns, watches, predicts, or decides without human review and traceable evidence.

### Unsupported Strategic Certainty

Flag confident strategy claims that lack current evidence, contradictions review, region/niche boundaries, or reviewer notes.

### Evidence Inflation

Flag cases where thin observations, synthetic summaries, or one pilot are presented as robust market proof.

### Abstraction Drift

Flag concepts that sound sophisticated but do not improve evidence review, PPC usefulness, or operator decisions.

### Over-Generalization

Flag heuristics applied across regions, niches, devices, or seasons without fresh review.

### Pseudo-Autonomous Claims

Flag language suggesting automatic governance, autonomous optimization, self-improvement, self-learning, telemetry awareness, or hidden runtime behavior.

### Synthetic Confidence

Flag confidence that comes from tidy documentation, repeated phrasing, or model fluency rather than evidence quality.

### Unsupported Market Claims

Flag market, competitor, CPC, conversion, lead quality, or user behavior claims without traceable evidence.

## Required Correction

- Replace certainty with bounded evidence statements.
- Add SAFE UNKNOWN where support is missing.
- Preserve contradictions.
- Lower or cap confidence.
- Mark stale or weak evidence.
- Remove autonomous or mythological language.
- Recenter commercial reality and human review.

## Boundary

These rules guide human anti-mythology review. They are not automated claim detection, policy enforcement, or autonomous governance.
