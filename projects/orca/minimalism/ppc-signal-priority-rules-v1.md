# PPC Signal Priority Rules v1

## Status

Manual priority rules. Not AI ranking, automated scoring, telemetry, or dashboards.

## Top Priority Signals

- strong semantic risks that waste spend;
- strong landing mismatch that breaks intent;
- strong trust failures that weaken action;
- major mobile friction that blocks contact;
- major CTA failures that reduce clarity;
- strong aggregator pressure that changes SERP reality;
- high commercial-impact signals.

## Low Priority Signals

- cosmetic observations;
- weak unsupported patterns;
- endless nuance;
- speculative abstraction;
- low-impact differences.

## Priority Test

If the signal does not affect PPC action, lead quality, spend risk, or commercial clarity, deprioritize it.

## Required Minimalism Discipline

Not everything deserves analysis. High-impact signals matter most. Theory expansion reduces usefulness.

## Boundary

These rules reject fake completeness, abstraction addiction, giant audits, and methodology obsession.
