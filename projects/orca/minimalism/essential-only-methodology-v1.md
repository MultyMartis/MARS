# Essential-Only Methodology v1

## Status

Manual methodology limit. Not governance expansion, automation, or intelligence mythology.

## Purpose

Keep ORCA methodology limited to what directly supports practical PPC review.

## Essential Methodology Must

- reduce operator work;
- clarify high-impact signals;
- support practical action;
- improve evidence discipline;
- define when to stop;
- preserve commercial realism.

## Non-Essential Methodology

- repeats existing concepts;
- adds abstract vocabulary;
- expands review without action;
- creates fake completeness;
- rewards endless nuance;
- increases operator fatigue.

## Required Minimalism Discipline

Not everything deserves analysis. Diminishing returns exist. Theory expansion reduces usefulness.

## Boundary

This guide rejects abstraction addiction, methodology obsession, giant audits, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
