# Practical Operator Focus v1

## Status

Manual operator-focus guide. Not productivity telemetry, automation, or governance expansion.

## Purpose

Keep the operator focused on practical PPC decisions instead of theoretical review completeness.

## Focus On

- what risk matters most;
- what evidence is strong;
- what action is practical;
- what uncertainty needs escalation;
- what can be safely ignored;
- when review should stop.

## Avoid

- cosmetic observations;
- weak unsupported patterns;
- endless nuance;
- speculative abstraction;
- low-impact differences;
- methodology obsession.

## Required Minimalism Discipline

Speed matters. Practical action matters. Operator energy matters. Simpler review often produces better decisions.

## Boundary

This guide rejects giant audits, fake completeness, endless observation loops, and abstraction addiction.
