# Minimal Review Scope Rules v1

## Status

Manual scope rules. Not automated workflow control or governance expansion.

## Minimal Scope

Review only:

- strong semantic risks;
- strong landing mismatch;
- strong trust failures;
- major mobile friction;
- major CTA failures;
- strong aggregator pressure;
- high commercial-impact signals.

## Scope Stop Conditions

- remaining issues are cosmetic;
- patterns are weak or unsupported;
- nuance is endless;
- abstraction is speculative;
- differences are low-impact;
- additional review will not change action.

## Required Minimalism Discipline

Not everything deserves analysis. Practical action matters. Diminishing returns exist. High-impact signals matter most.

## Boundary

These rules reject giant audits, fake completeness, endless observation loops, abstraction addiction, and methodology obsession.
