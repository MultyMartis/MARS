# High-Impact-Only Review v1

## Status

Manual review guide. Not automated scoring, dashboards, telemetry, or optimization.

## Purpose

Limit review time to PPC signals that can meaningfully affect spend, lead quality, or campaign alignment.

## Include

- strong semantic risks;
- strong landing mismatch;
- strong trust failures;
- major mobile friction;
- major CTA failures;
- strong aggregator pressure;
- high commercial-impact signals.

## Exclude

- cosmetic observations;
- weak unsupported patterns;
- endless nuance;
- speculative abstraction;
- low-impact differences;
- notes added for fake completeness.

## Stop Rule

Stop when the next observation is unlikely to change action, escalation, or risk understanding.

## Required Minimalism Discipline

Speed matters. Practical action matters. Operator energy matters. Diminishing returns exist.

## Boundary

This guide rejects giant audits, endless observation loops, fake completeness, and methodology obsession.
