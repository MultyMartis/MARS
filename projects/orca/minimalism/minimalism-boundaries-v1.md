# Minimalism Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA Operational Minimalism may help humans keep PPC review small, fast, and high-impact.

It does not create AI architecture, automation, orchestration, dashboards, telemetry, runtime systems, or intelligence mythology.

## Explicitly Rejected

- giant audits;
- fake completeness;
- endless observation loops;
- abstraction addiction;
- methodology obsession;
- speculative abstraction;
- low-impact analysis inflation.

## Allowed Scope

- high-impact-only PPC review;
- rapid prioritization;
- operational speed;
- practical focus;
- minimal viable review;
- signal prioritization;
- review stopping discipline.

## Required Minimalism Discipline

- not everything deserves analysis;
- speed matters;
- practical action matters;
- operator energy matters;
- diminishing returns exist;
- high-impact signals matter most;
- theory expansion reduces usefulness.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know which low-impact details matter in a specific business context unless responsible humans verify practical value.
