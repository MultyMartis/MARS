# Anti-Theory Expansion v1

## Status

Manual anti-expansion guide. Not governance expansion or methodology architecture.

## Purpose

Prevent practical PPC review from turning into theory management.

## Theory Expansion Signals

- new vocabulary without operator action;
- speculative abstraction;
- endless nuance;
- fake completeness;
- low-impact differences treated as major;
- weak unsupported patterns promoted to methodology;
- review loops created to explain review loops.

## Response

- return to high-impact signals;
- remove cosmetic observations;
- cut speculative abstraction;
- keep only commercially realistic actions;
- stop when diminishing returns appear.

## Required Minimalism Discipline

Practical action matters. Operator energy matters. High-impact signals matter most. Theory expansion reduces usefulness.

## Boundary

This guide rejects abstraction addiction, methodology obsession, giant audits, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
