# Operational Speed Rules v1

## Status

Manual speed rules. Not automation, dashboards, telemetry, or runtime systems.

## Purpose

Protect PPC review speed without ignoring high-impact risk.

## Speed Rules

- start with the highest commercial risk;
- check semantic, landing, trust, mobile, CTA, and aggregator pressure quickly;
- ignore cosmetic observations;
- ignore weak unsupported patterns;
- stop before endless nuance appears;
- escalate instead of over-reviewing uncertainty.

## Slow Down Only When

- spend risk is high;
- lead quality risk is high;
- evidence contradicts itself;
- legal, policy, brand, or operational capacity risk appears.

## Required Minimalism Discipline

Speed matters. Operator energy matters. Diminishing returns exist. Practical action matters.

## Boundary

These rules reject giant audits, fake completeness, endless observation loops, and methodology obsession.
