# ORCA Operational Minimalism Layer v1

## Purpose

This layer keeps ORCA PPC review small, fast, and commercially useful.

It supports high-impact-only review, rapid prioritization, minimal viable review, signal prioritization, and stopping discipline.

## What This Layer Is

- Practical PPC minimalism.
- Operator speed protection.
- High-impact signal focus.
- Anti-overengineering review discipline.
- Minimal viable review support.

## What This Layer Is Not

- Not giant audits.
- Not fake completeness.
- Not automation, orchestration, telemetry, dashboards, or runtime systems.
- Not AI mythology.
- Not methodology obsession.

## Prioritize Only

- strong semantic risks;
- strong landing mismatch;
- strong trust failures;
- major mobile friction;
- major CTA failures;
- strong aggregator pressure;
- high commercial-impact signals.

## Deprioritize

- cosmetic observations;
- weak unsupported patterns;
- endless nuance;
- speculative abstraction;
- low-impact differences.

## Required Minimalism Discipline

- Not everything deserves analysis.
- Speed matters.
- Practical action matters.
- Operator energy matters.
- Diminishing returns exist.
- High-impact signals matter most.
- Theory expansion reduces usefulness.
