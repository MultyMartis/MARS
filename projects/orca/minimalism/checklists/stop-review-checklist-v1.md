# Stop Review Checklist v1

## Status

Manual stop checklist. Not automated workflow control.

## Stop When

- next observation will not change action;
- remaining issues are cosmetic;
- evidence is weak and unsupported;
- nuance is endless;
- abstraction is speculative;
- differences are low-impact;
- operator energy is dropping;
- diminishing returns are visible.

## Before Stopping

- state top risk;
- state evidence quality;
- state practical action;
- mark SAFE UNKNOWN;
- escalate if needed.

## Required Minimalism Discipline

Not everything deserves analysis. Speed matters. Operator energy matters. Diminishing returns exist.

## Boundary

This checklist rejects fake completeness, endless observation loops, giant audits, and methodology obsession.
