# Operational Speed Checklist v1

## Status

Manual speed checklist. Not telemetry, dashboards, automation, or productivity scoring.

## Speed Checks

- review started with high-impact risk;
- semantic risk checked quickly;
- landing mismatch checked quickly;
- trust failure checked quickly;
- mobile friction checked quickly;
- CTA failure checked quickly;
- aggregator pressure checked quickly;
- low-impact observations ignored.

## Slow Down Only If

- spend risk is high;
- lead quality risk is high;
- evidence contradicts itself;
- escalation is required;
- operational capacity or policy risk appears.

## Required Minimalism Discipline

Speed matters. Practical action matters. Operator energy matters. Theory expansion reduces usefulness.

## Boundary

This checklist rejects giant audits, fake completeness, endless observation loops, abstraction addiction, and methodology obsession.
