# High-Impact Checklist v1

## Status

Manual checklist. Not automated scoring or approval.

## High-Impact Checks

- strong semantic risk exists;
- strong landing mismatch exists;
- strong trust failure exists;
- major mobile friction exists;
- major CTA failure exists;
- strong aggregator pressure exists;
- high commercial-impact signal exists.

## Deprioritize

- cosmetic observations;
- weak unsupported patterns;
- endless nuance;
- speculative abstraction;
- low-impact differences.

## Operator Questions

- Would this change PPC action?
- Would this reduce waste or improve lead quality?
- Is evidence strong enough?
- Should review stop now?

## Boundary

This checklist rejects giant audits, fake completeness, abstraction addiction, and methodology obsession.
