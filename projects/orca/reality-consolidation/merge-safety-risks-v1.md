# Merge Safety Risks v1

## Status

Safety review for future merge planning only. No source files changed.

## Core Warning

Compression has risks. Duplication also has risks. Operator clarity matters most.

## Risks

| Risk | How it could happen | Consequence | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deleting useful nuance | Removing confidence decay, contradiction status, or source reliability as "duplicate." | Evidence discipline degrades. | Preserve a minimal appendix for lifecycle and contradiction nuance. |
| Over-compression | Collapsing evidence, confidence, contradictions, and decisions into one vague document. | Operators lose precise handling of weak evidence and conflicts. | Keep clear sections: evidence, confidence, contradictions, decision. |
| Operator confusion | Keeping multiple scales: evidence strength, confidence level, reliability score. | Operator does not know which signal controls action. | Use one practical reliability scale or define scale relationships explicitly. |
| Hidden duplication surviving | Merging folders but keeping repeated templates and checklists. | Maintenance burden remains. | Audit templates after conceptual merge map is approved. |
| Breaking operator paths | Archiving decision files that operators actually use. | Usability drops. | Preserve operator-facing decision and escalation docs until real usage is checked. |
| Abstraction collapse | Removing all distinction between evidence, confidence, contradiction, and decision. | False certainty increases. | Keep role separation even if files are merged. |
| Evidence discipline degradation | Shortening evidence fields too aggressively. | Later conclusions become unsupported. | Keep minimal source, timestamp, region, niche, device, reviewer, SAFE UNKNOWN. |
| Contradiction loss | Treating contradictions as confidence notes only. | Conflicting evidence disappears. | Preserve contradiction states and linked observations. |
| Fake certainty | Merged structure makes weak evidence look cleaner. | Operators may over-act on weak evidence. | Keep downgrade, stop, defer, and escalation rules visible. |
| New bloat from merge planning | Creating more mapping documents without pruning later. | Consolidation becomes another documentation layer. | Use this map once, then perform focused file-level cleanup with approval. |

## Must-Preserve Safety Rules

- ORCA must stay usable.
- Simplicity is strategic.
- Evidence discipline must survive.
- Operator decisions must remain human-supervised.
- Contradictions must remain visible.
- Confidence must stay cautious and revisable.
- SAFE UNKNOWN must remain acceptable.
- Abstraction addiction is dangerous.

## Merge Red Flags

Do not proceed with an actual merge if:

- no one can identify the operator-facing entrypoint;
- evidence strength and confidence are still competing concepts;
- contradiction handling becomes a footnote;
- escalation rules are hidden;
- templates become longer after compression;
- the merge creates a new methodology layer.

## Boundary

This risk review does not approve deletion, archiving, or merge execution. It only identifies safety constraints for a later controlled cleanup task.
