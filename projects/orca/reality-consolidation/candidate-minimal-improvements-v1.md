# Candidate Minimal Improvements v1

## Status

Small improvement suggestions only.

No redesign. No replacement approval. No new methodology.

## Improvement Goal

Make `evidence-review-template-candidate-v1.md` faster and less tiring while preserving safety.

## Suggested Minimal Improvements

### 1. Mark Fields As Short-Answer

Add a short instruction near the top:

Use one line per field unless risk requires more.

Why: this tells operators the template is not asking for a report.

### 2. Reduce Repeated Evidence Source Fields

Keep evidence source in Review Identity or Evidence Summary, not both.

Why: duplicate fields slow the operator and create copy-paste behavior.

### 3. Make Tradeoff Review Optional For Tiny Issues

Rename the section to:

Tradeoff Review, if relevant

Why: many small PPC observations do not need every tradeoff lens.

### 4. Shorten Tradeoff Prompts

Replace the full tradeoff list with fewer grouped prompts:

- speed vs evidence quality:
- simplicity vs segmentation:
- trust / pricing / landing clarity:
- operator effort vs expected value:

Why: same practical coverage, less form fatigue.

### 5. Clarify Escalation Triggers

Add the missing high-signal triggers from the standalone checklist:

- spend risk
- brand or policy risk
- legal/compliance risk
- operator authority insufficient
- decision fatigue visible

Why: compressed escalation wording is the candidate's biggest safety weakness.

### 6. Merge Outcome With Decision Proposal

Either remove Outcome or make it a final checkbox:

- final outcome: proceed / defer / escalate / collect more evidence / reject

Why: current Outcome overlaps with action type and slows completion.

### 7. Keep SAFE UNKNOWN Explicit

Do not reduce SAFE UNKNOWN visibility.

Why: repetition here is useful. It prevents weak evidence from becoming confident-sounding strategy.

### 8. Add A Stop Rule

Add one line:

If risk is high and evidence is weak, stop and use the original checklists.

Why: this preserves the boundary between quick additive use and serious review.

## What Not To Change

- Do not add scoring.
- Do not add automation language.
- Do not add approval mechanics.
- Do not create tiers or a new review system.
- Do not make the candidate authoritative.
- Do not remove low-evidence discipline.

## Minimal Recommendation

Improve the candidate by shortening, clarifying optional sections, and strengthening escalation wording.

The best version is not more elegant. It is faster to complete honestly.
