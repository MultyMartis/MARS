# ORCA Real Operator Value Review v1

## Status

Value classification based on the first fictional operational pilot simulation.

This is not a universal ORCA ranking. It reflects one small PPC use case: emergency plumber in Kazan.

## Evaluation Criteria

- speed impact
- clarity
- safety
- usefulness
- operator load

## HIGH VALUE

### Minimal real pilot workflow

- value: high
- why: gave the whole session a narrow start, middle, and stop point
- speed impact: high
- safety: high, because it forced confidence and SAFE UNKNOWN
- operator load: low

Operational verdict: keep as primary entry point.

### Session execution guide

- value: high
- why: time limits and stop rules prevented review sprawl
- speed impact: high
- safety: medium-high
- operator load: low

Operational verdict: keep open during the session.

### Fast review workflow

- value: high
- why: focused the pilot on observations that change PPC action
- speed impact: high
- safety: medium
- operator load: low

Operational verdict: use as compression layer, not as another report format.

### Search-term cleanliness rules

- value: high
- why: quickly exposed urgent/generic/DIY/vacancy/materials mismatch
- speed impact: high
- safety: high, because it discouraged overblocking from weak evidence
- operator load: low

Operational verdict: essential for PPC workflows.

### Landing match boundaries

- value: high
- why: kept landing review practical and stopped fake conversion claims
- speed impact: medium
- safety: high
- operator load: low

Operational verdict: essential for landing-fit decisions.

## MEDIUM VALUE

### Essential signals model

- value: medium-high
- why: useful for filtering noise, but overlaps with fast-review discipline
- speed impact: medium
- safety: medium
- operator load: low

Operational verdict: useful as a short reminder; avoid opening multiple signal files.

### Ad copy boundaries

- value: medium
- why: useful once claims, CTA, and landing fit are reviewed
- speed impact: medium
- safety: high for preventing unsupported urgency and price claims
- operator load: low-medium

Operational verdict: use after semantic and landing checks, not before.

### Campaign QA boundaries

- value: medium
- why: useful to prevent false launch readiness, but too early for the first half of the session
- speed impact: low-medium
- safety: high
- operator load: medium

Operational verdict: use only as final check.

### Evidence review template candidate

- value: medium
- why: useful for evidence discipline, but full template is heavier than needed
- speed impact: low if filled fully, medium if used as a mental guard
- safety: high
- operator load: medium-high

Operational verdict: use selectively, one-line style only.

## LOW VALUE IN THIS PILOT

### Full desktop review detail

- value: low
- why: mobile intent and CTA friction mattered more for urgent plumbing
- speed impact: low
- safety: low-medium
- operator load: medium

Operational verdict: keep desktop as contrast only.

### Full competitor UX audit

- value: low
- why: the needed decision did not require exhaustive UX notes
- speed impact: low
- safety: medium if overclaims are avoided
- operator load: high

Operational verdict: avoid in first-pass pilot.

### Full semantic clustering

- value: low
- why: obvious dirty intent was enough for the first action
- speed impact: low
- safety: medium; over-filtering risk increases with premature detail
- operator load: high

Operational verdict: defer until real search-term data exists.

### Launch-readiness style review

- value: low
- why: evidence was too weak for launch approval
- speed impact: low
- safety: high only as a stop sign
- operator load: medium-high

Operational verdict: use only to say "not ready", then stop.

## Fastest Operator Value

The most valuable chain was:

`minimal workflow -> session guide -> fast review -> search-term cleanliness -> landing match -> SAFE UNKNOWN`

This chain produced a practical PPC action without pretending to know performance:

`Separate emergency intent, verify claims, reduce mobile CTA friction, and do not approve launch from a weak snapshot.`

## Value Conclusion

ORCA created value when it compressed operator attention toward risky PPC decisions.

ORCA lost value when available layers encouraged document selection, repeated boundary reading, or premature launch thinking.
