# Candidate Refinement Review v1

## Status

Minimal refinement note only.

No replacement approval. No redesign. No new methodology.

## What Changed

- Added a short-answer instruction so operators do not treat every field as a mini-report.
- Removed duplicate evidence-source capture from Review Identity.
- Made Tradeoff Review explicitly optional when not relevant.
- Shortened tradeoff prompts into fewer practical lines.
- Merged Outcome into Decision Proposal as one final outcome field.
- Strengthened escalation visibility for spend, brand, policy/legal, operator authority, and decision fatigue.

## What Was Intentionally Not Changed

- Additive-only status stayed intact.
- Original templates and checklists stayed authoritative.
- No scoring was added.
- No automation or approval language was added.
- No new sections were added to the candidate.
- No extra review logic was introduced.
- Low-evidence discipline was not reduced.

## Safety Preserved

- SAFE UNKNOWN remains explicit.
- Evidence strength and confidence remain visible.
- Contradictions remain visible.
- Low-evidence guard remains intact.
- Escalation guard remains intact and slightly clearer.
- Final checklist still checks evidence, uncertainty, escalation, and reversibility or human approval.

## Usability Improvements

The candidate should now be faster to scan and easier to complete under operator pressure.

Main improvement: fewer mandatory-feeling fields.

Second improvement: less duplication between decision and outcome.

Third improvement: escalation triggers are easier to notice without opening the standalone checklist.

## Remaining Weaknesses

- The candidate is still a form and can still feel heavy for very small observations.
- Optional tradeoff review depends on operator judgment.
- Standalone checklists remain safer for high-impact or unclear decisions.
- Real speed improvement is not proven until a human operator uses it under time pressure.

## Next Safe Step

Run one timeboxed operator test on a realistic small PPC issue.

If the operator completes it faster without losing SAFE UNKNOWN or escalation discipline, keep the candidate additive and usable.
