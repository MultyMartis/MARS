## Usability Findings

### Speed

The candidate was faster than opening separate decision, uncertainty, tradeoff, low-evidence, and escalation files. One pass was enough for a small PPC issue.

Main speed gain: fewer context switches.

Main speed drag: some fields feel unnecessary for a narrow observation, especially all tradeoff lines.

### Clarity

The candidate is clear because it keeps evidence strength, SAFE UNKNOWN, risk, fallback, and escalation visible in one place.

The clearest sections were:

- Evidence Summary
- Uncertainty Review
- Decision Proposal
- Final Operator Checklist

The least useful section for this small issue was the full Tradeoff Review. It helped discipline the decision, but it also slowed the review.

### Usability

Good for a practical operator note. It prevents fake certainty without requiring a long report.

However, the candidate still feels like a form, not a fast field note. For small PPC checks, operators may leave fields blank unless the form is visibly marked as skimmable.

### Unnecessary Sections

- Full tradeoff list is too broad for a one-issue PPC review.
- Repeating evidence source in Review Identity and Evidence Summary is mildly duplicative.
- Outcome fields overlap with Decision Proposal.

### Operator Friction

- Too many mandatory-looking fields.
- Some categories are useful but not always relevant.
- The operator must decide whether to answer every line or skip safely.

## Practical Verdict

The candidate is faster and clearer than the original multi-file flow for this small review, but only if operators are allowed to keep answers short.

It is not yet a perfect fast template. It is a usable additive shortcut with mild overbreadth.
