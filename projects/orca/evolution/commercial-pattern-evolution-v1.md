# Commercial Pattern Evolution v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA tracks changes in visible commercial patterns such as offers, CTAs, trust signals, aggregator pressure, and local competition. Pattern evolution remains evidence-based and human-supervised.

ORCA observes reality. ORCA does not define reality. Commercial patterns can decay, split by region, or be contradicted by newer evidence.

## Pattern Types

- offer framing;
- CTA structure;
- trust architecture;
- price sensitivity;
- urgency pressure;
- aggregator pressure;
- local pack dependence;
- review density;
- competitor positioning;
- mobile-first commercial behavior;
- commercial maturity.

## Evolution Rules

- Identify the baseline pattern and its original evidence.
- Attach newer evidence before describing change.
- Mark whether the change is repeated, isolated, regional, seasonal, or device-specific.
- Preserve contradictions instead of smoothing them into a cleaner story.
- Downgrade old patterns when newer observations reduce reliability.
- Do not convert emerging signals into rules without repeatability.
- Do not generalize from one niche to another without comparable evidence.

## Pattern Status

- `new_signal` - observed but weak.
- `strengthening` - repeated newer evidence increases reliability.
- `stable` - repeated evidence supports continued use in the same context.
- `fragmenting` - evidence differs by region, device, niche, or season.
- `weakening` - newer evidence reduces reliability.
- `obsolete` - pattern is no longer supported by current evidence.
- `contradicted` - conflicting evidence must be resolved or preserved.

## Boundary

This model supports research-oriented commercial intelligence. It is not autonomous trend detection, market prediction, runtime analytics, or campaign automation.
