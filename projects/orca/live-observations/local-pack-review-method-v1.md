# Local Pack Review Method v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA reviewers manually evaluate local pack evidence in live SERP reviews. Local pack observations are regional, volatile, and time-bound.

## Required Context

- reviewer;
- timestamp and timezone;
- region and city;
- search engine or map surface;
- exact query;
- device;
- location permission or localization notes;
- screenshot reference.

## What To Evaluate

- local pack presence and placement;
- map dominance and screen share;
- business names visible;
- review counts;
- review recency when visible;
- rating spread;
- WhatsApp or messenger visibility;
- phone prominence;
- website or route buttons;
- local trust markers;
- response messaging;
- operational hours;
- regional relevance;
- distance or service-area cues.

## Review Steps

1. Record whether the local pack appears for the query.
2. Capture visible businesses and trust markers.
3. Note whether direct contact is easier than website navigation.
4. Record review count and recency only when visible.
5. Compare local pack claims with landing pages when reviewed.
6. Mark contradictions, stale risk, and SAFE UNKNOWN items.

## Interpretation Rules

- Do not treat one local pack as stable dominance.
- Do not infer operational quality from rating alone.
- Do not assume review recency if it is not visible.
- Record device and region because local pack behavior can change sharply.
- Treat map dominance as evidence of SERP layout pressure, not proof of business performance.

## Boundary

This method is for human-supervised local pack observation. ORCA does not scrape maps, monitor rankings, automate browser checks, or produce live telemetry.
