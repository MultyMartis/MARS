# Competitor Landing Review Method v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA reviewers manually evaluate competitor landing pages reached from live SERP observations. Landing reviews are evidence inputs, not proof of conversion performance.

## Required Context

- reviewer;
- timestamp and timezone;
- source SERP query;
- region and device;
- landing URL;
- access path from SERP;
- screenshot reference;
- notes on popups, redirects, or localization.

## What To Evaluate

- CTA clarity;
- trust architecture;
- pricing visibility;
- urgency handling;
- mobile friction;
- conversion friction;
- response promises;
- differentiation;
- local trust signals;
- phone, WhatsApp, messenger, and form visibility;
- proof elements such as reviews, cases, photos, guarantees, certificates, and service areas.

## Review Steps

1. Record landing URL and source query.
2. Capture first-screen offer, CTA, trust, and pricing signals.
3. Review mobile usability when mobile SERP was the source.
4. Note conversion path length and friction.
5. Compare landing claims with SERP ad or snippet claims.
6. Preserve contradictions and SAFE UNKNOWN notes.

## Interpretation Rules

- Do not infer conversion rate from landing design.
- Do not assume offer truth without external validation.
- Treat response promises as claims, not verified operations.
- Separate visible trust architecture from actual trustworthiness.
- Record stale risk because landing pages change without notice.

## Boundary

This method supports manual competitor landing review. It is not automated crawling, browser automation, telemetry, live UX monitoring, or autonomous market intelligence.
