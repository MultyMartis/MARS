# Desktop SERP Review Method v1

## Purpose

Defines a practical method for human-reviewed desktop SERP observation. Desktop observations are time-bound and may conflict with mobile results.

## Required Context

- reviewer;
- timestamp and timezone;
- region;
- desktop device or screen class;
- browser context;
- search engine;
- exact query;
- localization and personalization notes;
- screenshot reference.

## Review Steps

1. Record review context before search.
2. Capture above-the-fold results.
3. Record visible ad blocks, local pack, organic results, and aggregators.
4. Scroll manually through the first result page.
5. Note differences from mobile review when available.
6. Record contradictions and confidence impact.

## What To Observe

- top and bottom ad blocks;
- visible ad density;
- local pack presence and placement;
- organic overlap with paid advertisers;
- aggregator, marketplace, directory, and map-service presence;
- CTA patterns in titles, descriptions, sitelinks, and snippets;
- trust patterns such as ratings, reviews, guarantees, certifications, and years in business;
- pricing visibility;
- urgency claims;
- informational contamination in commercial queries.

## Desktop-Specific Notes

- More results may be visible above the fold than on mobile.
- Sitelinks and expanded snippets can reveal trust architecture.
- Desktop local pack may be less dominant than mobile in some regions.
- Desktop layout cannot be used as proof of mobile behavior.

## Interpretation Rules

- Keep desktop and mobile findings separate unless both were reviewed.
- Treat layout and result order as volatile.
- Do not infer ad spend, bidding strategy, or conversion performance.
- Preserve regional and timestamp boundaries.
- Use SAFE UNKNOWN when personalization or localization cannot be assessed.

## Boundary

This method supports manual desktop SERP review. It is not browser automation, scraping, telemetry, monitoring, or autonomous market observation.
