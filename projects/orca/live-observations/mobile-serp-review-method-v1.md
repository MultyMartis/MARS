# Mobile SERP Review Method v1

## Purpose

Defines a practical method for human-reviewed mobile SERP observation. Mobile SERPs are time-bound, region-sensitive, and volatile. Screenshots can become outdated quickly.

## Required Context

- reviewer;
- timestamp and timezone;
- region;
- device model or device class;
- browser or app context;
- search engine;
- exact query;
- localization and personalization notes;
- screenshot reference.

## Review Steps

1. Record the exact query before searching.
2. Capture top visible results without scrolling.
3. Scroll manually and record ad, local pack, organic, and aggregator sections.
4. Open only pages selected for landing review and record URLs manually.
5. Compare visible claims against prior ORCA notes.
6. Mark contradictions and SAFE UNKNOWN items.

## What To Observe

- top ad block count and visible ad labels;
- local pack position and visibility;
- organic overlap with paid competitors;
- aggregator and directory presence;
- click-to-call, WhatsApp, messenger, and form CTAs;
- pricing visibility;
- urgency signals such as today, 24/7, fast dispatch, limited availability;
- trust patterns such as reviews, ratings, guarantees, certifications, photos;
- mobile friction such as intrusive popups, slow pages, hidden phone, or long forms.

## Mobile-Specific Notes

- Fold position matters because mobile screen space is limited.
- Tap-to-call and messenger visibility may be stronger signals than long copy.
- Local pack and map cards may dominate more than desktop organic results.
- Personalization, location permissions, app state, and recent searches can distort results.

## Interpretation Rules

- Describe visible evidence first.
- Do not infer market dominance from one mobile snapshot.
- Do not treat mobile findings as desktop findings.
- Mark regional transfer as unvalidated unless separately reviewed.
- Keep conclusions revisable.

## Boundary

This method supports manual mobile SERP review. ORCA does not autonomously inspect mobile SERPs, run browser automation, scrape results, or generate live market intelligence.
