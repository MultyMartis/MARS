# Confidence Review v1

## Purpose

Limit certainty after the pilot and prevent overclaiming from one live SERP session.

## Simple Confidence Scale

- `LOW` - one snapshot, weak evidence, unstable result, or unresolved contradiction.
- `MEDIUM` - repeated across several reviewed queries or competitors in this session.

Do not use `HIGH` for the first real pilot unless the evidence is repeated, current, screenshot-supported, and not contradicted inside the session.
