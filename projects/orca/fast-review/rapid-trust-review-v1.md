# Rapid Trust Review v1

## Status

Manual trust scan. Not reputation analysis, automated verification, telemetry, or proof of trustworthiness.

## Purpose

Identify visible trust mechanics that may affect PPC click quality, conversion confidence, or offer credibility.

## Review Only

- reviews or rating prominence;
- licenses, guarantees, warranties, or certifications;
- years in business or local proof;
- before-after or proof assets;
- response time or emergency claims;
- contact visibility;
- trust mismatch between ad promise and landing proof.

## Skip

- decorative badges with unclear meaning;
- unsupported trust narratives;
- tiny differences in wording;
- trust claims that cannot affect PPC action;
- endless inventory of every proof element.

## Output

- visible trust pattern:
- strength:
- evidence:
- PPC implication:
- action:
- SAFE UNKNOWN:

## Compression Discipline

Usefulness matters more than collecting every proof point. Operator fatigue can make trust review less accurate.

## Boundary

This review rejects fake completeness, unsupported trust certainty, and giant proof audits.
