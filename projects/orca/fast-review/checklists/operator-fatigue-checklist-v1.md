# Operator Fatigue Checklist v1

## Status

Manual self-check. Not wellness monitoring, telemetry, or productivity scoring.

## Fatigue Signals

- notes are getting longer but less actionable;
- weak observations are being promoted;
- operator is chasing completeness;
- review time keeps expanding;
- findings repeat without changing action;
- SAFE UNKNOWN is avoided by guessing;
- methodology becomes more important than usefulness.

## Corrective Action

- stop the review;
- summarize the top three signals;
- mark SAFE UNKNOWN;
- defer low-value questions;
- return only if new evidence is needed for a decision.

## Compression Reminder

Operator attention is limited. Fatigue reduces PPC judgment quality and increases false complexity.

## Boundary

This checklist rejects endless note-taking, fake completeness, and theoretical perfectionism.
