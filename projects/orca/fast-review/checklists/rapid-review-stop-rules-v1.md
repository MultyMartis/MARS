# Rapid Review Stop Rules v1

## Status

Manual stop rules. Not automated workflow control.

## Stop Immediately When

- the next note would not change PPC action;
- the review is becoming a giant audit;
- evidence is weak and speculative;
- operator fatigue is visible;
- local, aggregator, CTA, trust, semantic, landing, urgency, and mobile signals have been checked;
- the remaining questions require external verification.

## Do Not Continue For

- fake completeness;
- theoretical perfectionism;
- methodology addiction;
- cosmetic detail;
- unsupported signal importance;
- longer documentation for its own sake.

## Required Closing Note

- top signal:
- top risk:
- action:
- SAFE UNKNOWN:
- reason stopped:

## Boundary

Diminishing returns exist. Over-analysis is dangerous because it can make weak evidence look important.
