# Rapid Semantic Review v1

## Status

Manual semantic scan. Not NLP architecture, AI classification, automation, or semantic intelligence.

## Purpose

Detect obvious semantic contamination that could weaken PPC relevance, intent alignment, or landing consistency.

## Review Only

- service intent mismatch;
- informational intent leaking into commercial terms;
- job, DIY, definition, or research meanings;
- location ambiguity;
- urgent-intent mismatch;
- terms that conflict with the landing offer;
- strong SERP evidence that a term means something else.

## Skip

- weak semantic edge cases;
- theoretical taxonomy work;
- over-labeled keyword nuance;
- observations with no likely PPC action;
- long lists of low-impact variants.

## Output

- term or cluster:
- contamination type:
- evidence:
- PPC risk:
- action:
- SAFE UNKNOWN:

## Compression Discipline

Not every semantic observation matters. Weak signals should not dominate review time.

## Boundary

This review rejects abstraction overload, giant semantic maps, fake sophistication, and methodology addiction.
