# CTA Pattern Review v1

## Use When

Use when ads or landing pages feel weak, vague, or mismatched against competitor pressure.

## Review Flow

1. Compare top visible competitor CTAs.
2. Compare current ad CTA.
3. Compare landing first-screen CTA.
4. Decide whether CTA mismatch is a PPC risk.

## Check

- Is the action obvious?
- Does the CTA match intent: call, price, consultation, estimate, delivery, booking?
- Is the CTA mobile-friendly?
- Is the CTA supported by trust or proof?
- Does the page ask for form completion when a call-first intent is likely?

## Strong CTA Signals

- clear phone action;
- fast estimate;
- price or quote request;
- local service callout;
- urgent availability;
- low-friction messenger or callback.

## Weak CTA Signals

- generic "learn more";
- multiple competing buttons;
- CTA below the first mobile screen;
- promise in ad not repeated on page;
- no clear next step.

## Stop Rule

Do not rewrite all copy. Capture the CTA gap and one practical correction candidate.
