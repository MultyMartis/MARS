# Mobile Friction Review v1

## Use When

Use when mobile traffic is likely important or the landing page may block conversion on phone.

## Review Flow

1. Open the landing page on mobile viewport or real phone.
2. Review only the first screen and the first CTA path.
3. Check whether a motivated user can act quickly.
4. Record blockers, not design preferences.

## Check

- Page loads and becomes readable quickly.
- Service and geo are visible without deep scrolling.
- CTA is visible, tappable, and clear.
- Phone number or callback path is easy to use.
- Forms are short enough for mobile.
- Sticky elements do not hide content or controls.
- Trust proof appears before heavy commitment.

## Friction Signals

- tiny tap targets;
- hidden phone number;
- long form before value is clear;
- slow hero media;
- cluttered first screen;
- popup blocking the CTA;
- unclear price or service scope.

## Decision Cues

- Send to landing fix list.
- Use a better landing page.
- Reduce ad pressure until mismatch is resolved.
- Mark as SAFE UNKNOWN if real device behavior was not checked.

## Stop Rule

Stop after the first 3 material blockers. More design notes are out of scope.
