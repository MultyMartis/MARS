# 15-Minute SERP Review v1

## Status

Manual time-boxed SERP scan. Not scraping, monitoring, telemetry, or ranking intelligence.

## Purpose

Identify the strongest PPC-facing SERP pressures quickly enough to support operator action without creating a giant review.

## Review Window

Suggested limit: 15 minutes per query cluster.

## Check Only

- dominant CTA patterns;
- local pack pressure;
- aggregator dominance;
- urgent-intent behavior;
- trust patterns in visible results;
- pricing or offer clarity when obvious;
- mobile friction when reviewing on mobile.

## Ignore

- minor visual differences;
- weak copy variations;
- SERP clutter with no PPC implication;
- unsupported guesses about competitor strategy;
- details that do not change the PPC decision.

## Output

- query or cluster:
- dominant SERP pressure:
- highest PPC risk:
- evidence:
- action:
- SAFE UNKNOWN:

## Stop Rule

Stop when additional notes only make the review look more complete without improving operator judgment.

## Boundary

This is not a complete SERP audit. It explicitly rejects fake completeness, endless screenshots, and theoretical perfectionism.
