# Fast Review Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA Fast Review Mode may help a human operator conduct short PPC reviews and capture high-signal observations.

It does not prove market truth, automate research, predict performance, or replace human judgment.

## Explicitly Rejected

- giant audits;
- endless note-taking;
- fake completeness;
- theoretical perfectionism;
- methodology addiction;
- AI optimization narratives;
- telemetry or dashboard claims;
- orchestration or runtime claims.

## Allowed Scope

- fast SERP review;
- fast competitor review;
- fast semantic review;
- fast landing review;
- rapid PPC risk detection;
- high-signal observation capture;
- operator fatigue reduction.

## Required Operator Discipline

- prioritize CTA, trust, local pack, aggregator, semantic, landing, urgency, and mobile signals;
- ignore noise;
- stop when additional review has diminishing returns;
- record SAFE UNKNOWN instead of guessing;
- keep notes short enough to be used.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know true competitor performance, conversion rates, lead quality, account economics, hidden landing tests, or current auction dynamics unless independently verified by a human.
