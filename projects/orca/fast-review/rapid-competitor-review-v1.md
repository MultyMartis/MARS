# Rapid Competitor Review v1

## Status

Manual competitor scan. Not competitive intelligence, automation, scraping, or proof of competitor performance.

## Purpose

Find only the competitor patterns that may affect PPC messaging, offer framing, trust proof, or landing alignment.

## High-Signal Competitor Checks

- repeated CTA patterns;
- repeated trust mechanics;
- pricing transparency or absence;
- urgent-intent response promises;
- local proof or coverage claims;
- aggregator pressure against individual providers;
- landing flow friction on mobile.

## Low-Value Notes to Skip

- color choices;
- minor layout differences;
- unsupported assumptions about budgets;
- speculative strategy narratives;
- one-off claims with no visible PPC implication.

## Rapid Output

- competitor type:
- strongest visible pattern:
- PPC implication:
- supporting evidence:
- operator action:
- SAFE UNKNOWN:

## Compression Discipline

Speed matters. A competitor review is useful only when it identifies a pattern strong enough to affect PPC choices.

## Boundary

This review rejects giant competitor audits, fake completeness, methodology addiction, and unsupported claims of market superiority.
