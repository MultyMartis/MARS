# Rapid Landing Review v1

## Status

Manual landing scan. Not UX research, conversion prediction, telemetry, or automated page scoring.

## Purpose

Find landing issues that can quickly weaken PPC performance assumptions or create campaign risk.

## Review Only

- landing mismatch against query, ad, or offer;
- dominant CTA clarity;
- mobile friction;
- contact path friction;
- pricing or offer clarity;
- trust proof visibility;
- urgent-intent support;
- local relevance support.

## Skip

- cosmetic preferences;
- generic UX commentary;
- micro-copy notes with no action;
- perfectionist redesign ideas;
- full-page audits unless a major PPC risk is visible.

## Output

- landing URL:
- strongest mismatch or friction:
- evidence:
- PPC risk:
- action:
- SAFE UNKNOWN:

## Compression Discipline

Fast landing review should stop at decision-useful evidence. More notes can reduce clarity.

## Boundary

This review rejects giant landing audits, theoretical perfectionism, and unsupported conversion certainty.
