# Conversion Friction Rules v1

## Purpose

Identify obvious PPC conversion friction without turning the review into a full UX audit.

## Friction Types

- unclear service;
- unclear next action;
- trust gaps;
- contact friction;
- mobile friction;
- urgency mismatch;
- expectation mismatch;
- pricing confusion;
- geo uncertainty.

## High-Impact Friction

Prioritize friction that can block a paid-search user from acting:

- user cannot tell if the service matches the query;
- phone or WhatsApp is hard to find;
- form asks too much too early;
- urgent ad leads to slow generic page;
- trust claim in ad is unsupported;
- city or service area is unclear;
- pricing promise is not explained.

## Low-Value Review Areas

Do not spend the first PPC review on:

- decorative preferences;
- broad branding theory;
- minor spacing opinions;
- full content strategy;
- abstract UX maturity scoring.

## Operator Rule

If the friction does not affect PPC relevance, trust, CTA, or contact behavior, record it only if it is severe.

## Boundary

ORCA does not measure conversion rate or optimize the page. Observations remain revisable.
