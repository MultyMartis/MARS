# Intent-To-Landing Rules v1

## Purpose

Check whether the landing page actually answers the query and ad intent.

## Review Questions

- Does the page show the searched service immediately?
- Does the page support commercial intent?
- Does the page match urgent intent when urgency was promised?
- Is local relevance visible?
- Does the page explain what the user should do next?
- Does the page avoid drifting into unrelated services?

## Strong Alignment

Strong alignment exists when:

- the service is clear above or near the first screen;
- the location or service area is visible;
- the CTA matches the ad;
- trust proof supports the decision;
- pricing or estimate logic is not misleading;
- urgent claims are supported by contact and availability.

## Mismatch Signals

- query asks one service, page emphasizes another;
- ad promises urgency, page feels slow or generic;
- city or district is absent;
- CTA is hard to find;
- page is informational when the query is commercial;
- page looks like a category dump instead of a service answer.

## Boundary

ORCA records observed alignment risk. It does not infer conversion outcomes or optimize the page autonomously.
