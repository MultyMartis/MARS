# Landing Mismatch Risks v1

## Purpose

List common PPC landing mismatch risks for human review.

## Risk Types

- misleading intent alignment;
- wrong-service landing;
- geo mismatch;
- fake urgency mismatch;
- weak trust support;
- low-commercial-intent mismatch;
- aggregator-style confusion;
- CTA mismatch;
- pricing mismatch;
- mobile mismatch.

## Wrong-Service Landing

Occurs when the query or ad promises one service but the page emphasizes another.

Examples:

- equipment rental query leads to equipment sales page;
- urgent repair query leads to general company page;
- local service query leads to broad category page.

## Geo Mismatch

Occurs when:

- ad mentions a city not visible on the page;
- service area is unclear;
- local proof is missing;
- page appears to serve another region.

## Fake Urgency Mismatch

Occurs when:

- ad promises urgent help;
- landing has no urgent contact path;
- response time is unclear;
- availability is unsupported.

## Aggregator-Style Confusion

Occurs when a landing feels like a directory, comparison page, or generic category instead of a clear service provider page.

## Boundary

ORCA identifies risks for human review. It does not decide business truth or conversion certainty automatically.
