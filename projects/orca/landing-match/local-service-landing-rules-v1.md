# Local Service Landing Rules v1

## Purpose

Support practical landing review for local-service PPC traffic.

## Supported Business Types

- dispatch-style businesses;
- emergency services;
- cargo and moving services;
- equipment services;
- local-response businesses;
- repair and installation services.

## Local-Service Essentials

A landing should make clear:

- what service is offered;
- where the service is available;
- how to contact the business;
- how quickly or when they can respond if claimed;
- what information the user should provide;
- why the business is credible enough to call.

## Dispatch-Style Review

For dispatch services, check:

- route or location details needed;
- equipment or team availability;
- response promise;
- phone and messenger access;
- estimate process;
- operating area;
- trust proof near CTA.

## Emergency-Service Review

For emergency intent, check:

- urgent CTA visibility;
- availability truthfulness;
- response expectation clarity;
- mobile contact speed;
- no fake pressure;
- service fit for the emergency query.

## Boundary

ORCA does not verify live operations or optimize websites. Human review is mandatory.
