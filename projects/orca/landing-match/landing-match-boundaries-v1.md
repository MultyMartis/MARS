# Landing Match Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA may help operators review PPC landing alignment manually.

ORCA does not autonomously optimize websites, run CRO experiments, track behavior, analyze heatmaps, change pages, or claim conversion certainty.

## Explicitly Rejected

- autonomous CRO claims;
- fake conversion certainty;
- unsupported UX certainty;
- runtime optimization claims;
- automated business conclusions;
- heatmap interpretation systems;
- behavioral tracking systems;
- website editing automation;
- automatic landing scoring.

## Required Human Review

A human must review:

- query and page relevance;
- service accuracy;
- geo accuracy;
- CTA support;
- trust evidence;
- pricing evidence;
- mobile usability;
- business reality;
- current landing content.

## Review Discipline

- Record observed friction, not universal UX theory.
- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Mark weak evidence as `SAFE_UNKNOWN`.
- Do not infer conversion rate from appearance.
- Do not claim a page will or will not convert without performance evidence.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know actual conversion rate, lead quality, analytics, user behavior, A/B test results, heatmap data, business capacity, or future landing performance unless verified by a human.
