# Pricing Transparency Rules v1

## Purpose

Review whether landing pricing supports PPC traffic without misleading users.

## When Visible Pricing Helps

Visible pricing can help when:

- the price is current and real;
- the service has strong price-comparison behavior;
- the landing explains what is included;
- the price improves trust;
- competitors use confusing or hidden pricing.

## When Visible Pricing Hurts

Visible pricing can hurt when:

- final cost depends on route, equipment, volume, or time;
- the visible price is incomplete;
- fake cheapness attracts poor-fit leads;
- the business cannot support the price;
- price distracts from trust and service fit.

## Estimate Request Logic

Use estimate logic when price depends on variables.

Good estimate support explains:

- what details are needed;
- how the estimate is calculated;
- how to request it;
- whether call or WhatsApp is better;
- what can change the final cost.

## Trust vs Pricing Tradeoff

Cheap-looking pages can reduce trust if pricing feels incomplete or manipulative.

For local services, clear estimate logic may be more credible than an unsupported low anchor.

## Boundary

ORCA does not verify pricing accuracy automatically or predict pricing impact. Human review is mandatory.
