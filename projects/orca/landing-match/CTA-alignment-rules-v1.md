# CTA Alignment Rules v1

## Purpose

Review whether the landing page CTA matches PPC intent and real local-service behavior.

## CTA Review Areas

- call-first logic;
- WhatsApp visibility;
- response clarity;
- estimate request flow;
- contact accessibility;
- CTA overload risks.

## Call-First Logic

Call-first alignment is strong when:

- phone is visible quickly;
- mobile click-to-call is easy;
- the service benefits from immediate conversation;
- urgent intent is supported;
- response hours or availability are clear enough.

## WhatsApp Visibility

WhatsApp or messenger alignment is useful when:

- users may send photos, location, or details;
- operator response is real;
- the landing clearly supports chat action;
- WhatsApp does not hide the primary phone path when calls matter.

## Estimate Request Flow

Estimate flow should be clear when price depends on:

- distance;
- equipment;
- timing;
- volume;
- task complexity.

## CTA Overload Risks

CTA overload appears when:

- call, form, chat, callback, and calculator all compete;
- the primary action is unclear;
- urgent users must choose too much;
- mobile screen space is cluttered.

## Boundary

ORCA does not configure calls, forms, chats, tracking, or landing behavior. Human review is mandatory.
