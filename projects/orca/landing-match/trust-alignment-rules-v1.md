# Trust Alignment Rules v1

## Purpose

Review whether landing trust signals support the ad promise and local-service buying decision.

## Trust Priorities

- local trust signals;
- equipment or service proof;
- review visibility;
- operational transparency;
- business legitimacy signals;
- response expectation clarity.

## Useful Trust Signals

- real service area;
- visible phone and working hours;
- photos of equipment, team, or work;
- review count or review snippets if current;
- legal/company details where relevant;
- clear process explanation;
- estimate or dispatch logic;
- guarantees only when real and supported.

## Weak Trust Support

- vague "best quality" claims;
- stock-like proof without context;
- no service area;
- no response expectation;
- no equipment/service evidence;
- reviews mentioned but not visible;
- claims in ad not supported on page.

## Review Rule

Trust alignment is not decoration. It must reduce commercial uncertainty for the traffic being purchased.

If the ad uses a trust claim, the landing should support it visibly.

## Boundary

ORCA does not validate business truth automatically. Human review and evidence quality are mandatory.
