# Fictional Trust Mismatch Example v1

## Status

Fictional demonstration only. Non-production. Non-validated.

This is not a verified business audit or conversion prediction.

## Scenario

- niche: fictional equipment service;
- region: fictional city;
- ad promise: experienced team and reliable equipment;
- review purpose: show trust mismatch risk.

## Observed Mismatch

- landing mentions experience but shows no proof;
- equipment claim is not supported by photos or details;
- service area is vague;
- response promise is visible but hours are unclear;
- pricing says "from low price" without conditions.

## PPC Risk

- ad trust claim may be unsupported;
- user may doubt equipment fit;
- local relevance is weak;
- pricing may look like fake cheapness;
- moderation or user trust risk may increase.

## Safer Handling

- remove unsupported experience claim;
- add or verify equipment proof;
- clarify service area;
- explain estimate basis;
- mark response promise as `SAFE_UNKNOWN` until verified.

## Boundary

This example is demonstration only. ORCA does not validate business truth or guarantee conversion outcomes.
