# Landing Observation Rules v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA pilot cases record landing page observations for PPC research. Landing observations evaluate fit, friction, trust, and message match. They do not trigger automatic page changes or campaign optimization.

## What To Observe

- landing page type;
- service match;
- regional match;
- query-intent match;
- headline and offer clarity;
- CTA visibility;
- mobile usability cues;
- trust proof;
- price or guarantee visibility;
- form friction;
- load or accessibility concerns when visible;
- policy or claim risks.

## Required Fields

- landing URL or page reference;
- niche;
- region target;
- observed device;
- matched query or cluster;
- fit rating;
- friction notes;
- trust notes;
- CTA notes;
- SAFE UNKNOWN.

## Fit Ratings

- `strong` - service, region, offer, CTA, and trust signals match observed intent.
- `partial` - main service matches, but trust, region, CTA, or offer evidence is incomplete.
- `weak` - page is generic, mismatched, unclear, or likely to create PPC relevance risk.
- `unknown` - evidence is insufficient.

## Interpretation Rules

- Evaluate landing fit against observed intent, not generic design preference.
- Treat region mismatch as a commercial risk.
- Treat CTA absence or friction as a conversion-risk hypothesis, not a proven conversion loss.
- Do not infer conversion rate.
- Do not prescribe page changes without human review.

## SAFE UNKNOWN Examples

- actual conversion performance;
- platform quality score impact;
- unseen mobile behavior;
- analytics data;
- A/B test history;
- compliance review outcome.

## Boundary

Landing observations are research notes for human review. They are not landing page automation, CRO automation, live experiment control, or automatic campaign optimization.
