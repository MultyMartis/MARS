# ORCA Future Testing Boundaries v1

## Status

Testing boundary note for the stabilized starter pack.

This does not create a testing platform, automation, runtime system, governance process, or new methodology.

## Future Testing Should Focus On

Real starter-pack testing should focus on:

- one local-service niche at a time;
- one city or tight service region;
- one small campaign or campaign draft;
- 5-10 seed queries;
- mobile-first SERP and landing review;
- semantic cleanliness;
- landing fit;
- CTA clarity;
- trust and claim support;
- SAFE UNKNOWN and escalation behavior;
- whether the operator can finish without opening extra layers.

The main question:

`Does the starter pack help a PPC operator make a better small-campaign decision faster?`

## Do Not Test Yet

Do not test yet:

- multi-region strategy;
- large account workflows;
- automated SERP collection;
- campaign upload readiness;
- bid optimization;
- conversion-rate claims;
- AI agent orchestration;
- scoring systems;
- full semantic-core production;
- full competitor market maps;
- governance enforcement.

These would expand the surface before the starter core is validated.

## Requires Live Evidence

The following require live evidence before stronger conclusions:

- actual SERP structure;
- real competitor ads and landings;
- CPC ranges;
- search-term waste;
- conversion rate;
- lead quality;
- call quality;
- moderation outcomes;
- landing performance;
- repeatability across weeks;
- differences across cities or niches.

Without live evidence, use `SAFE UNKNOWN`.

## Requires Human Validation

Human validation is required for:

- business availability;
- service area;
- response-time claims;
- 24/7 claims;
- price claims;
- guarantees;
- licenses or compliance claims;
- brand risk;
- budget risk;
- final campaign launch readiness.

ORCA can structure review, but it cannot approve these claims alone.

## Should Remain Optional

Keep optional unless the operator hits a real need:

- essential signal filtering;
- ad-copy boundary review;
- final campaign QA boundary review;
- one-line evidence template;
- deeper competitor review;
- deeper desktop comparison;
- negative keyword templates.

Optional means available, not visible by default.

## Testing Stop Rules

Stop a future test when:

- the next PPC action is clear;
- new notes repeat old findings;
- evidence is too weak to support more conclusions;
- the operator opens documents to feel safer, not to decide better;
- fatigue appears;
- the session starts proving ORCA instead of testing usefulness.

## Testing Output

Each real test should produce only:

- scenario;
- starter-stack layers used;
- decision reached;
- evidence strength;
- SAFE UNKNOWN;
- friction observed;
- optional layers used or not used;
- next practical step.

No giant report is needed for a starter-pack test.
