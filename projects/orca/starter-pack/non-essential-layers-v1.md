# ORCA Non-Essential Layers v1

## Status

Operational classification for starter-pack use.

This document does not delete, deprecate, or redesign existing ORCA material. It only separates starter value from later value.

## Classification Rule

A layer is non-essential for the starter pack when it does not help a normal PPC operator quickly decide:

- is the search intent clean?
- does the landing page match?
- is the CTA clear?
- are claims supported?
- is there enough evidence to proceed?

## Useful Later

Use after several small sessions or when real account data exists:

- deeper competitor review;
- deeper landing examples;
- search-term templates;
- negative keyword review templates;
- tradeoff and uncertainty templates;
- richer mobile / desktop comparisons.

Reason:

These can improve quality later, but they slow the first operator pass.

## Advanced Only

Reserve for senior operators or complex accounts:

- full semantic clustering;
- full campaign assembly review;
- multi-campaign QA;
- large competitor inventories;
- multi-region comparisons;
- pattern libraries;
- contradiction mapping;
- extensive evidence normalization.

Reason:

These require judgment and time. Used too early, they create false precision and operator fatigue.

## Experimental

Treat as non-starter unless explicitly selected for a test:

- candidate templates not yet approved;
- fictional examples used for reasoning practice;
- additive review candidates;
- proposed consolidation materials;
- pilot-case experiments.

Reason:

They may be useful for learning, but normal operators need a stable small path.

## Low Operational Value In Starter Sessions

Keep out of the starter path:

- architecture explanations;
- methodology expansion;
- broad theory;
- exhaustive inventories;
- cleanup planning;
- documentation family maps;
- export package preparation;
- runtime, orchestration, or automation framing.

Reason:

These do not directly improve the first PPC decision for a small local campaign.

## High Maintenance Cost

Watch carefully:

- repeated boundary-only files;
- overlapping anti-fake-certainty warnings;
- duplicate SAFE UNKNOWN reminders;
- many similar templates with slightly different field names;
- documents that require operators to choose between layers before reviewing the market.

Reason:

Maintenance cost is not only editing cost. It is also attention cost.

## Starter-Pack Exclusion Test

Exclude a layer from normal operator view when:

- it takes longer to choose than to use;
- it repeats another visible layer;
- it pushes the operator toward launch approval too early;
- it encourages theory instead of observation;
- it creates a larger report without a clearer PPC action;
- it needs senior judgment to avoid misuse.

## Important Boundary

Non-essential does not mean useless.

It means:

`not needed for the smallest practical ORCA subset that still delivers PPC operational value.`
