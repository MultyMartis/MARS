# ORCA Operational Principles v1

## Status

Concise principle snapshot from starter-pack extraction and pilot simulation.

This is not a new methodology. It is a practical reminder for future operational testing.

## Core Principles

### Simplicity Is Strategic

Small workflows preserve operator attention. A smaller ORCA surface is better when it produces the same PPC decision faster.

### Speed Matters

A useful local PPC review should quickly identify the next action: revise, collect evidence, escalate, or stop.

### Operator Fatigue Is Real

Fatigue weakens judgment. Long notes, repeated fields, and document hunting can make PPC decisions worse.

### Semantic Cleanliness Matters

Dirty intent creates wasted spend. Check service match, commercial intent, geo fit, urgent vs planned intent, and obvious negative candidates early.

### Landing Fit Matters

Traffic must land on a page that supports the query, service, city, CTA, trust proof, and mobile behavior.

### Evidence Matters

Evidence should support the next decision. It does not need to become a giant archive.

### Escalation Is Healthy

Escalate unsupported claims, budget risk, policy risk, business capacity uncertainty, and low-evidence high-impact decisions.

### SAFE UNKNOWN Is Valid

Unknowns are not failures. Guessing is worse than marking a gap honestly.

### Document Selection Load Is Operational Cost

Every visible layer asks for operator attention. Too many choices can slow the workflow before review even starts.

### Keep Closed Unless Useful

If a layer does not improve the next PPC decision, keep it closed.

## Practical Test

An ORCA layer earns starter-pack visibility only if it helps answer at least one of these:

- is the search intent clean?
- does the landing page match?
- is the CTA clear on mobile?
- are trust, price, urgency, and guarantee claims supported?
- is there enough evidence to proceed?
- should the operator escalate or stop?

## Boundary

ORCA operational principles support human-reviewed PPC judgment.

They do not create automation, launch approval, performance certainty, or autonomous PPC intelligence.
