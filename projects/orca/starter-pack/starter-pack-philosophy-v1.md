# ORCA Starter Pack Philosophy v1

## Status

Practical philosophy for the smallest useful ORCA operator subset.

This is not governance, architecture, automation, or a new methodology.

## Core Position

The starter pack exists to reduce PPC waste and operator confusion.

It should make a small local campaign easier to judge, not make ORCA look bigger.

## Simplicity Is Strategic

Small is not a compromise here.

For a real operator, a smaller path means:

- faster review;
- fewer wrong turns;
- less fatigue;
- clearer action;
- less fake certainty.

If a layer does not improve the next PPC decision, it should stay closed.

## Speed Matters

Local-service PPC often needs direction before perfection.

The starter pack should quickly answer:

- is the intent commercially clean?
- is the landing page aligned?
- is the CTA obvious on mobile?
- are trust and price claims supported?
- should the operator revise, escalate, collect evidence, or stop?

Slow review is only useful when it changes a material decision.

## Operator Fatigue Is Real

Fatigue creates bad PPC judgment.

Warning signs:

- notes get longer but less actionable;
- weak evidence becomes a strong claim;
- the operator opens more documents than SERPs;
- templates are filled mechanically;
- SAFE UNKNOWN is avoided by guessing.

The correct response is compression, not another layer.

## Semantic Cleanliness Matters

Search-term waste is one of the fastest PPC risks to catch.

The starter pack should prioritize:

- service match;
- commercial intent;
- geo fit;
- urgent vs planned intent;
- DIY, vacancy, materials, comparison, and low-intent leakage;
- cautious negative keyword decisions.

Cleanliness matters, but overblocking from weak evidence is also risk.

## Landing Fit Matters

Traffic quality is wasted if the landing page does not support the query.

The starter pack should check:

- service match;
- city / region match;
- phone and CTA clarity;
- mobile friction;
- trust proof;
- price and guarantee support;
- business reality.

It must not claim conversion certainty without performance evidence.

## Evidence Matters

Evidence should be enough to support the next action.

It does not need to become a giant archive.

Good starter evidence is:

- named;
- current enough for the decision;
- bounded by device, region, and query;
- honest about confidence;
- explicit about SAFE UNKNOWN.

## Fake Sophistication Is Dangerous

Complex language can hide weak PPC evidence.

Avoid:

- fake market certainty;
- AI mythology;
- automated intelligence claims;
- universal rules from one snapshot;
- launch confidence from a small review;
- sophisticated reports that do not change action.

The operator should trust the evidence, not the vocabulary.

## Unused Docs Become Liabilities

A document that is not used still has cost:

- selection cost;
- maintenance cost;
- attention cost;
- contradiction risk;
- onboarding friction.

The starter pack should make unused layers invisible by default.

## Practical Rule

The smallest useful ORCA starter pack is successful when it helps one operator make this kind of decision:

`This traffic is risky because intent and landing fit are not clean enough yet. Fix the obvious mismatch, verify claims, record SAFE UNKNOWN, and do not pretend the campaign is ready.`

That is real operational value.
