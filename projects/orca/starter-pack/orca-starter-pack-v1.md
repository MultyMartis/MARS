# ORCA Starter Pack v1

## Status

Operational extraction from the first fictional ORCA pilot.

This is a starter subset for practical PPC review. It is not new architecture, governance, automation, runtime orchestration, or methodology growth.

## Target Operator

- one PPC operator;
- small business;
- local service;
- small campaign;
- limited time;
- practical need: avoid obvious PPC waste and weak landing fit.

## Starter Question

What is the smallest realistic ORCA subset that still delivers PPC operational value?

Answer:

`A short manual review path that catches semantic mismatch, landing mismatch, unsupported claims, mobile CTA friction, and low-evidence launch risk.`

## Minimal Required Layers

Use these first:

- `minimal-real-pilot-workflow-v1.md`
- `session-execution-guide-v1.md`
- `fast-review/fast-review-workflow-v1.md`
- `search-terms-review/search-term-cleanliness-rules-v1.md`
- `landing-match/landing-match-boundaries-v1.md`

Why these stay:

- they create a narrow session path;
- they protect speed;
- they catch the highest-cost PPC risks early;
- they keep uncertainty visible;
- they avoid fake launch confidence.

## Optional Layers

Use only when the decision needs them:

- `essential-signals/essential-signals-model-v1.md`
- `ad-copy/ad-copy-boundaries-v1.md`
- `campaign-qa-assembly/campaign-qa-boundaries-v1.md`
- `operator-decisions/templates/evidence-review-template-candidate-v1.md`

Use them lightly:

- essential signals: when noise is taking over;
- ad copy: after semantic and landing fit are checked;
- campaign QA: at the end, mostly as a stop sign;
- evidence template: one-line evidence guard, not a full form.

## Advanced Layers

Keep closed for normal starter use:

- full semantic clustering;
- full competitor inventories;
- detailed desktop analysis;
- detailed UX audits;
- pattern libraries;
- heuristic expansion;
- large evidence normalization;
- multi-region comparison;
- export or upload readiness.

These may be useful later, but they are not starter-pack material.

## Not Recommended For Normal Operators

Do not expose normal operators to:

- architecture-heavy documents;
- methodology expansion documents;
- governance-heavy documents;
- runtime or automation framing;
- large review chains;
- duplicate boundary files;
- anything that requires more reading than observing.

Reason:

normal PPC operators need better decisions, not a larger system surface.

## Starter Pack Boundary

The starter pack supports human-reviewed PPC judgment only.

It does not:

- launch campaigns;
- approve ads;
- optimize bids;
- scrape SERPs;
- automate QA;
- prove conversion performance;
- create market certainty.

## Success Condition

The starter pack works when the operator can say, quickly and plainly:

- which search intent is risky;
- whether the landing page fits the intent;
- whether the CTA is clear on mobile;
- which claims need proof;
- whether the campaign needs revision, escalation, or more evidence.

If the operator spends more time navigating ORCA than improving PPC judgment, the starter pack is too large.
