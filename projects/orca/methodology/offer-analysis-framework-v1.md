# Offer Analysis Framework v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA evaluates a service offer before PPC architecture and ad copy drafting.

## Analysis Areas

- Core service and target audience.
- Commercial promise.
- Price or quote model.
- Speed, urgency, and availability.
- Geography and service coverage.
- Differentiation against visible competitors.
- Trust proof: reviews, cases, licenses, guarantees, address.
- Lead capture method: call, form, messenger, booking, callback.

## PPC Fit Questions

- Can the offer satisfy hot commercial intent?
- Is the CTA specific enough for the cluster?
- Is urgency real or only marketing language?
- Is pricing transparent enough for price-sensitive queries?
- Does the offer support local lead generation?
- Are regulated or risky claims present?

## Output

- Offer strengths.
- Offer gaps.
- Message angles.
- Unsupported claim risks.
- Required human decisions.
- SAFE UNKNOWN list.

## Boundary

Offer analysis informs human-supervised PPC decisions. It does not create autonomous ads, select bids, control campaigns, or guarantee performance.
