# PPC Research Flow v1

## Purpose

Defines the ORCA research-first flow for PPC preparation. The flow prioritizes operational relevance and quality before campaign generation.

## Flow

1. Intake - clarify offer, geography, landing pages, constraints, and human owner.
2. SERP snapshots - observe Yandex and other search results by query, region, and device.
3. Competitor intelligence - record visible offer, CTA, trust, and aggregator patterns.
4. Semantic collection - expand only from relevant commercial sources and approved seed terms.
5. Intent classification - label hot, warm, cold, urgent, comparison, local, B2B, B2C, branded, competitor, informational, or mixed.
6. Clustering - group by intent, service, geography, audience, and landing fit.
7. QA - remove contamination, duplicates, and unsupported assumptions.
8. Campaign architecture - draft structure for human review.

## Operating Principles

- Research first, generation second.
- Commercial intent over keyword volume.
- Quality over automation.
- Human supervision at every strategic decision.
- SAFE UNKNOWN instead of invented certainty.

## Boundary

This flow is methodology only. It is not runtime implementation, browser automation, scraping infrastructure, autonomous optimization, or bidding automation.
