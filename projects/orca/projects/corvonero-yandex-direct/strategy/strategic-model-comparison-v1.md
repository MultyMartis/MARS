## Model A — Concentrated Core *(SUPERSEDED BY OPERATOR)*

### Composition

| Included | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| **Доработки 1С** | MIG `supported`; strong SERP; hourly model fit |
| **Сопровождение 1С** | MIG `conditionally_supported`; R1 B; retainer upside |
| **Отчёты и печатные формы** | MIG `supported`; scoped intent; site pricing anchor |

**Optional narrow add-on (same landing):** обновление доработанной базы — secondary, same page family.

### Advantages

- Highest evidence confidence among clusters
- Shared B2B «1C partner» narrative fits universal Tilda page
- Lower informational noise than marking or head «programmist» terms
- Fewer dedicated landings required (minor edits vs new pages)

### Disadvantages

- Misses compliance-driven marking demand competitors monetize
- Does not capture urgent troubleshooting volume
- Broad «programmist» head terms excluded — may limit top-of-funnel discovery
- Franchise competitors strong on support/mod SERP (r1q02–r1q04)

### Budget dispersion risk

**Low** — 3 thematic families, same landing intent family.

### Landing requirements

- Universal page: **usable with minor edits** — name configs (УТ, УНФ, Розница, КА, БП), min order 6 000 ₽, remote + NSO geo
- **No** new Tilda pages mandatory for launch interpretation

### Proof requirements

- Hourly rate (on site) — OK
- Min order — **needs operator decision** to publish
- Cases/reviews — strongly recommended; not blocking for test learning **operator decision**
- Configurations list — recommended before paid traffic

### Excluded

- Marking / Честный знак isolated campaigns
- Bitrix / site integration isolated campaigns
- Troubleshooting-led acquisition
- ТС ПИОТ, РМК, product marking niches
- Broad unfiltered «programmist 1C» head campaign

### SAFE UNKNOWN blocking final choice

- Real CPL and lead quality by cluster
- Roman’s margin preference by service
- Whether min order and prices may appear in ads

## Model B — Core Plus Specialist

### Composition

**Core (same as Model A):** доработки + сопровождение + отчёты/формы

**Plus one specialist lane (operator picks one):**

| Option B1 | Честный знак | MIG supported R1; specialist SERP pattern |
| Option B2 | Troubleshooting | r1q10 Grade B; urgency; Model C overlap |
| Option B3 | Website integration | MIG supported but web-studio SERP risk |

**Recommended specialist default for evidence:** **B1 Честный знак** — clearest competitor landing pattern (AB OnlineKassa) and supported R1.

### Advantages

- Tests one differentiated lane without full portfolio spray
- Can learn if specialist landing converts better than universal page
- Aligns with intake service breadth while limiting paid breadth

### Disadvantages

- **Two intent families** — core B2B services vs compliance/marking — must not share one ad group philosophy (future stage)
- Specialist lane likely **requires dedicated Tilda page**
- Marking: informational noise still high even with dedicated page
- B3 (site integration) highest wrong-audience risk

### Budget dispersion risk

**Medium** — two parallel strategic lanes; risk of 50/50 split without learning if not gated.

### Landing requirements

- Core: minor edits on universal page
- Specialist: **dedicated landing required** (Честный знак or integration or urgent-fix variant)

### Proof requirements

- Core proof as Model A
- Specialist: category experience, process, pricing band — **cannot invent** partner/certificates
- Marking: explicit product categories only if operator confirms delivery

### Excluded

- Full marking portfolio (beer, water, medicines, …) simultaneously
- Bitrix without operator charter + page
- ТС ПИОТ, RMK primary focus
- More than **one** specialist lane at start

### SAFE UNKNOWN blocking final choice

- Which specialist Roman prefers to sell
- Whether Roman/Evgenia approve separate Tilda page
- Regulatory/marketing claims allowed for marking

## Model C — Problem-Led

### Composition

| Included | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| **Troubleshooting / «1С не работает»** | r1q10 Grade B; urgency; site mentions audit |
| **Доработки 1С** | Converts fix → customization pipeline |
| **Обновление доработанной базы** | Update pain adjacent to troubleshooting |
| **Отчёты/формы** (optional 4th) | Task-specific entry — lower urgency |

**Excluded by default:** broad «programmist 1C», marking, integrations.

### Advantages

- Problem intent often higher urgency and clearer task description in lead form
- Differentiates from franchise «we do everything» ads
- Matches site «аудит / почему не работает» block

### Disadvantages

- **Vacancy noise** on troubleshooting SERP (r1q10)
- Wordstat: «срочно программист 1С» no-result — urgent phrasing fragile
- May attract small one-off fixes below min order — **operator decision** on qualification
- Weak proof increases distrust for urgent buyers

### Budget dispersion risk

**Low–medium** — 2–3 related problem/task intents; same landing family possible.

### Landing requirements

- Hero/above-fold: urgent fix + phone + response expectation
- State min order / hourly — **operator decision**
- Dedicated «не работает» section prominence — minor edits or section anchor

### Proof requirements

- Response time SLA — **SAFE UNKNOWN** — mandatory before scaling urgent lane
- Cannot claim 24/7 unless true

### Excluded

- Head programmer terms
- Marking/compliance lanes
- Integration studio competition
- ТС ПИОТ

### SAFE UNKNOWN blocking final choice

- Lead handling speed and after-hours coverage
- Minimum project filter (6 000 ₽ enforcement)
- Call tracking / Metrika goals for «urgent» segment
