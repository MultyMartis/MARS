# ORCA Production Method Improvements — v5

1. Generated per-phrase record ≠ human evidence unless phrase-specific reason.
2. Confidence cannot default HIGH from group membership.
3. Commerciality and group ownership are separate gates.
4. Literal collision and semantic-risk checks are separate.
5. Preventive negative changes classified as semantic_risk_correction.
6. Unresolved warnings block PASS.
7. Operator workbooks are controlled deliverables.
8. Placeholder/debug data invalidates QA.
9. Report claims must reconcile with rows and XLSX.
10. Final QA validates exported artefacts.
