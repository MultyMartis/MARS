# Gold / Silver Authority Policy v1

**Status:** `IMPLEMENTED — Wave 3.1`

## Gold

Only records supported by:

- Explicit operator policy
- Unambiguous protected intent (career, education, DIY, navigation, download)
- Verified historical production truth (operator-approved)
- Separately approved expert adjudication

## Silver

Strong but not final evidence:

- Agreement of independent assessors
- Validated deterministic protected-intent rules
- Known semantic patterns (not auto-promoted to gold)

## Diagnostic

Historical model/regex output without validated truth. **Cannot pass D3 gates as ground truth.**

## Adversarial

Purpose-built test cases and minimal pairs.

## Metric rule

D3 commercial precision and protected FPR gates require **gold** authority labels only. Diagnostic labels reported separately.
