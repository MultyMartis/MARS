# Fictional Noise Filter Example v1

## Status

Fictional demonstration only. Non-production. Non-validated. Not a performance claim.

## Context

- market: fictional local plumbing service;
- query cluster: fictional same-day repair terms;
- review type: manual noise-filter example.

## Observations Rejected As Noise

- one competitor used a blue button and another used a green button;
- one headline was shorter by a few words;
- a directory result had a different thumbnail;
- two pages used slightly different testimonial placement;
- a weak assumption was made about competitor budget from ad position.

## Why These Were Noise

- no clear PPC action changed;
- evidence was cosmetic or unsupported;
- documenting them would increase review length without improving judgment;
- stronger signals were available.

## Higher-Value Signals Kept

- repeated call-now CTA pattern;
- pricing transparency difference;
- visible local pack pressure;
- major mobile contact friction on the fictional landing page.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

True customer behavior, conversion performance, lead quality, and competitor economics are unknown.

## Boundary

This example shows how to reject observation inflation. It is not validation, automation, or pseudo-intelligence.
