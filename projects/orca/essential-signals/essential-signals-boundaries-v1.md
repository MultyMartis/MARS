# Essential Signals Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA Essential Signals may help a human operator prioritize PPC observations and filter low-value noise.

It does not create autonomous intelligence, telemetry, dashboards, optimization systems, or proof of performance.

## Explicitly Rejected

- fake sophistication;
- observation inflation;
- abstraction overload;
- unsupported signal importance;
- pseudo-intelligence narratives;
- AI optimization claims;
- hidden automation;
- giant governance.

## Allowed Scope

- high-value observation detection;
- PPC signal prioritization;
- commercial signal identification;
- trust signal prioritization;
- landing signal prioritization;
- semantic signal prioritization;
- noise filtering.

## Required Operator Discipline

- not all observations are equally valuable;
- signal quality matters more than volume;
- evidence quality matters;
- operator attention is limited;
- weak signals should not dominate review time;
- SAFE UNKNOWN is better than invented importance.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know true conversion impact, auction economics, competitor performance, user psychology, or business value unless verified through reliable human-reviewed evidence.
