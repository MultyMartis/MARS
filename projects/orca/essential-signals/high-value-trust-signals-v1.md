# High-Value Trust Signals v1

## Status

Manual trust signal guide. Not reputation intelligence, automated verification, or proof of trustworthiness.

## High-Value Trust Signals

- dominant trust mechanics across competitors;
- review or rating prominence;
- visible guarantees, licenses, warranties, or certifications;
- strong local proof;
- response-time proof for urgent intent;
- clear proof supporting the landing CTA;
- obvious trust mismatch between ad, SERP, and landing page.

## Evidence Standard

A trust signal is high-value only when it can plausibly affect PPC confidence, click quality, or conversion friction.

## Low-Value Trust Noise

- decorative badges with unclear meaning;
- every small testimonial variation;
- unsupported assumptions about credibility;
- trust claims with no visible support;
- low-impact differences that do not change PPC action.

## Operator Action

Record the strongest trust mechanic, the evidence, the risk or opportunity, and SAFE UNKNOWN when verification is missing.

## Boundary

This guide rejects fake sophistication, unsupported trust importance, and giant trust inventories.
