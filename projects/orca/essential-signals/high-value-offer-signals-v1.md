# High-Value Offer Signals v1

## Status

Manual offer signal guide. Not business intelligence, conversion prediction, or automated offer optimization.

## High-Value Offer Signals

- high commercial clarity;
- pricing transparency patterns;
- urgent-intent mechanics;
- dominant CTA pattern;
- strong trust mechanics supporting the offer;
- local availability or response clarity;
- clear differentiation that affects PPC messaging;
- offer mismatch between SERP, ad, and landing.

## Evidence Standard

An offer signal is high-value only when it is visible, commercially meaningful, and likely to change ad copy, landing alignment, or campaign risk review.

## Low-Value Offer Noise

- vague branding opinions;
- unsupported assumptions about customer psychology;
- low-impact wording differences;
- speculative competitor economics;
- offer notes with no action.

## Operator Action

Record the offer pattern, evidence, PPC implication, and SAFE UNKNOWN where real performance or economics are not verified.

## Boundary

This guide rejects fake sophistication, unsupported offer superiority, and pseudo-intelligence narratives.
