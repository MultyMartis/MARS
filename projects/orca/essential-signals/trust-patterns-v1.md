# Trust Patterns v1

## Use When

Use when the market requires proof before a user will click, call, or submit a form.

## High-Value Signals

- reviews or ratings visible in SERP or landing;
- real photos, cases, fleet, team, certificates, or completed work;
- warranty, guarantees, official status, or years in market;
- local address, map, service area, or regional proof;
- price transparency or estimate method;
- phone-first contact and response time.

## Weak Signals

- vague "quality service";
- stock imagery without proof;
- unsupported "best" claims;
- hidden company identity;
- trust blocks below heavy conversion pressure.

## Operator Decision

Ask: does the current ad and landing page answer the trust pattern users see in SERP?

If not, record the missing proof and decide whether to adjust copy, landing, or campaign expectations.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

If proof cannot be verified from visible SERP or page evidence, mark it unknown. Do not invent credibility.
