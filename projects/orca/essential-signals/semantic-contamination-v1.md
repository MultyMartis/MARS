# Semantic Contamination v1

## Use When

Use when a keyword set, search-term report, or SERP contains mixed intent that can waste PPC spend.

## Contamination Signals

- informational intent mixed into commercial queries;
- DIY, free, instruction, photo, review-only, or job-seeking terms;
- wrong product, wrong geography, wrong price segment, or wrong audience;
- broad modifier creates unrelated services;
- competitor or aggregator intent not intended for the campaign;
- query expects comparison, but landing sells one offer.

## Fast Review

1. Mark each suspect term as commercial, mixed, or irrelevant.
2. Capture only terms that can change negatives, grouping, ad copy, or landing.
3. Separate obvious negatives from SAFE UNKNOWN.

## Decision Cues

- Add negative keyword candidate.
- Split ad group.
- Tighten match type or query expansion.
- Review landing/ad mismatch.
- Hold uncertain terms for human platform review.

## Stop Rule

Stop when the top waste pattern is visible. Do not classify every low-volume edge term in live review.
