# ORCA Essential Signals Layer v1

## Purpose

Essential Signals helps a human operator prioritize the few PPC observations most likely to matter.

It reduces noise, observation inflation, and false complexity during SERP, trust, semantic, landing, and offer review.

## What This Layer Is

- High-value observation discipline.
- PPC signal prioritization support.
- Noise filtering.
- Commercial signal identification.
- Evidence-first review compression.

## What This Layer Is Not

- Not artificial intelligence architecture.
- Not automation, telemetry, dashboards, or orchestration.
- Not a signal-scoring engine.
- Not a replacement for human judgment.
- Not a proof of performance.

## Essential Signal Types

- dominant CTA patterns;
- dominant trust mechanics;
- local pack dominance;
- aggregator pressure;
- pricing transparency patterns;
- urgent-intent mechanics;
- strong semantic contamination;
- obvious landing mismatch;
- major mobile friction;
- high commercial clarity.

## Required Discipline

- Not all observations are equally valuable.
- Signal quality matters more than volume.
- Evidence quality matters.
- Operator attention is limited.
- Noise creates false complexity.
- Weak signals should not dominate review time.
