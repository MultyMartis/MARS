# High-Value Landing Signals v1

## Status

Manual landing signal guide. Not UX scoring, conversion prediction, telemetry, or automated page analysis.

## High-Value Landing Signals

- obvious landing mismatch;
- major mobile friction;
- dominant CTA clarity or confusion;
- contact path friction;
- missing trust proof for the ad promise;
- pricing or offer opacity;
- urgent-intent support or failure;
- local relevance support or failure.

## Evidence Standard

A landing signal is high-value only when it can affect PPC alignment, user action, or risk of wasted spend.

## Low-Value Landing Noise

- cosmetic preferences;
- full redesign ideas;
- tiny copy edits;
- non-actionable UX opinions;
- page details unrelated to PPC intent.

## Operator Action

Capture the mismatch or friction, evidence, likely PPC impact, and the smallest useful fix.

## Boundary

This guide rejects giant landing audits, unsupported conversion certainty, and theoretical perfectionism.
