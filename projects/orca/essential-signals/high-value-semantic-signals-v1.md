# High-Value Semantic Signals v1

## Status

Manual semantic signal guide. Not NLP architecture, AI classification, automation, or semantic intelligence.

## High-Value Semantic Signals

- strong semantic contamination;
- mixed commercial and informational intent;
- service terms that trigger unrelated meanings;
- job, DIY, research, definition, or tutorial leakage;
- local intent ambiguity;
- urgent-intent mismatch;
- landing offer mismatch against query meaning.

## Evidence Standard

A semantic signal is high-value only when visible evidence suggests the term may attract wrong-intent PPC traffic or weaken landing relevance.

## Low-Value Semantic Noise

- tiny wording nuances;
- theoretical keyword taxonomies;
- weak unsupported assumptions;
- long lists of low-impact variants;
- semantic labels with no campaign action.

## Operator Action

Record the contamination type, evidence, likely PPC consequence, and recommended action or SAFE UNKNOWN.

## Boundary

This guide rejects abstraction overload, pseudo-intelligence narratives, and semantic over-documentation.
