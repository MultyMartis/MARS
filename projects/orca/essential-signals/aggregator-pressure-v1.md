# Aggregator Pressure v1

## Use When

Use when directories, marketplaces, classifieds, maps, or lead aggregators dominate the SERP.

## Check

- How much of the first mobile screen is aggregator-controlled?
- Do aggregators show prices, ratings, availability, or many options?
- Do single businesses still appear credible?
- Are ads competing against "compare many providers" intent?
- Does the query imply marketplace behavior rather than direct service selection?

## Pressure Levels

- Low: mostly direct providers, aggregators are secondary.
- Medium: aggregators visible, but direct providers can still compete with proof.
- High: aggregators dominate first screen or set price/trust expectations.

## Decision Cues

- Strong aggregator pressure may require sharper local proof, price clarity, or niche semantic focus.
- Avoid broad claims if aggregators offer better comparison value.
- Review landing mismatch if the page lacks proof that counters aggregator convenience.

## Stop Rule

Record the pressure level and one PPC implication. Do not build a market model.
