# Signal Priority Rules v1

## Status

Manual prioritization rules. Not automated scoring, AI ranking, telemetry, or orchestration.

## Prioritize First

- dominant CTA patterns;
- dominant trust mechanics;
- local pack dominance;
- aggregator pressure;
- pricing transparency patterns;
- urgent-intent mechanics;
- strong semantic contamination;
- obvious landing mismatch;
- major mobile friction;
- high commercial clarity.

## Priority Test

A signal deserves review time only if:

- evidence is visible;
- PPC relevance is clear;
- action could change;
- impact is more than cosmetic;
- uncertainty is recorded honestly.

## Deprioritize

- weak unsupported assumptions;
- low-impact differences;
- endless micro-details;
- observations collected only for volume;
- unsupported claims that a signal is important.

## Boundary

Signal priority is an operator aid, not a scoring engine. It rejects observation inflation and abstraction overload.
