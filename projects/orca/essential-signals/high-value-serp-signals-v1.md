# High-Value SERP Signals v1

## Status

Manual SERP signal guide. Not scraping, monitoring, ranking intelligence, or telemetry.

## High-Value SERP Signals

- local pack dominance;
- aggregator pressure;
- repeated urgent-intent mechanics;
- dominant CTA patterns in ads or organic results;
- pricing transparency patterns;
- visible trust mechanics;
- strong semantic contamination;
- commercial clarity or confusion in visible results.

## Evidence Standard

A SERP signal is high-value only when it is visible, repeated or dominant, and likely to affect PPC positioning.

## Low-Value SERP Noise

- over-documented SERP clutter;
- isolated wording differences;
- speculative competitor strategy;
- non-actionable layout observations;
- screenshots kept only for completeness.

## Operator Action

Capture the dominant pressure, the evidence, the PPC implication, and the next action. Stop there unless more evidence is needed.

## Boundary

This guide rejects fake completeness, observation inflation, and unsupported SERP importance.
