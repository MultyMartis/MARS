# Emergency Service Patterns v1

## Purpose

Documents PPC heuristics for emergency-style local services. These patterns are revisable and must be validated by real operational capability.

## Heuristic

Emergency-service demand often compresses decision-making around speed, direct contact, local availability, and trust under time pressure.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when repeated observations show:

- emergency or urgent modifiers;
- high visibility of call CTAs;
- 24/7, same-day, or fast-arrival claims;
- local pack contact prominence;
- landing pages with dispatch, technician, or operator language.

## Operational Guidance

- Separate emergency clusters from planned-service clusters.
- Use short CTAs that match real operations.
- Prioritize phone and WhatsApp paths when evidence supports them.
- Keep pricing claims clear when pricing visibility is a market expectation.
- Add local proof such as districts, photos, reviews, licenses, or service-area details when true.

## Risks

- Overstating urgency can create compliance and trust risk.
- False 24/7 or fast-arrival claims can damage strategy.
- High urgency does not prove high lead quality.
- Aggregators may intercept emergency intent with comparison flows.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- actual dispatch capacity;
- true after-hours availability;
- lead quality;
- competitor operational truth;
- seasonal surge effects.

## Boundary

This heuristic guides human PPC planning. It is not autonomous strategy generation, automatic optimization, bidding automation, or campaign execution.
