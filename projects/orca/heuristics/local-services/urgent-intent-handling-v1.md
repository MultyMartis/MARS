# Urgent Intent Handling v1

## Purpose

Documents a local-service heuristic for isolating urgent commercial intent in PPC strategy. This heuristic is contextual and requires human interpretation.

## Heuristic

Urgent-intent queries often need separate campaign, ad, and landing handling because user expectations may emphasize speed, direct contact, availability, and low-friction response.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when repeated observations show:

- urgent modifiers in queries;
- same-day, 24/7, emergency, or fast-dispatch claims in SERPs;
- call-first or messenger-first CTAs;
- local pack or map pressure;
- landing pages built around immediate response.

## Operational Guidance

- Keep urgent clusters separate from research or price-comparison clusters.
- Use dispatch-style CTA language only when operationally true.
- Favor direct phone or WhatsApp paths when evidence supports urgent behavior.
- Avoid long forms as the primary path for emergency-like intent.
- Preserve city, district, and device boundaries.

## Contradiction Sensitivity

Contradictions are high impact when:

- urgent queries show informational results;
- desktop and mobile urgency signals differ;
- competitors use urgency claims without local proof;
- season or time of day changes behavior.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- actual response speed;
- real availability;
- conversion rate impact;
- CPC impact;
- stability across regions.

## Boundary

This heuristic informs human PPC strategy. It does not automate campaign segmentation, bidding, ad publishing, or optimization decisions.
