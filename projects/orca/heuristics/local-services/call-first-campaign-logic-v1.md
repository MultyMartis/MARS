# Call-First Campaign Logic v1

## Purpose

Documents a local-service heuristic for call-first PPC behavior. Call-first logic is evidence-sensitive and not a universal campaign rule.

## Heuristic

In some local-service markets, visible SERP and landing patterns suggest that immediate calls may be a stronger primary action than form completion, especially on mobile and urgent-intent queries.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when repeated evidence shows:

- phone numbers or call buttons are prominent;
- mobile SERPs emphasize tap-to-call;
- competitors place phone CTAs above forms;
- local pack contact buttons are visible;
- landing pages promise fast callback or dispatch.

## Operational Guidance

- Consider call-first ad and landing paths for urgent or high-intent clusters.
- Keep form-first logic for non-urgent comparison or planned-service queries when supported.
- Validate phone visibility on mobile and desktop separately.
- Match call promises to real operations before recommendation.
- Preserve evidence freshness because CTA patterns can change quickly.

## Contradiction Sensitivity

Reduce confidence when:

- form-heavy competitors dominate;
- high-value B2B queries require qualification forms;
- mobile and desktop behavior diverge;
- call tracking or operations capacity is unknown.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- actual call quality;
- operational answer rate;
- conversion rate;
- lead value;
- competitor call routing.

## Boundary

This heuristic supports human-reviewed campaign architecture. It is not an automatic call campaign generator, bidding system, or optimization engine.
