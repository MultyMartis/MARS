# Geo Segmentation Logic v1

## Purpose

Documents a local-service heuristic for geographic PPC segmentation. Geo segmentation depends on region, service radius, local trust, and SERP behavior.

## Heuristic

Local-service campaigns may benefit from city, district, metro, or service-area segmentation when evidence shows different intent, competition, local pack behavior, or landing expectations by geography.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when observations show:

- service + city or district query patterns;
- local pack variation by geography;
- different aggregator pressure across regions;
- local proof requirements;
- competitors using district-specific offers;
- different CTA or urgency patterns by area.

## Operational Guidance

- Separate geo clusters when SERP or landing requirements differ.
- Use local proof only when it is true and supportable.
- Avoid broad regional generalization from one city snapshot.
- Review mobile and desktop differences where local pack behavior matters.
- Reassess when service radius, season, or competitor coverage changes.

## Contradiction Sensitivity

High sensitivity applies when:

- nearby districts show different SERPs;
- local pack changes by device;
- aggregator pressure is uneven;
- landing claims conflict with service-area reality.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- actual service coverage;
- true local demand volume;
- CPC by district;
- map personalization;
- stability outside reviewed regions.

## Boundary

This heuristic supports human-supervised segmentation recommendations. It does not automate geo targeting, bid modifiers, campaign publishing, or local ranking analysis.
