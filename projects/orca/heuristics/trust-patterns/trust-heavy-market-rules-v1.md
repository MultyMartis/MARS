# Trust-Heavy Market Rules v1

## Purpose

Documents heuristics for markets where visible trust signals appear strategically important. Trust-heavy behavior is contextual and evidence-sensitive.

## Heuristic

When SERPs and landing pages repeatedly emphasize reviews, guarantees, certifications, photos, cases, or local reputation, PPC strategy should treat trust architecture as a primary commercial variable.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when repeated evidence shows:

- review counts or ratings are prominent;
- trust claims appear in ads or snippets;
- competitors use guarantees, licenses, certificates, or cases;
- local pack trust markers dominate;
- landing pages dedicate strong space to proof.

## Operational Guidance

- Review ad-to-landing trust consistency.
- Include trust proof only when real and supportable.
- Avoid generic claims when competitors use specific proof.
- Consider separate landing blocks for reviews, photos, guarantees, and local coverage.
- Preserve region and niche limits because trust expectations vary.

## Contradiction Sensitivity

Reduce confidence when:

- price-first competitors dominate;
- trust signals are visible but not repeated;
- aggregator trust framing displaces local provider proof;
- mobile screens compress trust evidence below the fold.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- actual trust impact on conversion;
- review authenticity;
- competitor proof validity;
- user preference;
- stability over time.

## Boundary

This heuristic supports human-reviewed trust strategy. It does not automate creative generation, landing optimization, or market intelligence.
