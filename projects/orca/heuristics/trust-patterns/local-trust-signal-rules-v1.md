# Local Trust Signal Rules v1

## Purpose

Documents heuristics for local trust signals in PPC strategy. Local trust is regional, evidence-sensitive, and not automatically transferable.

## Heuristic

In local-service markets, visible local proof can reduce uncertainty when users need a nearby, reachable, or accountable provider.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when observations show:

- city, district, or service-area proof;
- local reviews or map ratings;
- local photos, vehicles, teams, offices, or technicians;
- regional phone numbers or messenger channels;
- local pack trust markers;
- competitors emphasizing nearby availability.

## Operational Guidance

- Match local claims to real service coverage.
- Use district or city proof when validated.
- Avoid fake local presence.
- Keep local proof visible on mobile when local pack pressure is strong.
- Validate local trust needs separately by region.

## Contradiction Sensitivity

High sensitivity applies when:

- landing claims exceed actual coverage;
- SERP suggests aggregator or map dominance;
- local pack differs from landing trust claims;
- regional evidence is reused without review.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- actual local reputation;
- review quality;
- map personalization;
- service-area accuracy;
- local conversion impact.

## Boundary

This heuristic informs human PPC strategy. It is not local SEO automation, map monitoring, scraping, or autonomous market analysis.
