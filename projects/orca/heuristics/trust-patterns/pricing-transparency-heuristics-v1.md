# Pricing Transparency Heuristics v1

## Purpose

Documents heuristics for pricing visibility in commercial PPC strategy. Pricing expectations vary by niche, region, urgency, and competitor behavior.

## Heuristic

When SERPs and landing pages repeatedly show price-from claims, estimates, tariffs, or comparison framing, pricing transparency may become a strategic trust and qualification variable.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when repeated evidence shows:

- price claims in ads or snippets;
- pricing blocks on competitor landings;
- aggregator price comparison framing;
- search queries with price modifiers;
- user intent likely includes budgeting or quote comparison.

## Operational Guidance

- Separate price-comparison clusters from urgent-service clusters when evidence supports different behavior.
- Use pricing only when accurate and supportable.
- Prefer bounded price ranges or estimate framing when exact pricing is variable.
- Watch for low-price positioning that may create trust or margin risk.
- Compare mobile visibility of price claims with desktop visibility.

## Contradiction Sensitivity

Reduce confidence when:

- urgent queries show little pricing visibility;
- competitors hide prices but emphasize speed or trust;
- aggregator prices distort market perception;
- service complexity makes price claims misleading.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- actual CPC impact;
- user price sensitivity;
- competitor pricing truth;
- margin fit;
- conversion quality impact.

## Boundary

This heuristic supports human-reviewed pricing strategy. It is not automated pricing, bidding, campaign optimization, or market intelligence.
