# WhatsApp CTA Heuristics v1

## Purpose

Documents heuristics for WhatsApp and messenger CTA use in PPC strategy. Messenger behavior is niche, region, and device dependent.

## Heuristic

When local SERPs, local pack entries, and competitor landings repeatedly expose WhatsApp or messenger paths, messenger CTAs may be important for low-friction quote requests and fast response expectations.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when observations show:

- WhatsApp visibility in local pack or landing pages;
- messenger CTAs above the fold;
- quote-request language;
- mobile-heavy interaction patterns;
- fast-response promises tied to chat.

## Operational Guidance

- Treat WhatsApp as a conversion path only when operations can respond.
- Use messenger CTAs for quote requests when evidence supports it.
- Keep phone paths available for urgent intent.
- Validate mobile placement because messenger CTAs may be hidden on desktop.
- Avoid implying instant response unless it is operationally true.

## Contradiction Sensitivity

Reduce confidence when:

- phone-first behavior dominates;
- WhatsApp appears on landings but not SERPs;
- B2B or high-value queries require form qualification;
- regional messenger usage is unvalidated.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- actual response rate;
- lead quality;
- user preference;
- operations capacity;
- regional messenger adoption.

## Boundary

This heuristic guides human CTA review. It is not chat automation, lead routing automation, telemetry, or campaign optimization.
