# Local Mobile Conversion Patterns v1

## Purpose

Documents heuristics for local mobile conversion patterns. These patterns are strategic guidance for human review, not proof of conversion performance.

## Heuristic

Local mobile conversion paths often need compressed trust, clear availability, direct contact, and minimal friction because screen space and user patience are limited.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when observations show:

- local pack dominance;
- direct phone or messenger CTAs;
- short forms or no forms;
- visible local proof;
- response-time promises;
- pricing or estimate cues above the fold.

## Operational Guidance

- Keep primary CTA visible without deep scrolling.
- Compress trust into reviews, local proof, photos, guarantees, or service-area markers.
- Reduce form length for urgent or simple quote paths.
- Preserve phone and messenger options where evidence supports them.
- Validate region and niche before reusing the pattern.

## Risks

- Too little qualification can lower lead quality.
- Over-compressed trust can become generic.
- False fast-response promises create trust risk.
- Mobile behavior can change with SERP layout and device context.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- actual scroll depth;
- true form tolerance;
- conversion quality;
- response-time truth;
- regional behavior outside reviewed area.

## Boundary

This heuristic informs human mobile conversion review. It is not automated landing optimization, telemetry, browser automation, or autonomous strategy generation.
