# Mobile Call Behavior Rules v1

## Purpose

Documents heuristics for mobile call behavior in local commercial PPC. Call behavior must be validated by evidence and operations context.

## Heuristic

For some local-service and urgent-intent queries, mobile users may favor immediate calls when phone CTAs are prominent, response expectations are high, and forms create friction.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when repeated observations show:

- tap-to-call buttons in ads, local pack, or landing pages;
- phone numbers above the fold;
- urgent or same-day language;
- short decision cycles;
- competitors reducing form friction.

## Operational Guidance

- Keep call paths visible for mobile urgent clusters.
- Do not remove form paths when users may need quotes or documentation.
- Match call promises to real answer capacity.
- Review call-first and WhatsApp-first behavior separately.
- Reassess when mobile SERP layout changes.

## Contradiction Sensitivity

Reduce confidence when:

- users appear to need comparison or documentation;
- B2B intent requires qualification;
- forms dominate competitor landing pages;
- phone CTAs are visible but not repeated across queries.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- answer rate;
- call quality;
- conversion rate;
- user preference;
- operational capacity.

## Boundary

This heuristic supports human strategy review. It is not call tracking automation, bidding automation, lead routing, telemetry, or optimization.
