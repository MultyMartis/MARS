# ORCA Strategic Heuristics

## Purpose

Defines the ORCA Strategic Heuristics Layer v1 for reusable human-supervised PPC strategic guidance derived from repeated operational observations and evidence-backed pilot findings.

Heuristics are disciplined strategic prompts for human interpretation. They are not universal truth, autonomous strategy generation, campaign decisions, optimization engines, self-learning AI, runtime orchestration, automated market intelligence, or bidding systems.

## Scope

Use this layer for:

- reusable PPC strategic guidance;
- local-service strategic observations;
- trust-oriented commercial logic;
- mobile-first PPC logic;
- aggregator-pressure handling;
- urgency-intent handling;
- campaign segmentation recommendations.

## Required Discipline

- Heuristics are revisable.
- Heuristics depend on region, niche, time, device, and evidence freshness.
- Heuristics may conflict.
- Contradictions must remain visible.
- Human interpretation is required before use.
- Confidence may degrade over time.
- ORCA does not autonomously make strategy decisions.

## Directory Guide

- `strategic-heuristics-model-v1.md` - reusable heuristic record model.
- `heuristic-evidence-requirements-v1.md` - evidence thresholds and penalties.
- `heuristic-validation-rules-v1.md` - human review and validation discipline.
- `heuristic-confidence-rules-v1.md` - confidence handling.
- `heuristic-expiration-rules-v1.md` - evidence aging and review triggers.
- `local-services/` - urgent intent, call-first, geo, and emergency service heuristics.
- `trust-patterns/` - trust, pricing, local proof, and WhatsApp CTA heuristics.
- `aggregator-pressure/` - aggregator defense and high-pressure market heuristics.
- `mobile-commercial/` - mobile-first commercial behavior heuristics.

## Boundary

This layer documents human-supervised strategic intelligence. It does not automate campaign publishing, bidding, targeting, optimization, telemetry, scraping, dashboards, or runtime decision-making.
