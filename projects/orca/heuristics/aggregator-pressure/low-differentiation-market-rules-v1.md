# Low Differentiation Market Rules v1

## Purpose

Documents heuristics for markets where visible offers are similar and differentiation is weak. Low differentiation is a strategic observation, not a proof of market sameness.

## Heuristic

When competitors repeatedly use similar CTAs, pricing claims, trust claims, and landing structures, PPC strategy may need sharper segmentation, proof, or offer specificity to avoid commodity positioning.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when observations show:

- repeated generic CTAs;
- similar price-from claims;
- similar guarantees or trust markers;
- aggregator comparison framing;
- weak local proof differences;
- landing pages with interchangeable offers.

## Operational Guidance

- Look for niche, geo, urgency, service-type, or trust differentiation.
- Avoid generic ad copy when SERP sameness is high.
- Use specific proof only when validated.
- Test narrow clusters before broad messaging recommendations.
- Preserve contradictions where some competitors show strong differentiation.

## Risks

- Over-differentiation can create unsupported claims.
- Price-only positioning can reduce trust or margin.
- Aggregators may make local providers look interchangeable.
- Mobile screen limits can hide deeper differentiation.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- real customer preference;
- true competitor quality;
- offer performance;
- differentiation impact;
- stability across regions.

## Boundary

This heuristic supports human strategic review. It is not autonomous positioning, creative generation, optimization, scraping, or market intelligence automation.
