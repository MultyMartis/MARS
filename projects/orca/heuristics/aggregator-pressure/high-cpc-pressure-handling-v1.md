# High CPC Pressure Handling v1

## Purpose

Documents heuristics for suspected high-pressure PPC markets. CPC pressure must be treated carefully because ORCA does not infer actual CPC from SERP visibility alone.

## Heuristic

When SERPs show dense ad blocks, aggregator competition, broad commercial overlap, and low differentiation, human reviewers may consider conservative launch scope and tighter campaign segmentation.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when repeated observations show:

- heavy ad density;
- repeated aggregator visibility;
- many similar offers;
- strong commercial intent;
- visible trust or price competition;
- prior pilot evidence of expensive or contested clusters.

## Operational Guidance

- Start with narrow, high-intent clusters when evidence supports pressure.
- Separate urgent, geo, price, and comparison intent.
- Avoid assuming CPC values without platform evidence.
- Use differentiation and landing match review before broad launch.
- Preserve confidence limits when CPC data is unavailable.

## Contradiction Sensitivity

Reduce confidence when:

- ad density is inconsistent;
- organic and local results dominate instead of ads;
- pressure appears only on one device;
- region or season differs from supporting evidence.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- actual CPC;
- auction pressure;
- competitor budgets;
- conversion rate;
- lead quality.

## Boundary

This heuristic supports human launch planning. It is not an automatic bidding system, optimization engine, telemetry model, or campaign control system.
