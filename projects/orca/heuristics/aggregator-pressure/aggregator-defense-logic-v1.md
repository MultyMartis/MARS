# Aggregator Defense Logic v1

## Purpose

Documents heuristics for PPC strategy when aggregators, directories, marketplaces, or lead platforms visibly pressure a SERP. Aggregator defense is contextual and evidence-sensitive.

## Heuristic

When aggregators dominate ads, organic results, local comparison paths, or trust framing, local providers may need narrower clusters, stronger differentiation, and clearer direct-response value.

## Evidence Basis

Use only when repeated observations show:

- aggregator SERP dominance;
- directory or marketplace visibility;
- review/rating comparison framing;
- price comparison framing;
- lead form interception;
- local provider displacement.

## Operational Guidance

- Consider narrow-cluster launches where direct provider relevance is strongest.
- Emphasize local proof, speed, specialization, or direct contact when supportable.
- Avoid broad generic clusters if aggregators compress visibility.
- Compare mobile and desktop pressure separately.
- Treat aggregator pressure as visibility evidence, not proof of conversion share.

## Risks

- Aggregator trust can displace local provider trust.
- Lead interception can shape user expectations.
- Direct provider ads may need stronger differentiation.
- Broad campaigns may waste budget in comparison-heavy intent.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

- true click share;
- aggregator lead quality;
- actual CPC impact;
- user preference;
- stability across query sets.

## Boundary

This heuristic informs human PPC strategy. It is not autonomous competitor strategy, scraping, telemetry, bidding automation, or optimization logic.
