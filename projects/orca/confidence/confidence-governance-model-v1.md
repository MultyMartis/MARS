# Confidence Governance Model v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA assigns, increases, decreases, and limits confidence in commercial intelligence. Confidence is evidence-backed caution, not certainty.

ORCA observes reality. ORCA does not define reality. All confidence remains human-supervised, contextual, revisable, and subject to decay.

## Confidence Levels

## LOW

Use LOW when evidence is thin, isolated, stale, unstable, contradicted, or poorly contextualized.

Allowed use: research lead or warning only.

## MEDIUM

Use MEDIUM when evidence repeats in a limited context and key context is known, but volatility, contradiction, or regional uncertainty remains.

Allowed use: human-reviewed hypothesis and QA focus.

## HIGH

Use HIGH when evidence is repeated, current, traceable, and supported across comparable observations with bounded contradictions.

Allowed use: reusable intelligence candidate within explicit context limits.

## VERY HIGH

Use VERY HIGH only when evidence is repeatedly validated across time, cases, sources, and relevant regions or when boundaries are clearly documented.

Allowed use: strong human-supervised planning input. It still does not become an automatic rule.

## Confidence Increases

Confidence may increase when:

- observations repeat across comparable query sets;
- multiple cases confirm the pattern;
- multiple regions confirm the pattern where regional transfer is justified;
- SERP conditions are stable across snapshots;
- contradictions are resolved or bounded;
- evidence is fresh;
- human reviewers validate reuse.

## Confidence Decreases

Confidence must decrease when:

- contradictory evidence appears;
- SERPs become unstable;
- evidence ages or decays;
- region, niche, or device context is missing;
- source reliability weakens;
- market volatility increases;
- seasonal distortion is unmarked;
- pattern reuse exceeds observed evidence.

## Boundary

Confidence governance is a research control. It is not automated scoring infrastructure, telemetry, market prediction, or autonomous campaign optimization.
