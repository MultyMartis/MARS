# Trust Signal Intelligence v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA pilot cases accumulate reusable intelligence about trust signals in PPC research. Trust signal intelligence helps operators understand visible proof expectations without claiming conversion causality or automatic strategy.

## Trust Signal Types

- review count;
- rating score;
- testimonials;
- guarantees;
- licenses;
- certifications;
- local address;
- service-area proof;
- before-after proof;
- portfolio or cases;
- team or company proof;
- payment and refund clarity;
- complaint or risk cues.

## Trust Sensitivity Levels

## LOW

Few competitors show trust proof, and SERP messaging is driven by service or price.

## MEDIUM

Trust proof appears in some visible results or landing pages, but it is not dominant.

## HIGH

Reviews, guarantees, proof, or local credibility appear repeatedly across competitors.

## VERY HIGH

Trust proof appears central to visible competition, especially in high-risk, urgent, expensive, or local service niches.

## Required Context

- niche;
- region;
- device;
- query set;
- SERP date;
- landing references;
- observed trust signals;
- confidence level;
- SAFE UNKNOWN.

## Interpretation Rules

- Trust signals are contextual and region-sensitive.
- Review visibility does not prove conversion performance.
- Competitor trust proof does not automatically define required page changes.
- Sensitive niches may require policy and compliance review.
- Contradictory evidence should lower confidence or split the pattern.

## PPC Research Implications

Trust signal intelligence may guide human review of:

- landing proof completeness;
- ad claim risk;
- offer credibility;
- local provider differentiation;
- review visibility;
- QA focus.

It must not automatically rewrite ads, change landing pages, or optimize campaigns.

## SAFE UNKNOWN Examples

- actual trust impact on conversion;
- authenticity of reviews;
- compliance status of claims;
- unseen landing proof;
- regional trust expectations outside observed queries;
- seasonal or event-driven trust pressure.

## Boundary

This intelligence layer accumulates research patterns only. It is not autonomous CRO, reputation management, ad optimization, or campaign control.
