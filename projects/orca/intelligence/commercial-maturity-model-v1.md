# Commercial Maturity Model v1

## Purpose

Classifies how mature visible PPC competition appears in an ORCA pilot case. Commercial maturity is a human-reviewed research interpretation based on observed messaging, landing fit, trust signals, and campaign-like structure.

## Maturity Levels

## BASIC

Visible market signals are weak or inconsistent.

- generic messages;
- limited trust proof;
- unclear landing fit;
- weak local or service specificity;
- few visible competitors.

## DEVELOPING

Some competitors show structured commercial behavior.

- clearer offers;
- partial local relevance;
- visible CTAs;
- some review or trust signals;
- inconsistent semantic-to-landing fit.

## MATURE

Most visible competitors show disciplined commercial presentation.

- specific offers;
- strong landing fit;
- repeated trust signals;
- local or service-specific pages;
- clear CTAs;
- differentiated positioning.

## ADVANCED

Visible competitors show high commercial discipline across multiple surfaces.

- consistent ad-to-landing alignment;
- strong trust proof;
- segmentation by service or region;
- mature urgency, price, and guarantee framing;
- visible aggregator or brand pressure;
- low tolerance for generic PPC structure.

## Evidence Inputs

- SERP observations;
- landing observations;
- CTA observations;
- trust observations;
- offer observations;
- aggregator observations;
- semantic fit notes;
- QA findings.

## Interpretation Rules

- Maturity is contextual to region, niche, and observed query set.
- Do not infer actual campaign performance.
- Do not infer account sophistication from one ad.
- Do not assume mature visible messaging means profitable advertising.
- Lower confidence when observations are stale, volatile, or contradictory.

## Reuse Rules

Commercial maturity findings can inform:

- human review priorities;
- campaign architecture caution;
- landing QA focus;
- trust proof requirements;
- offer differentiation research.

They must not automatically trigger campaign generation, budget changes, bidding strategy, or client positioning claims.

## SAFE UNKNOWN Examples

- hidden account structure;
- actual conversion rate;
- real CPC;
- profitability;
- historical market behavior;
- platform quality score.
