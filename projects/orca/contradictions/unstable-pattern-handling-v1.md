# Unstable Pattern Handling v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA handles commercial patterns that appear inconsistent, volatile, or weakly repeated. Unstable patterns must not be promoted into rules.

ORCA observes reality. ORCA does not define reality. Unstable patterns remain revisable and must carry confidence limits.

## Unstable Pattern Signals

- inconsistent SERP layout across snapshots;
- aggregator visibility changes frequently;
- ad density changes without clear context;
- local pack appears inconsistently;
- CTA patterns vary by device or region;
- offer patterns appear only once;
- evidence conflicts across sources;
- market behavior changes during seasonal windows.

## Handling Rules

- Mark the pattern as unstable.
- Preserve all supporting and contradictory observations.
- Keep confidence LOW or MEDIUM until repeatability improves.
- Identify whether instability is caused by SERP volatility, seasonality, region, niche, device, source quality, or evidence age.
- Do not reuse unstable patterns outside the observed context.
- Require fresh validation before strategic use.
- Downgrade stale unstable patterns faster than stable patterns.

## Allowed Uses

- research backlog;
- QA warning;
- hypothesis for new observation;
- context note for human review.

## Forbidden Uses

- campaign rule;
- broad market conclusion;
- cross-region assumption;
- high-confidence commercial recommendation;
- evidence of autonomous intelligence.

## Boundary

This document supports disciplined human interpretation. It is not instability detection software, telemetry, scraping, browser automation, or automated strategy.
