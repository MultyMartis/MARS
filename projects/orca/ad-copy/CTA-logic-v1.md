# CTA Logic v1

## Purpose

Support practical CTA selection for local-service search ads.

CTA should match user intent, device behavior, and real business response capacity.

## CTA Types

- call-first;
- WhatsApp-first;
- request estimate;
- request callback;
- book service;
- check availability;
- get consultation.

## Call-First Logic

Use when:

- intent is urgent;
- mobile behavior is strong;
- phone answering is reliable;
- price or dispatch depends on details;
- SERP competitors emphasize calls.

## WhatsApp-First Logic

Use when:

- users often send photos or details;
- operator response via messenger is real;
- the service needs clarification;
- call friction may be high;
- landing supports messenger action.

## Fast-Response Expectations

Only use fast-response messaging when the business can support it.

Avoid:

- fake "instant" claims;
- unsupported response-time promises;
- urgency that operations cannot meet;
- pressure language without service value.

## Estimate Requests

Use estimate CTAs when:

- price depends on distance, equipment, time, or volume;
- transparent fixed pricing is not realistic;
- user details are needed before quote.

## Boundary

ORCA does not configure calls, messengers, tracking, uploads, or campaign launch. CTA drafts require human review.
