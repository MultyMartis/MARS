# Ad Relevance Rules v1

## Purpose

Keep ad copy aligned with query, ad group, service, geo, landing, and trust evidence.

## Relevance Areas

- query alignment;
- landing alignment;
- service alignment;
- geo alignment;
- intent alignment;
- trust alignment;
- CTA alignment.

## Query Alignment

The ad should answer the searched intent directly.

Avoid writing one generic ad for keywords with different service meanings, urgency levels, or geo logic.

## Landing Alignment

The landing should support:

- the service in the ad;
- the region in the ad;
- the CTA in the ad;
- the trust claim in the ad;
- the pricing claim in the ad;
- the urgency claim in the ad.

## Intent Alignment

Urgent, planned, competitor, branded, and informational-adjacent queries should not receive the same message by default.

Use separate drafts only when the difference changes user expectations or PPC control.

## Trust Alignment

Trust claims should match real proof.

If the landing cannot support the claim, remove it or mark `SAFE_UNKNOWN`.

## Boundary

ORCA does not autonomously publish relevant ads. Human review is mandatory before any campaign use.
