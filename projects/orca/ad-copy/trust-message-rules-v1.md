# Trust Message Rules v1

## Purpose

Guide trust messaging for search ads without hype or unsupported claims.

Trust should be specific, useful, and supported by real evidence.

## Priorities

- clarity;
- proof;
- local trust;
- response expectations;
- equipment visibility;
- experience signals;
- dispatch trust;
- operational transparency.

## Useful Trust Signals

- local service area;
- visible phone support;
- verified working hours;
- equipment photos or fleet proof;
- experience if supported;
- review count if current and accurate;
- documents or contracts where relevant;
- clear dispatch or arrival process;
- transparent quote logic.

## Weak Trust Signals

- "best";
- "number one";
- "guaranteed result";
- vague "professionals";
- unsupported "fastest";
- fake scarcity;
- claims not visible on landing.

## Trust Message Rule

A trust claim must be supportable by the landing page, business evidence, or verified operator knowledge.

If support is missing, mark the claim as `SAFE_UNKNOWN` or remove it.

## Boundary

ORCA does not validate business truth automatically. Human review is mandatory before any trust claim is used.
