# Moderation Risk Notes v1

## Purpose

Help operators notice copy risks before human platform review.

This is not legal advice, policy automation, or a guarantee of Yandex Direct moderation outcomes.

## Common Risk Areas

- unsupported guarantees;
- unverifiable superiority claims;
- misleading pricing;
- fake urgency;
- restricted service claims;
- claims not supported by landing;
- inaccurate geo or availability claims;
- aggressive pressure tactics;
- brand or competitor naming issues;
- inconsistent business details.

## Review Questions

- Can the business prove the claim?
- Does the landing support the claim?
- Is the price current and complete enough?
- Is availability real?
- Is the CTA operationally supported?
- Is the geo claim accurate?
- Could the wording mislead a user?
- Does current platform policy allow this wording?

## Safer Handling

- remove unsupported claims;
- replace hype with service clarity;
- use "check availability" when availability is uncertain;
- use quote language when final price varies;
- mark uncertain claims as `SAFE_UNKNOWN`;
- ask for human policy review when risk is unclear.

## Boundary

ORCA does not predict moderation, guarantee compliance, or replace human review.
