# Local Service Copy Rules v1

## Purpose

Support commercially realistic ad copy for local-service PPC campaigns.

This guidance is search-first, local-service oriented, and manually usable.

## Local-Service Contexts

- emergency services;
- moving and cargo services;
- equipment rental or service;
- repair and installation;
- dispatch-based services;
- local professional services.

## Copy Priorities

- name the service clearly;
- name the region when relevant;
- match urgency level;
- make the CTA obvious;
- show practical trust;
- avoid unsupported claims;
- match the landing page.

## Dispatch Logic

For dispatch-style services, review:

- service area;
- availability;
- response promise;
- phone or messenger path;
- equipment or team readiness;
- estimate process;
- local proof.

## Commercial Practicality

Prefer copy that helps the user decide:

- can they do this service?
- do they serve my area?
- can I contact them quickly?
- what information do I need to provide?
- why should I trust them enough to call?

## Boundary

ORCA does not autonomously generate production ads. Human review is mandatory, and ads remain revisable.
