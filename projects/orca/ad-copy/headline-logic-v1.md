# Headline Logic v1

## Purpose

Guide practical headline drafting for Yandex Direct search ads.

Headlines should make the service, region, and intent obvious without unsupported claims.

## Headline Inputs

Use evidence from:

- query intent;
- service scope;
- geo scope;
- urgency level;
- landing page;
- SERP competitor patterns;
- verified trust signals.

## Useful Headline Angles

- service relevance;
- geo relevance;
- urgency;
- trust;
- equipment or service specificity;
- commercial intent alignment;
- call-first or request-first action.

## Local-Service Examples Of Logic

- service + city;
- urgent service + city;
- equipment capacity + service;
- service + call CTA;
- service + verified trust proof.

## Avoid

- fake guarantees;
- unsupported superiority claims;
- clickbait;
- unclear abbreviations;
- hype instead of service clarity;
- claims not supported by landing content;
- urgency when the business cannot respond quickly.

## Review Questions

- Does the headline match the query?
- Does it match the ad group?
- Does it match the landing?
- Is the geo claim true?
- Is the trust claim supported?
- Is it readable on mobile?

## Boundary

ORCA does not autonomously generate production headlines. Human review and moderation awareness are mandatory.
