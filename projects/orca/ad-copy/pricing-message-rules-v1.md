# Pricing Message Rules v1

## Purpose

Guide pricing messages in search ads without fake cheapness or misleading claims.

## When Pricing Helps

Pricing can help when:

- the price is real and current;
- the landing supports the claim;
- the service has common price comparison behavior;
- transparency improves trust;
- the quote basis is clear.

## When Pricing Hurts

Pricing can hurt when:

- the final cost depends heavily on details;
- "cheap" positioning attracts poor-fit leads;
- the price is outdated or incomplete;
- competitors use misleading low anchors;
- the business cannot support the advertised price.

## Transparency Tradeoffs

Use clear wording when price depends on variables:

- from price, if real and supported;
- estimate after details;
- price depends on distance or scope;
- call to calculate.

Avoid:

- fake low-price hooks;
- hidden conditions;
- unsupported discounts;
- "best price" without proof;
- claims that contradict the landing.

## Trust vs Price Positioning

In local services, trust may matter more than cheapest price.

Do not sacrifice service clarity, response confidence, or proof just to sound cheaper.

## Boundary

ORCA does not verify current pricing automatically. Human review is mandatory before price claims are used.
