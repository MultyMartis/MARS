# Ad Copy Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA may help prepare search ad copy drafts for human review.

ORCA does not autonomously generate production ads, launch campaigns, upload ads, manage bids, optimize performance, or claim that draft copy is platform-ready.

## Explicitly Rejected

- fake guarantees;
- unsupported claims;
- fake urgency;
- misleading pricing;
- manipulative pressure tactics;
- autonomous generation claims;
- bulk production ad generation;
- automatic launch;
- Direct API integration;
- runtime ad optimization.

## Required Human Review

Before any platform use, a human must check:

- query relevance;
- service accuracy;
- geo accuracy;
- landing alignment;
- CTA truthfulness;
- trust proof;
- price claim support;
- moderation risk;
- business capacity;
- current platform rules.

## Copy Discipline

- Say only what the business can support.
- Prefer clear service language over hype.
- Prefer proven trust signals over vague superiority.
- Do not create false scarcity or pressure.
- Mark uncertain claims as `SAFE_UNKNOWN`.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know actual moderation outcomes, current business availability, live response time, lead quality, conversion rate, legal constraints, or platform policy changes unless verified by a human.
