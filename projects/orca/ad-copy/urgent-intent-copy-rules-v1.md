# Urgent Intent Copy Rules v1

## Purpose

Guide urgent-intent ad copy without fake pressure or unsupported speed claims.

## Urgency Without Fake Pressure

Use urgency only when the query and service context support it.

Good urgency is practical:

- call now;
- check availability;
- urgent dispatch;
- same-day service if verified;
- fast estimate if verified.

Bad urgency is manipulative:

- fake scarcity;
- unsupported "instant arrival";
- panic framing;
- pressure without useful service information;
- promises the business cannot keep.

## Speed Messaging

Speed claims require evidence.

Review:

- actual response capacity;
- working hours;
- service area;
- dispatch model;
- operator availability;
- landing support for the claim.

## Availability Messaging

Only mention 24/7, today, now, or same-day availability when verified.

If availability is uncertain, use safer language:

- check availability;
- call to confirm time;
- request urgent service.

## Dispatch-Style Messaging

Useful when:

- user needs immediate help;
- location matters;
- equipment or team dispatch matters;
- phone contact is the fastest path.

## Boundary

ORCA does not verify live availability automatically and does not launch ads. Human review is mandatory.
