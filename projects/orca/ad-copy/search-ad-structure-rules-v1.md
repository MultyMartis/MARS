# Search Ad Structure Rules v1

## Purpose

Keep search ads clear, relevant, and commercially useful for manual PPC preparation.

This is not autonomous copy generation, bulk automation, upload tooling, or ad optimization.

## Core Structure

A practical search ad draft should cover:

- service;
- region;
- commercial intent;
- CTA;
- trust signal;
- landing match;
- urgency level;
- moderation risk.

## Priorities

- commercial clarity;
- intent relevance;
- local relevance;
- CTA clarity;
- trust clarity;
- mobile readability;
- honest urgency handling;
- semantic alignment.

## Good Search Ad Signals

- the user can identify the service quickly;
- the region is clear when region matters;
- the CTA matches the likely action;
- trust proof is specific enough to be credible;
- urgency does not sound fake;
- landing page can support the promise;
- wording fits the query group.

## Bad Search Ad Signals

- vague service language;
- unsupported "best" or "guaranteed" claims;
- fake urgency;
- misleading low-price framing;
- CTA that the business cannot handle;
- geo claim not supported by service coverage;
- ad copy that fits only some keywords in the ad group.

## Boundary

ORCA prepares draft guidance only. Human review is mandatory before any platform use.
