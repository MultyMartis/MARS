# Negative Keyword Strategy v1

## Purpose

Defines a practical negative keyword methodology for protecting Yandex.Direct and search PPC campaigns from irrelevant spend.

## Negative Sources

- Informational query leakage.
- Free, DIY, job, vacancy, education, forum, review-only, and template modifiers.
- Irrelevant services with shared roots.
- Wrong geography.
- Wrong audience: B2B vs B2C mismatch.
- Marketplace or aggregator intent when direct lead generation cannot compete.
- Brand or competitor terms requiring human policy review.

## Strategy Rules

- Build negatives per cluster, campaign, and account level separately.
- Avoid aggressive negatives before human review.
- Protect urgent and local service clusters from research traffic.
- Do not block comparison terms automatically; decide based on offer strength.
- Record reason and expected protection for each important negative.

## Yandex.Direct Notes

- Morphology and match behavior must be checked against the actual platform rules before use.
- Negative phrases can accidentally suppress useful variants.
- Human review is required before import into Direct Commander or account tools.

## QA Checks

- Does the negative block only unwanted traffic?
- Could it block a valuable local or urgent query?
- Is it needed at campaign level or only ad group level?
- Is the term based on evidence or guesswork?

## Boundary

This strategy creates reviewable negative keyword candidates. It does not upload negatives, edit accounts, automate optimization, or manage spend.
