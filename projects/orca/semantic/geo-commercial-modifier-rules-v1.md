# Geo Commercial Modifier Rules v1

## Purpose

Defines how ORCA handles geography and commercial modifiers in service-business PPC semantics.

## Geo Modifiers

- City names.
- Districts.
- Metro stations.
- Near-me wording.
- Regional abbreviations.
- Service area phrases.
- Delivery or visit zones.

## Commercial Modifiers

- price, cost, quote, calculate;
- order, book, request, call;
- urgent, emergency, today, 24/7;
- official, certified, licensed;
- nearby, with visit, at home, on site;
- for business, corporate, contract.

## Rules

- Keep geo purity when landing pages and service coverage differ.
- Separate urgent modifiers when operations can actually fulfill urgency.
- Separate price-sensitive terms if pricing transparency is weak.
- Do not mix city-level and national intent by default.
- Validate geo terms against SERP and real service area.

## Risks

- Geo mismatch creates wasted local traffic.
- Commercial modifiers can imply promises the business cannot support.
- Near-me intent may require strong map and mobile landing quality.
- B2B modifiers can contaminate B2C lead campaigns.

## Boundary

Modifier rules guide human-reviewed semantic design. They do not set targeting, bids, budgets, or live Yandex.Direct configuration automatically.
