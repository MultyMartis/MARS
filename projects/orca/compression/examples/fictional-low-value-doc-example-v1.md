# Fictional Low-Value Doc Example v1

## Status

Fictional demonstration only. Non-production. Non-validated.

## Situation

A fictional document describes a new "multi-dimensional PPC resonance layer" but gives no operator checklist, no evidence rule, and no decision action.

## Low-Value Signals

- theoretical-only layer;
- non-operational complexity;
- pseudo-intelligence narrative;
- no practical PPC action;
- maintenance cost without usability value.

## Compression Action

Remove the document or replace it with one sentence in an existing practical layer.

## Why

Practical usability matters. Fake sophistication is dangerous. Documentation for its own sake creates operational confusion.

## Boundary

This example is demonstration-only and rejects abstraction inflation, giant governance layering, and intelligence mythology.
