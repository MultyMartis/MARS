# Complexity Warning Signals v1

## Status

Manual complexity warning guide. Not telemetry, dashboards, automation, or governance scoring.

## Warning Signals

- duplicated methodology;
- repeated abstractions;
- low-signal documentation;
- theoretical-only layers;
- non-operational complexity;
- excessive review loops;
- operator fatigue amplification;
- fake sophistication language;
- pseudo-intelligence narratives.

## Practical Test

Complexity is harmful when it:

- slows PPC review;
- hides high-impact signals;
- increases maintenance cost;
- makes decisions harder;
- rewards completeness over usefulness.

## Required Compression Discipline

Smaller can be better. Practical usability matters. Operator attention is limited. Fake sophistication is dangerous.

## Boundary

This guide rejects abstraction inflation, giant governance layering, methodology addiction, and documentation for its own sake.
