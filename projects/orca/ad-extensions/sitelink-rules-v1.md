# Sitelink Rules v1

## Purpose

Support practical sitelink preparation for local-service search ads.

## Useful Sitelinks

- service detail pages;
- price or estimate page;
- service area page;
- urgent service page;
- equipment or fleet proof;
- reviews or trust proof;
- contact page.

## Relevance Rules

- match the campaign intent;
- support the ad promise;
- avoid duplicate messaging;
- keep labels readable on mobile;
- use landing pages that actually answer the sitelink label.

## Local-Service Use

Sitelinks should help users quickly find:

- service scope;
- local availability;
- estimate flow;
- trust proof;
- fast contact path.

## Risks

- misleading page labels;
- too many similar sitelinks;
- generic company pages instead of useful actions;
- unsupported urgency or trust claims.

## Boundary

ORCA does not create production sitelinks autonomously. Human review is mandatory.
