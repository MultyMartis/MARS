# Structured Snippet Rules v1

## Purpose

Support manual preparation of structured snippets for service clarity.

## Useful Snippet Themes

- services;
- equipment types;
- service areas;
- cargo or task types;
- response options;
- support channels.

## Rules

- use real service categories;
- keep snippets aligned with campaign intent;
- avoid listing services the business cannot provide;
- keep wording short;
- avoid stuffing unrelated options.

## Local-Service Fit

Structured snippets are useful when they clarify what the business actually handles, especially for dispatch-style, cargo, equipment, repair, or emergency services.

## Boundary

ORCA does not generate production snippets or verify business truth automatically. Human review is mandatory.
