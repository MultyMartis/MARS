# WhatsApp Extension Rules v1

## Purpose

Support WhatsApp-oriented messaging where messenger contact fits the service.

## Use WhatsApp Messaging When

- users need to send photos, location, or task details;
- operator response is real;
- estimate flow works through chat;
- landing supports WhatsApp clearly;
- WhatsApp does not hide a more important call path.

## Review Areas

- messenger availability;
- expected response time;
- what details users should send;
- fit with urgent intent;
- mobile readability;
- landing consistency.

## Risks

- fake availability;
- slow response behind fast-contact messaging;
- unclear whether call or WhatsApp is primary;
- unsupported claims about instant reply.

## Boundary

ORCA does not configure WhatsApp, tracking, uploads, or campaign launch. Human review is mandatory.
