# Extension Risk Signals v1

## Purpose

Identify practical extension risks before campaign assembly.

## Risk Signals

- duplicate messaging;
- fake urgency;
- unsupported guarantees;
- clutter;
- weak relevance;
- extension overload;
- mobile readability issues;
- fake trust signals;
- misleading service claims;
- contact claims operations cannot support.

## Overload Risk

Extension overload appears when extensions add more choices but no clearer action.

Watch for:

- too many similar sitelinks;
- competing phone, form, and messenger messages;
- callouts that repeat the ad;
- snippets that list unrelated services.

## Operator Rule

Use fewer stronger extensions when additional assets reduce clarity.

## Boundary

ORCA flags risks for human review. It does not generate or upload extensions autonomously.
