# Ad Extension Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA may help operators prepare and review ad extension drafts.

ORCA does not autonomously generate extensions, upload assets, launch campaigns, optimize ads, or prove extension performance.

## Explicitly Rejected

- autonomous extension generation;
- unsupported claims;
- fake trust signals;
- misleading service claims;
- fake urgency;
- automatic launch;
- Direct API integration;
- performance certainty.

## Human Review Requirements

A human must verify:

- service accuracy;
- local relevance;
- CTA relevance;
- landing support;
- trust proof;
- contact availability;
- mobile readability;
- current platform rules.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know moderation outcomes, extension eligibility, click behavior, conversion effect, live availability, or business capacity unless verified by a human.
