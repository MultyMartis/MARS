# Contact Extension Rules v1

## Purpose

Review contact-oriented extension logic for local-service campaigns.

## Use Contact Emphasis When

- calls are operationally supported;
- urgent intent is common;
- estimates require details;
- local response matters;
- mobile users need quick action.

## Review Areas

- phone accuracy;
- working hours;
- call handling capacity;
- landing contact match;
- response expectation;
- mobile click path;
- backup contact method.

## Risks

- phone not answered;
- unsupported 24/7 implication;
- CTA conflicts with landing;
- too many competing contact options;
- response promise exceeds operations.

## Boundary

ORCA does not configure calls, tracking, upload settings, or launch campaigns. Human review is mandatory.
