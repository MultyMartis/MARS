# Search Terms Review Model v1

## Purpose

Define a simple model for reviewing search terms before PPC cleanup decisions.

This is manual review guidance, not campaign automation.

## Review Areas

- commercial intent;
- informational leakage;
- semantic contamination;
- equipment ambiguity;
- competitor ambiguity;
- geo ambiguity;
- B2B / B2C ambiguity;
- irrelevant modifiers;
- accidental broad-match pollution;
- negative keyword candidates.

## Simple Statuses

- keep;
- monitor;
- split;
- exclude;
- SAFE_UNKNOWN.

## Operator Rule

Clean terms that clearly waste budget or confuse intent.

Do not over-filter terms just because they are imperfect. Some noisy phrases may still carry commercial value in local-service markets.

## Required Evidence

Record:

- source campaign or ad group;
- observed term;
- likely intent;
- risk reason;
- proposed action;
- confidence;
- human reviewer.

## Boundary

ORCA does not automatically optimize campaigns or apply negatives. Human review is mandatory.
