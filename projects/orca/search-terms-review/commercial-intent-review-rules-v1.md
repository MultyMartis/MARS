# Commercial Intent Review Rules v1

## Purpose

Help an operator judge whether a search term is likely useful commercial demand.

## Strong Commercial Intent Signals

- service request language;
- local service modifier;
- urgent or dispatch wording;
- price or estimate request;
- provider selection language;
- call or order behavior implied;
- service + city or district.

## Weak Commercial Intent Signals

- how-to wording;
- definition or instruction wording;
- photos, examples, or specs only;
- free or DIY modifiers;
- job or vacancy modifiers;
- general research without service action;
- unrelated comparison behavior.

## Ambiguous Commercial Intent

Mark `SAFE_UNKNOWN` when the term may be commercial but evidence is weak.

Examples:

- equipment term without service verb;
- city term without action;
- broad price query;
- competitor name with unclear intent.

## Review Rule

Commercial intent is contextual. A term that is weak in one niche may be useful in another local-service niche.

## Boundary

ORCA does not determine conversion value or optimize campaigns automatically. Human review is mandatory.
