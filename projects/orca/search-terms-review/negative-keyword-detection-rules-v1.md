# Negative Keyword Detection Rules v1

## Purpose

Support careful negative keyword discovery without overblocking useful demand.

## When Negatives Help

Negatives help when a term clearly shows:

- irrelevant service;
- wrong equipment meaning;
- informational-only intent;
- job or vacancy intent;
- free or DIY intent;
- wrong region;
- competitor terms outside competitor campaigns;
- B2B/B2C mismatch.

## When Negatives Become Dangerous

Negatives become dangerous when:

- the phrase has mixed commercial meaning;
- regional language is ambiguous;
- the modifier appears in valuable long-tail terms;
- the term may indicate a useful service variant;
- the operator lacks landing or business context;
- one bad search term triggers an overbroad exclusion.

## Overblocking Risks

- lost commercial long-tail demand;
- blocked regional phrases;
- blocked urgent variants;
- blocked competitor research that may be strategically useful;
- blocked B2B/B2C terms that the business can actually serve.

## Review Rule

Prefer narrow negatives when evidence is weak.

Use `SAFE_UNKNOWN` for terms that need more account or business context.

## Boundary

ORCA does not add negatives automatically or claim semantic certainty. Human review is mandatory.
