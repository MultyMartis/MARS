# Search Term Cleanliness Rules v1

## Purpose

Keep search-term review focused on PPC cleanliness, not theory.

## Cleanliness Checks

- does the term match the service?
- does it show commercial intent?
- does geo match campaign and landing?
- does it fit the ad group?
- does it need a separate intent group?
- does it reveal a negative keyword candidate?
- does it show broad-match pollution?

## Clean Term Signals

- clear service request;
- useful local modifier;
- compatible landing;
- compatible CTA;
- no obvious irrelevant modifiers;
- no major B2B/B2C mismatch;
- no misleading equipment meaning.

## Dirty Term Signals

- mixed service meaning;
- informational-only language;
- wrong city or region;
- job or DIY intent;
- competitor ambiguity outside strategy;
- unclear equipment/service overlap;
- low-commercial modifier.

## Operator Rule

Semantic cleanliness matters, but over-filtering can lose useful demand.

Do not remove terms without checking intent-loss risk.

## Boundary

ORCA does not optimize search terms automatically. Human review is mandatory.
