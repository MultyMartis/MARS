# Search Term Risk Signals v1

## Purpose

List practical risk signals for search-term review.

## Risk Signals

- low-commercial-intent queries;
- informational leakage;
- equipment ambiguity;
- competitor ambiguity;
- geo ambiguity;
- B2B / B2C ambiguity;
- irrelevant modifiers;
- accidental broad-match pollution;
- wrong service intent;
- price-only research;
- job, vacancy, training, or DIY modifiers.

## Ambiguity Types

### Equipment Ambiguity

The term may refer to equipment purchase, specs, rental, repair, or service.

### Competitor Ambiguity

The term may indicate competitor brand interest, comparison, complaint, or navigation.

### Geo Ambiguity

The term may mention a location that does not match the campaign, landing, or service area.

### B2B / B2C Ambiguity

The term may imply a buyer type the business cannot serve profitably.

## Operator Rule

Record the risk and action. Do not invent certainty when the term has multiple plausible meanings.

## Boundary

ORCA supports human review only. It does not optimize campaigns autonomously.
