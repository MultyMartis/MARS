# Search Terms Review Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA may help humans review search terms for PPC cleanliness and semantic risk.

ORCA does not autonomously optimize campaigns, add negatives, change bids, upload changes, or claim semantic certainty.

## Explicitly Rejected

- autonomous optimization claims;
- automatic negatives;
- unsupported semantic certainty;
- fake PPC intelligence claims;
- runtime campaign management;
- hidden account operations;
- telemetry or dashboarding;
- scraping or browser agents.

## Human Review Requirements

A human must review:

- query meaning;
- commercial intent;
- service relevance;
- geo relevance;
- negative keyword candidates;
- overblocking risk;
- regional phrase risk;
- current account context.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know actual lead quality, conversion value, account history, match-type behavior, search volume stability, or business fit unless verified by a human.
