# Evidence Strength Model v1

## Purpose

Classifies how strongly ORCA evidence supports a commercial pattern. Evidence strength is not truth certification. It is a human-reviewed reliability signal for contextual PPC research.

ORCA observes reality. ORCA does not define reality. Patterns remain revisable when new, contradictory, regional, seasonal, or fresher evidence appears.

## Strength Levels

## WEAK

Use WEAK when evidence is isolated, unstable, poorly contextualized, or contradicted.

Typical signals:

- single observation;
- one query or one page only;
- missing timestamp, region, niche, or device context;
- high SERP volatility;
- high market volatility;
- frequent contradictions;
- source reliability is uncertain.

Allowed use: follow-up research lead only.

## MODERATE

Use MODERATE when evidence repeats in a limited context but remains narrow.

Typical signals:

- repeated observations inside one case;
- several related queries in one region;
- timestamp and device are known;
- contradiction frequency is low-to-medium;
- SERP stability is partial;
- market volatility is manageable but present.

Allowed use: hypothesis support for human review.

## STRONG

Use STRONG when evidence repeats across comparable contexts and contradictions are explained.

Typical signals:

- repeated observations across query groups;
- multi-case confirmation in a related niche;
- multi-source support from SERP, landing, competitor, or local evidence;
- clear region and device boundaries;
- low-to-medium contradiction frequency;
- SERP stability is observed across snapshots.

Allowed use: reusable intelligence candidate with explicit context limits.

## VERY STRONG

Use VERY STRONG only when evidence is repeatedly confirmed across time, cases, regions, or evidence types.

Typical signals:

- multi-region confirmation where regional transfer is justified;
- multi-case confirmation across comparable niches;
- repeated observations over time;
- low contradiction frequency;
- stable SERP conditions;
- low market volatility or documented volatility boundaries;
- human-reviewed reuse history.

Allowed use: high-reliability commercial intelligence for human-supervised planning. It still does not become an automatic rule.

## Scoring Inputs

- repeated observations;
- multi-region confirmation;
- multi-case confirmation;
- contradiction frequency;
- SERP stability;
- market volatility;
- source reliability;
- evidence freshness.

## Penalties

- Unstable SERPs reduce strength.
- Contradictory evidence reduces strength unless clearly bounded.
- Regional uncertainty caps strength at MODERATE.
- Seasonal distortion caps strength until comparable seasonal evidence exists.
- Stale evidence must be downgraded or marked SAFE UNKNOWN.

## Boundary

Evidence strength describes support quality. It does not authorize automatic bidding, automatic keyword decisions, market forecasting, or unsupported commercial conclusions.
