# Call-First Campaign Rules v1

## Purpose

Support manual preparation of call-first campaign structures where immediate contact is commercially important.

This is not call tracking automation, bid automation, or autonomous campaign launch.

## Use Call-First Thinking When

- urgent intent is visible;
- users likely need immediate service;
- SERP competitors emphasize phone calls;
- mobile click-to-call is prominent;
- WhatsApp is secondary or supporting;
- forms are likely too slow for the intent;
- response time is a key trust factor.

## Structure Guidance

For call-first segments, review:

- query urgency;
- mobile visibility;
- phone CTA;
- landing phone placement;
- hours or availability claims;
- response promise;
- local service coverage;
- trust proof near CTA.

## Ad Group Fit

Keep call-first ad groups commercially tight.

Avoid mixing:

- urgent and planned-service queries;
- call-ready and research-only queries;
- local emergency demand and broad informational demand;
- services that require different response promises.

## Practical Output

Record:

- call-first segment: yes / no / SAFE_UNKNOWN;
- primary phone CTA;
- backup CTA;
- landing proof needed;
- risk notes;
- human reviewer.

## Boundary

ORCA does not place calls, configure tracking, upload campaigns, manage bids, or launch ads. Human review is mandatory.
