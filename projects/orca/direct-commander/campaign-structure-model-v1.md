# Campaign Structure Model v1

## Purpose

Define a simple campaign structure model for manual preparation before possible Direct Commander import work.

This is not an export engine, upload tool, Direct API integration, or autonomous campaign builder.

## Practical Campaign Layers

Use only layers that help PPC control.

- search campaigns - core commercial demand;
- urgent-intent campaigns - immediate need, call-heavy queries;
- geo campaigns - separate cities, districts, or service zones when useful;
- competitor campaigns - competitor-name demand where legally and strategically appropriate;
- branded campaigns - own brand and known business names;
- mobile-priority campaigns - mobile-heavy demand where CTA and landing behavior differ;
- high-trust campaigns - queries needing stronger trust proof, pricing clarity, or service assurance.

## Minimal Campaign Record

Each campaign draft should define:

- campaign name;
- region;
- device priority;
- intent type;
- service scope;
- landing URL;
- primary CTA;
- main trust requirement;
- negative keyword layer;
- human review owner;
- production status: draft / reviewed / blocked.

## Structure Principles

- Segment only when control improves.
- Keep intent clean.
- Keep service scope clear.
- Keep geo logic understandable.
- Avoid duplicate semantic intent.
- Prefer a smaller usable structure over a large fragile one.

## Revisions

Campaign structures remain revisable. A first structure is a working draft, not proof of optimal account architecture.

## Boundary

ORCA does not upload, launch, manage bids, or optimize campaigns. Human review is mandatory before any platform use.
