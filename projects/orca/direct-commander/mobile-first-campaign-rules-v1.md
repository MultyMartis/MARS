# Mobile-First Campaign Rules v1

## Purpose

Help an operator decide when mobile-first campaign thinking is useful.

This is preparation guidance only. It is not device automation, bid automation, or automatic optimization.

## Use Mobile Priority When

- mobile SERP shows stronger call-first behavior;
- click-to-call is central to conversion;
- WhatsApp or messenger behavior is common;
- urgent intent is likely mobile-heavy;
- landing experience differs meaningfully on mobile;
- competitors rely on mobile-visible trust signals;
- desktop and mobile SERPs show materially different pressure.

## Mobile Review Points

- phone visibility;
- call CTA clarity;
- WhatsApp visibility;
- first-screen trust proof;
- fast response promise;
- map/local pack pressure;
- page speed or obvious loading friction;
- form friction;
- pricing visibility.

## Avoid Mobile Over-Segmentation

Do not create separate mobile-priority structures when:

- mobile and desktop behavior are effectively the same;
- volume is too low;
- landing and CTA do not differ;
- the split creates reporting noise;
- the operator cannot explain the control benefit.

## Practical Output

Record:

- mobile priority: yes / no / SAFE_UNKNOWN;
- reason;
- CTA requirement;
- landing concern;
- confidence;
- human reviewer.

## Boundary

ORCA does not manage device bids, upload settings, or optimize campaigns. Human review is mandatory.
