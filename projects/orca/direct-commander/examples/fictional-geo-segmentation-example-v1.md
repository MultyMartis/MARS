# Fictional Geo Segmentation Example v1

## Status

Fictional demonstration only. Non-production. Non-validated.

This example is not an upload file, not a Direct Commander export, and not proof that geo segmentation is useful in a real account.

## Scenario

- niche: fictional cargo delivery service;
- region: fictional metro area;
- review purpose: show when geo separation may help and when it may harm.

## Useful Geo Split

### Campaign A - City Core

- region: city core;
- service coverage: strong;
- landing: city-core landing;
- CTA: call or form;
- reason: fast response and strong local proof.

### Campaign B - Outer District

- region: outer district;
- service coverage: limited;
- landing: district-compatible landing;
- CTA: call to confirm availability;
- reason: different availability and response promise.

## Harmful Geo Split

Avoid separate campaigns for tiny districts when:

- the same landing serves all areas;
- there is no budget difference;
- volume is too low;
- the operator cannot maintain exclusions;
- reporting would become unreadable.

## Review Checks

- region matches landing;
- service coverage is real;
- geo keywords do not conflict with targeting;
- wrong-region negatives are reviewed;
- duplicate intent is not spread across too many campaigns.

## Boundary

ORCA does not configure platform targeting, upload geo settings, launch campaigns, manage bids, or optimize performance.
