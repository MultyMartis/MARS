# Fictional Local Service Campaign Example v1

## Status

Fictional demonstration only. Non-production. Non-validated.

This example is not an upload file, not a Direct Commander export, and not proof of campaign performance.

## Scenario

- niche: local emergency plumbing;
- region: fictional city;
- purpose: show simple campaign separation;
- human review: required before any real use.

## Draft Campaigns

### Search - Core Service

- intent: planned and general commercial service demand;
- geo: fictional city;
- device priority: balanced;
- landing: core service page;
- CTA: call or form;
- risk: broad queries may include informational leakage.

### Search - Urgent Service

- intent: immediate repair demand;
- geo: fictional city;
- device priority: mobile priority;
- landing: urgent repair page;
- CTA: call-first;
- risk: response promise must be real.

### Search - District Geo

- intent: local district demand;
- geo: one fictional district;
- device priority: balanced;
- landing: district-compatible page;
- CTA: call;
- risk: low-volume fragmentation.

## Example Ad Group Logic

- one service meaning per ad group;
- urgent and planned terms separated;
- district terms separated only if useful;
- informational terms reviewed for exclusion;
- landing must match service and urgency.

## Human Review Notes

- check real geo coverage;
- verify response promises;
- review negative keywords;
- confirm landing match;
- avoid over-segmentation.

## Boundary

ORCA does not upload, launch, bid, optimize, or autonomously create production campaigns.
