# Ad Group Structure Rules v1

## Purpose

Keep ad groups clean enough for practical PPC work and human review.

## Priorities

Ad groups should prioritize:

- intent purity;
- service purity;
- geo purity;
- commercial purity;
- semantic clarity;
- landing match;
- CTA match.

## Good Ad Group

A good ad group has:

- one clear commercial intent;
- one main service meaning;
- one geo logic;
- keywords that can share ad copy honestly;
- a landing page that matches the intent;
- negative keywords that reduce obvious waste;
- no hidden informational or irrelevant equipment intent.

## Bad Ad Group Signals

- mixed urgent and planned-service intent;
- mixed service and equipment meanings;
- multiple cities or districts without reason;
- commercial and informational queries together;
- competitor and generic demand together;
- keywords needing different landing pages;
- ad copy that only fits some keywords;
- too many near-duplicate meanings.

## Operator Rule

If one ad text cannot honestly answer the whole group, split or clean the group.

If splitting creates low-volume fragments with no control benefit, clean keywords instead.

## Boundary

Ad group drafts are not production campaigns. ORCA does not upload, launch, bid, or optimize them. Human review is mandatory.
