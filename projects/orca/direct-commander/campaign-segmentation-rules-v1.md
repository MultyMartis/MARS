# Campaign Segmentation Rules v1

## Purpose

Help an operator decide when campaign segmentation is useful and when it becomes operational waste.

## Segment When It Improves Control

Segmentation can be useful when it separates:

- different regions with different economics;
- urgent and planned-service intent;
- mobile-first and desktop-balanced behavior;
- different service lines;
- branded and non-branded demand;
- competitor demand;
- trust-heavy offers from simple transactional offers.

## Do Not Segment Just To Look Sophisticated

Segmentation becomes harmful when:

- each campaign has too little volume;
- the same intent appears in many places;
- geo logic becomes hard to audit;
- negative keywords become inconsistent;
- landing URLs repeat without a reason;
- naming becomes unclear;
- the operator cannot explain why a split exists.

## Over-Fragmentation Risks

- low-volume campaigns;
- slower learning;
- duplicate-intent competition;
- confusing reports;
- more human QA work;
- more chances for wrong geo, landing, or CTA settings;
- higher maintenance overhead.

## Practical Rule

Create a segment only if it changes at least one real PPC control:

- budget;
- geo;
- landing;
- CTA;
- ad copy;
- negative keywords;
- urgency handling;
- trust proof.

## Human Review

Every segmentation decision should be reviewed by a human before import preparation continues.

## Boundary

ORCA prepares structure drafts only. It does not upload, launch, bid, optimize, or create production campaigns autonomously.
