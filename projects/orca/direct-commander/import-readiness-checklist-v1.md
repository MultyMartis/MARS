# Import Readiness Checklist v1

## Purpose

Provide a final human review checklist before any Direct Commander import preparation proceeds.

This checklist does not authorize upload, launch, bidding, or production use.

## Naming Consistency

- campaign names are readable;
- ad group names match intent;
- geo names are consistent;
- urgent, mobile, competitor, and branded labels are clear;
- draft status is visible.

## Geo Consistency

- campaign region is clear;
- keyword geo modifiers match region;
- landing can serve the region;
- local trust proof matches region;
- wrong-location negatives are reviewed.

## Intent Consistency

- urgent and planned intent are not mixed without reason;
- competitor and generic demand are separated;
- branded demand is separated where needed;
- informational leakage is marked or removed;
- each ad group has one main commercial meaning.

## Negative Keyword Review

- shared negatives reviewed;
- campaign negatives reviewed;
- ad group negatives reviewed;
- overbroad exclusions checked;
- missing obvious exclusions noted;
- uncertain exclusions marked `SAFE_UNKNOWN`.

## Landing Consistency

- landing URL matches service;
- landing URL matches geo;
- CTA matches intent;
- trust proof supports the promise;
- pricing claims are not contradicted;
- mobile landing is usable.

## Mobile CTA Review

- phone CTA visible;
- WhatsApp or messenger role clear;
- form friction reviewed;
- urgent-intent mobile path reviewed;
- mobile-first split justified or rejected.

## Trust CTA Review

- response promise reviewed;
- local proof reviewed;
- equipment/service proof reviewed;
- reviews or testimonials reviewed where available;
- legal or company proof reviewed where relevant.

## Duplication Checks

- duplicate keywords;
- duplicate semantic intent;
- duplicate geo segments;
- duplicate landing without control reason;
- duplicate competitor terms;
- duplicate negatives.

## Final Human Decision

- ready for manual Direct Commander preparation: yes / no;
- blocked by:
- SAFE UNKNOWN:
- reviewer:
- date:

## Boundary

ORCA does not upload, launch, bid, optimize, or autonomously create production campaigns.
