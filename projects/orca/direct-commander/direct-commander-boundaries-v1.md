# Direct Commander Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA may help prepare campaign structures for human review before possible Yandex Direct Commander work.

ORCA does not upload campaigns, launch campaigns, manage bids, optimize budgets, operate accounts, or create production campaigns autonomously.

## Explicitly Rejected

- autonomous upload;
- automatic campaign launch;
- Direct API integration;
- runtime export generation;
- autonomous bidding;
- automatic optimization;
- hidden campaign management;
- browser automation;
- fake AI campaign generation claims.

## Human Review Requirements

Before any platform action, a human operator must review:

- campaign names;
- geo settings;
- ad group intent;
- keyword cleanliness;
- negative keywords;
- landing match;
- mobile CTA;
- trust and response promises;
- duplication risks;
- compliance with current platform rules.

## Operational Rule

Treat every ORCA structure as a draft.

No structure is production-ready until a qualified human checks it against the real account, real business, real landings, real geo, and current Yandex Direct requirements.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know actual account history, auction behavior, CPC, conversion quality, moderation outcomes, platform changes, or business capacity unless a human provides verified evidence.
