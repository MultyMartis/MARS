# Geo Segmentation Rules v1

## Purpose

Keep regional campaign logic clear before Direct Commander preparation.

Geo separation should improve control, not create needless fragments.

## Segment Geo When

- cities have different economics;
- service availability differs;
- landing content differs by region;
- ad copy requires local proof;
- urgent response time differs by area;
- local pack or SERP pressure differs materially;
- budget control requires separation.

## Avoid Geo Segmentation When

- the same service, landing, CTA, and budget apply everywhere;
- search volume is too low;
- district names are unstable or rarely used;
- geo keywords duplicate campaign geo settings without benefit;
- the operator cannot maintain exclusions cleanly.

## Geo Consistency Checks

- campaign geo matches query geo;
- ad group geo does not conflict with campaign geo;
- landing page can serve the region;
- local trust signals match the region;
- negative keywords reduce irrelevant locations;
- neighboring region risk is understood.

## Common Risks

- city and district mixed without reason;
- service area broader than landing proof;
- geo keyword says one location while campaign targets another;
- duplicate intent split across many geo campaigns;
- low-volume fragments that cannot be interpreted.

## Boundary

ORCA does not configure platform targeting, upload geo settings, or launch campaigns. Human review is mandatory.
