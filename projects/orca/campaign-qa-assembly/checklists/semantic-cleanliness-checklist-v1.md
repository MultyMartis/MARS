# Semantic Cleanliness Checklist v1

## Status

Manual semantic QA checklist.

## Checklist

- one main intent per ad group;
- service meaning is clear;
- urgent and planned intent separated where needed;
- geo modifiers match campaign region;
- informational leakage reviewed;
- equipment ambiguity reviewed;
- competitor ambiguity reviewed;
- duplicate intent reviewed;
- negatives do not overblock useful demand;
- low-commercial terms reviewed;
- SAFE_UNKNOWN recorded.

## Boundary

ORCA does not apply semantic changes automatically. Human review is mandatory.
