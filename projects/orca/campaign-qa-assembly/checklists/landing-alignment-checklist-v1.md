# Landing Alignment Checklist v1

## Status

Manual ad-to-landing review checklist.

## Checklist

- service in ad appears on landing;
- region in ad appears on landing or service area;
- CTA in ad is visible on landing;
- phone or WhatsApp path works if promised;
- trust claims are supported;
- pricing claims are explained;
- urgency claims have fast contact path;
- landing is not generic for a specific query;
- mobile landing is reviewed;
- SAFE_UNKNOWN recorded.

## Boundary

ORCA does not optimize landings or predict conversion. Human review is mandatory.
