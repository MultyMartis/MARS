# Pre-Launch Checklist v1

## Status

Manual checklist. Not autonomous launch approval.

## Checklist

- campaign names are clear;
- geo settings are consistent;
- ad groups have clean intent;
- keywords match ads;
- ads match landings;
- CTA is supported;
- trust claims are supported;
- mobile path is usable;
- extensions are relevant;
- negative keywords reviewed;
- unsupported claims removed;
- fragmentation risk reviewed;
- SAFE_UNKNOWN recorded.

## Boundary

ORCA does not launch campaigns. Human review is mandatory.
