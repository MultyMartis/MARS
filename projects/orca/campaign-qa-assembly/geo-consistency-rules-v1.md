# Geo Consistency Rules v1

## Purpose

Review whether geo logic is consistent across campaign, keywords, ads, and landing.

## Checks

- campaign region is clear;
- keyword geo modifiers match the region;
- ad geo claim matches targeting;
- landing shows service area;
- local proof supports the claim;
- wrong-region negatives are reviewed;
- district splits have a real control reason.

## Risk Signals

- city in ad missing from landing;
- mixed districts without reason;
- campaign targets one area while keywords imply another;
- low-volume geo fragmentation;
- local trust proof absent.

## Boundary

ORCA does not configure geo targeting or launch campaigns. Human review is mandatory.
