# Campaign QA Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA may help humans review campaign assembly risks.

ORCA does not launch campaigns, approve launches autonomously, optimize accounts, manage bids, upload changes, or claim campaign certainty.

## Explicitly Rejected

- autonomous launch approval;
- autonomous optimization;
- fake campaign certainty;
- unsupported performance claims;
- automatic QA pass/fail;
- Direct API integration;
- hidden account changes.

## Human Review Requirements

A human must review:

- keywords;
- ads;
- extensions;
- landing pages;
- geo settings;
- CTA and trust claims;
- mobile behavior;
- final platform requirements.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know actual moderation outcome, CPC, conversion quality, lead value, account history, platform eligibility, or future performance unless verified by a human.
