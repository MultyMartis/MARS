# Campaign Assembly Model v1

## Purpose

Define a simple final review model for PPC campaign assembly.

This is manual QA support, not autonomous campaign approval.

## Review Areas

- keyword-to-ad alignment;
- ad-to-landing alignment;
- geo alignment;
- CTA alignment;
- trust alignment;
- mobile consistency;
- intent consistency;
- negative keyword consistency;
- extension relevance;
- fragmentation risk.

## Simple Statuses

- ready for human platform preparation;
- revise;
- blocked;
- SAFE_UNKNOWN.

## Operator Rule

Review the campaign as a working system. A clean keyword, ad, or landing can still fail if the parts do not match.

## Boundary

ORCA does not launch campaigns or approve readiness autonomously. Human review is mandatory.
