# Final Risk Review Rules v1

## Purpose

List final campaign risks for human review before platform preparation.

## Final Risks

- landing mismatch;
- semantic contamination;
- weak CTA alignment;
- weak trust support;
- geo mismatch;
- mobile friction;
- unsupported claims;
- operational overload;
- extension overload;
- negative keyword overblocking;
- unclear launch ownership.

## Review Rule

Block or revise anything that could waste spend, mislead users, or create operational confusion.

Do not claim performance readiness. This is risk review, not proof.

## Boundary

ORCA does not approve launch or predict performance. Human review is mandatory.
