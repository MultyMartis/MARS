# Campaign Fragmentation Rules v1

## Purpose

Identify over-segmentation and unnecessary campaign complexity.

## Fragmentation Risks

- over-segmentation;
- duplicate campaigns;
- duplicate intent;
- low-volume fragmentation;
- operational overhead;
- unnecessary complexity;
- duplicated negatives;
- repeated landing URLs without control reason.

## Useful Segmentation

Segmentation is useful only when it changes real PPC control:

- budget;
- geo;
- landing;
- CTA;
- ad copy;
- negative keyword logic;
- urgency handling;
- trust proof.

## Review Rule

If the operator cannot explain why a campaign split exists, merge, simplify, or mark for review.

## Boundary

ORCA does not restructure campaigns autonomously. Human review is mandatory.
