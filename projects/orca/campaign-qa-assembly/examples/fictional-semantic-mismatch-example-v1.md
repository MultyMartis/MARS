# Fictional Semantic Mismatch Example v1

## Status

Fictional demonstration only. Non-production. Non-validated.

This is not account evidence or an optimization recommendation.

## Scenario

- campaign: fictional urgent service campaign;
- issue: mixed urgent and informational terms;
- purpose: show semantic mismatch risk.

## Observed Mismatch

- urgent ad promises fast contact;
- ad group includes how-to and price-research terms;
- landing is urgent service page;
- negative keywords are incomplete;
- some broad-match terms may waste spend.

## Risk

- low-commercial clicks;
- weak ad relevance;
- user expectation mismatch;
- wasted urgent campaign budget;
- overblocking risk if negatives are too broad.

## Safer Handling

- separate urgent commercial terms;
- review informational terms as negatives;
- keep ambiguous price terms in review;
- mark uncertain exclusions as `SAFE_UNKNOWN`.

## Boundary

ORCA does not apply negatives or optimize campaigns autonomously.
