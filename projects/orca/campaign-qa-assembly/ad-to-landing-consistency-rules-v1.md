# Ad-To-Landing Consistency Rules v1

## Purpose

Check whether ads and landings support the same user expectation.

## Checks

- service in ad is visible on landing;
- geo claim is supported;
- CTA matches page action;
- trust claim is visible or supported;
- pricing claim is explained;
- urgency claim has a fast contact path;
- landing answers the query intent.

## Risk Signals

- wrong-service landing;
- generic landing for specific ad;
- ad promises price not shown on page;
- ad promises urgency but page is slow or generic;
- trust claim unsupported by page evidence.

## Boundary

ORCA does not optimize landing pages or predict conversion. Human review is mandatory.
