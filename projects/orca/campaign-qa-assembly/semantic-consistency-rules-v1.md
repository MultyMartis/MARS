# Semantic Consistency Rules v1

## Purpose

Review whether campaign semantics stay clean across keywords, ads, negatives, and landing pages.

## Checks

- keyword intent matches ad promise;
- ad group has one main intent;
- service meaning is not mixed;
- urgent and planned intent are separated where needed;
- competitor, branded, and generic terms are not mixed without reason;
- negative keywords do not overblock useful demand;
- low-commercial terms are handled.

## Risk Signals

- semantic contamination;
- broad-match pollution;
- duplicate intent;
- mixed geo modifiers;
- informational leakage;
- equipment/service ambiguity.

## Boundary

ORCA does not autonomously clean semantics or apply negatives. Human review is mandatory.
