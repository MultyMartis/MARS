# CTA Consistency Rules v1

## Purpose

Review whether CTA logic is consistent across keywords, ads, extensions, and landing pages.

## Checks

- call-first ads lead to visible phone path;
- WhatsApp messaging has real support;
- estimate requests are explained;
- urgent CTAs match response capacity;
- extensions do not create competing actions;
- landing primary action matches the ad.

## Risk Signals

- call-first ad with hidden phone;
- WhatsApp claim without response support;
- form-heavy page for urgent traffic;
- too many CTAs;
- response promise not supported.

## Boundary

ORCA does not configure calls, forms, WhatsApp, tracking, or launch campaigns. Human review is mandatory.
