# ORCA SAFE UNKNOWN Rules v1

## Mark SAFE UNKNOWN When

- SERP behavior was not checked live.
- Mobile behavior was inferred, not tested.
- Landing claims cannot be verified.
- Search-term volume, cost, or conversion impact is not available.
- Competitor claims are visible but not proven.
- Platform settings are unavailable.

## Do Not Guess

- expected CPC;
- conversion rate;
- campaign performance;
- hidden targeting;
- competitor budgets;
- landing analytics;
- user behavior beyond visible evidence.

## Useful Format

- Unknown:
- Why it matters:
- What would verify it:
- Whether action should wait:

## Rule

Unknown does not block every decision. It blocks unsupported certainty.
