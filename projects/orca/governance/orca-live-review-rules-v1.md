# ORCA Live Review Rules v1

## Live Review Priorities

1. PPC decision usefulness.
2. Visible evidence.
3. Mobile-first reality.
4. Operator energy.
5. Short reporting.

## During Review

- Use one primary checklist.
- Capture top signals only.
- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Mark unknowns plainly.
- Convert findings into human decisions.

## After Review

- Write a short report.
- Note whether the checklist helped.
- Remove or revise steps that slowed the operator down.
- Do not claim campaign impact without platform evidence.

## Fatigue Rule

When attention drops, stop. A shorter correct review is better than a long noisy one.
