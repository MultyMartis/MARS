# Landing Match QA v1

## Purpose

Checks whether a landing page can satisfy the search intent, offer promise, local context, and conversion path implied by a PPC cluster.

## Checks

- Offer clarity.
- CTA strength.
- Trust architecture.
- Pricing transparency.
- Urgency support.
- Differentiation.
- Lead capture structure.
- Mobile commercial quality.
- Local trust signals.
- Conversion friction.

## Practical Questions

- Does the page clearly provide the searched service?
- Can the user act quickly from mobile?
- Are phone, form, messenger, or quote actions visible?
- Is price, process, or timing explained where users expect it?
- Are reviews, licenses, address, examples, or guarantees credible?
- Does the page match the region and service area?

## Failure Signals

- Cluster points to homepage when a service page is needed.
- Local query points to non-local content.
- Emergency query points to slow or generic lead form.
- Price query points to no pricing context.
- Trust-heavy niche lacks proof.

## Boundary

Landing match QA informs human campaign decisions. It does not edit websites, automate CRO, publish campaigns, or optimize bids.
