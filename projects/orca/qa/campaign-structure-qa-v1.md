# Campaign Structure QA v1

## Purpose

Checks whether a draft PPC campaign structure is clean, reviewable, and aligned with search-first architecture.

## Checks

- Campaign segmentation follows business, geography, intent, and platform logic.
- Ad groups map cleanly to semantic clusters.
- Brand, competitor, local, urgent, and informational intents are separated where needed.
- Naming is readable and stable.
- Negatives are assigned at the right level.
- Landing pages are mapped or marked SAFE UNKNOWN.
- Budget and bid placeholders are not treated as decisions.

## Yandex.Direct Realities

- Direct Commander exports need predictable structure and clear naming.
- Local service campaigns often require geo-sensitive segmentation.
- Aggregator-heavy niches may need tighter commercial filtering.
- Broad campaign structures can hide low-quality traffic.

## Blockers

- Mixed intent ad groups.
- Missing landing pages for core clusters.
- Hidden B2B/B2C conflict.
- Unresolved competitor policy risk.
- Structure implies automatic budget or bid decisions.

## Boundary

Campaign structure QA approves review readiness only. It does not activate campaigns, control account settings, automate bidding, or run orchestration.
