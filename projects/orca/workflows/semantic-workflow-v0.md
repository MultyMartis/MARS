# Semantic Workflow v0

## Goal

Collect, clean, and cluster PPC semantics into a reviewable structure for campaign architecture.

## Steps

1. Confirm approved seed keywords and research notes.
2. Collect keyword candidates from approved sources.
3. Normalize wording and remove obvious duplicates.
4. Mark intent, relevance, and source context.
5. Build clusters and negative keyword candidates.
6. Flag ambiguous or low-confidence terms.

## Inputs

- Project input contract.
- SERP research output contract.
- Seed keywords.
- Offer categories.
- Human exclusions.

## Outputs

- Semantic cluster contract.
- Keyword candidate list.
- Negative keyword candidates.
- Ambiguity and duplicate notes.

## Human Checkpoints

- Approve keyword sources.
- Review exclusions and negatives.
- Resolve ambiguous keyword intent.
- Confirm clusters before campaign generation.

## Failure Risks

- Keyword expansion drifts away from the offer.
- Duplicates create internal competition.
- Negative keywords block useful traffic.
- Mixed-intent clusters produce weak ads.

## REPORT Expectations

The report must include source coverage, cluster count, removed duplicates, negative candidates, SAFE UNKNOWN items, and readiness for campaign architecture.

No keyword should be treated as platform-ready without human review.
