# Campaign Generation Workflow v0

## Goal

Convert reviewed semantic clusters into a draft PPC campaign structure and ad copy package for human approval.

## Steps

1. Confirm approved semantic clusters and project constraints.
2. Define draft campaign segmentation.
3. Map clusters to campaigns and ad groups.
4. Add targeting, landing page, and exclusion notes.
5. Draft ad copy variants where source evidence supports the claim.
6. Record unresolved decisions for the human operator.

## Inputs

- Semantic cluster contract.
- Project input contract.
- SERP research output.
- Landing page notes.
- Brand and copy constraints.

## Outputs

- Campaign structure contract.
- Draft ad copy.
- Naming and targeting notes.
- Human decision list.

## Human Checkpoints

- Approve campaign segmentation.
- Confirm landing page mapping.
- Review ad copy and claims.
- Confirm platform assumptions before export.

## Failure Risks

- Campaigns become too fragmented or too broad.
- Ad copy includes unsupported claims.
- Landing page mismatch reduces relevance.
- Human budget or bid decisions are implied instead of requested.

## REPORT Expectations

The report must include campaign and ad group counts, unresolved decisions, ad copy risks, SAFE UNKNOWN items, and readiness for export packaging.

Generated structures are drafts only and must not be uploaded without human review.
