# SERP Research Workflow v0

## Goal

Create reviewable SERP and competitor research notes for PPC planning without claiming access to hidden platform data or competitor performance.

## Steps

1. Confirm project brief, target geography, language, and seed queries.
2. Review search results and ad examples using human-approved sources.
3. Record visible competitors, offers, wording patterns, and result types.
4. Identify query intent patterns and unclear cases.
5. Produce research notes and SAFE UNKNOWN items for downstream workflows.

## Inputs

- Project input contract.
- Seed query list.
- Target geography.
- Known competitors.
- Offer and landing page notes.

## Outputs

- SERP research output contract.
- Competitor observation notes.
- Intent notes.
- Research risk list.

## Human Checkpoints

- Approve source and region assumptions.
- Confirm competitor relevance.
- Resolve unclear offer positioning.
- Decide whether research depth is enough for semantic work.

## Failure Risks

- Localized results differ from operator assumptions.
- Ads are temporary or personalized.
- Competitor claims are misread as verified strategy.
- Informational queries are mixed into commercial clusters.

## REPORT Expectations

The report must include completed queries, source limitations, key observations, competitor notes, SAFE UNKNOWN items, and whether the output is ready for semantic collection.

All findings require human review before use in campaign planning.
