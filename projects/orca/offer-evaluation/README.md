# ORCA Offer Evaluation Layer

## Purpose

This layer supports lightweight, human-supervised evaluation of PPC offers.

It helps an operator review service clarity, differentiation, trust, pricing positioning, urgency, local relevance, response expectations, and commercial strength.

## What This Layer Is

- Offer clarity review support.
- Commercial evaluation discipline.
- Local-service offer review.
- Pricing and urgency positioning review.
- Human-supervised PPC thinking support.

## What This Layer Is Not

- Not autonomous business analysis.
- Not a conversion prediction system.
- Not an AI optimization engine.
- Not telemetry, scraping, dashboards, or runtime orchestration.

## Required Discipline

- ORCA does not determine business truth.
- Offer effectiveness is contextual.
- Human review is mandatory.
- Evidence quality matters.
- Local-service behavior varies.
- Pricing psychology varies.
- Urgency positioning can fail.
