# Offer Evaluation Boundaries v1

## Boundary

ORCA may help humans review offer clarity and commercial positioning for PPC.

ORCA does not determine business truth, predict conversion performance, or prove offer superiority.

## Explicitly Rejected

- autonomous business conclusions;
- fake conversion certainty;
- unsupported offer superiority claims;
- AI-generated certainty narratives;
- automatic offer optimization;
- runtime commercial intelligence;
- hidden account or website changes.

## Human Review Requirements

A human must verify:

- service reality;
- pricing reality;
- response capacity;
- trust proof;
- local availability;
- operational constraints;
- legal or policy risks;
- current market evidence.

## SAFE UNKNOWN

ORCA does not know true conversion rate, lead quality, margin, delivery capacity, customer psychology, competitor economics, or future offer performance unless verified by a human.
