# Commercial Clarity Rules v1

## Purpose

Review whether the offer is clear enough for PPC traffic.

## Clarity Checks

- service is immediately understandable;
- target location is clear;
- user action is clear;
- response expectation is clear;
- pricing or estimate logic is clear;
- trust proof is visible enough;
- offer matches the search intent.

## Weak Clarity Signals

- vague service wording;
- unclear audience;
- unclear region;
- generic "quality service" language;
- no obvious CTA;
- no estimate or pricing explanation;
- service details hidden behind brand slogans.

## Review Rule

If the offer cannot be explained in one plain sentence, it is probably too vague for search traffic.

## Boundary

ORCA does not determine business truth or conversion certainty. Human review is mandatory.
