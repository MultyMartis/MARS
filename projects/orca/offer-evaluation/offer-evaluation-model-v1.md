# Offer Evaluation Model v1

## Purpose

Define a simple model for reviewing PPC offer strength without claiming business certainty.

## Review Areas

- service clarity;
- differentiation clarity;
- trust positioning;
- pricing communication;
- urgency handling;
- local relevance;
- response expectations;
- operational transparency;
- commercial strength.

## Simple Statuses

- strong;
- usable;
- weak;
- risky;
- SAFE_UNKNOWN.

## Core Question

Can a paid-search user quickly understand:

- what is offered;
- where it is available;
- why it is credible;
- how to respond;
- what price or estimate logic applies;
- whether urgency claims are real.

## Operator Rule

Evaluate the offer as a PPC working draft, not as universal business truth.

## Boundary

ORCA does not predict conversion performance or determine offer superiority. Human review is mandatory.
