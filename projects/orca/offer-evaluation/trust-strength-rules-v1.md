# Trust Strength Rules v1

## Purpose

Evaluate whether the offer has enough trust support for PPC traffic.

## Trust Strength Signals

- local proof;
- review visibility;
- business legitimacy signals;
- equipment or service proof;
- real photos or work examples;
- clear process;
- response expectation;
- transparent pricing or estimate basis.

## Weak Trust Signals

- vague claims;
- no local proof;
- no visible contact path;
- no service evidence;
- unsupported experience claims;
- unclear business identity;
- promises without operational detail.

## Trust Review Rule

Trust should reduce buyer uncertainty. It should not be decoration or hype.

## Boundary

ORCA does not validate business truth automatically. Human review is mandatory.
