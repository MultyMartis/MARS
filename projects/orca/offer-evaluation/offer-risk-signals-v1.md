# Offer Risk Signals v1

## Purpose

Identify practical offer risks before ad copy, landing, or campaign preparation.

## Risk Signals

- generic offers;
- fake differentiation;
- fake urgency;
- weak trust support;
- unclear service positioning;
- misleading pricing;
- unrealistic promises;
- unclear response expectations;
- local relevance gaps;
- operational transparency gaps.

## Generic Offer Risk

The offer sounds like any competitor could say the same thing.

Examples:

- high quality;
- professional service;
- affordable prices;
- fast work;
- reliable team.

These may be usable only if backed by specific proof.

## Unrealistic Promise Risk

Promises are risky when they exceed visible operational support.

Examples:

- instant arrival;
- guaranteed lowest price;
- always available;
- perfect result;
- no delays.

## Boundary

ORCA flags risks for human review. It does not determine business truth or conversion certainty.
