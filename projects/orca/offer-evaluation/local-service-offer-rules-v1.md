# Local Service Offer Rules v1

## Purpose

Support practical offer review for local-service PPC.

## Local-Service Offer Needs

- service clarity;
- region clarity;
- contact path;
- response expectation;
- trust support;
- estimate or price logic;
- operational transparency.

## Supported Contexts

- emergency services;
- cargo and moving services;
- equipment services;
- repair and installation;
- dispatch-style businesses;
- local-response businesses.

## Response Expectations

Review whether the offer explains:

- when the business can respond;
- how the user should contact;
- what details are needed;
- whether call or WhatsApp is preferred;
- what happens after contact.

## Boundary

ORCA does not determine whether the business can actually deliver. Human review is mandatory.
