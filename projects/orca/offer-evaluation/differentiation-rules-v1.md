# Differentiation Rules v1

## Purpose

Review whether an offer has clear, supportable differentiation.

## Useful Differentiation

- faster response if verified;
- local coverage advantage;
- specific equipment or capability;
- transparent estimate process;
- stronger trust proof;
- specialized service scope;
- clearer dispatch or communication process.

## Fake Differentiation

- "best";
- "highest quality";
- "number one";
- "professional team" without proof;
- fake urgency;
- unsupported guarantees;
- generic claims every competitor could say.

## Review Questions

- Is the difference specific?
- Is it visible to the user?
- Is it supported by evidence?
- Does it matter for this query?
- Does it change ad copy, landing, or CTA?

## Boundary

ORCA does not prove superiority. Differentiation claims require human evidence review.
