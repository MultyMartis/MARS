# Urgency Positioning Rules v1

## Purpose

Review whether urgency is useful, truthful, and operationally supported.

## Useful Urgency

- urgent service if the business can respond;
- same-day wording if verified;
- check availability;
- call to confirm time;
- dispatch-style wording when dispatch exists;
- response promise backed by operations.

## Failed Urgency

- fake pressure;
- panic language;
- unsupported "instant" claims;
- 24/7 claims without proof;
- urgent ad with slow contact path;
- urgency that attracts poor-fit demand.

## Review Questions

- Is urgency present in the query?
- Can the business support the promise?
- Does the landing support urgent action?
- Does mobile contact work quickly?
- Is the claim safer as "check availability"?

## Boundary

ORCA does not verify live availability or determine conversion impact. Human review is mandatory.
