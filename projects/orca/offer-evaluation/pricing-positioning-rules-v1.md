# Pricing Positioning Rules v1

## Purpose

Review whether pricing communication supports the offer without misleading users.

## Useful Pricing Positioning

- current price if real;
- "from" price with conditions;
- estimate after details;
- transparent variables;
- call or WhatsApp estimate logic;
- value explanation when price is not cheapest.

## Pricing Risks

- fake cheapness;
- outdated price;
- hidden conditions;
- unsupported discounts;
- price that conflicts with landing;
- low anchor that attracts poor-fit leads.

## Pricing Psychology Varies

Some users need price clarity before calling. Others need trust and response confidence first.

Do not assume the cheapest-looking offer is the strongest offer.

## Boundary

ORCA does not verify current pricing or predict price response. Human review is mandatory.
