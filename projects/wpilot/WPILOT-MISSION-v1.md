# WPilot Mission Charter v1

## Identity

WPilot is a Personal WordPress Operations Platform.

Owner:

Andrey / MetaCODE

Status:

Active

Maturity:

MVP Stage

Operational Model:

Human-Supervised

---

## Primary Mission

Provide operational control, inspection, change management, validation, and rollback capabilities for WordPress websites operated by the owner.

Primary targets:

* Personal websites
* Client websites
* Internal projects
* Website Factory execution projects
* Legacy WordPress installations

---

## Primary Goal

Enable management of WordPress websites through the MARS ecosystem.

MARS is the primary operating environment.

Cursor, AI agents, dashboards, APIs, and future interfaces are operational interfaces rather than the mission itself.

---

## Core Principles

* Human-supervised
* Backup-first
* Validation-first
* Rollback-capable
* Audit-friendly
* Operational-first
* Evidence-driven

---

## Non Goals

WPilot is NOT:

* Autonomous WordPress administrator
* Autonomous deployment platform
* Autonomous website manager
* Public SaaS by default
* Generic hosting control panel
* Replacement for Website Factory
* Replacement for MARS
* Autonomous AI runtime

---

---

## Ecosystem Relationships

WPilot ↔ Website Factory

Execution and operational support relationship.

WPilot ↔ ATLAS

Consumer of business and project context.

WPilot ↔ OCPilot

Sibling CMS Operations Platform.

Shared principles may exist, but platforms remain independent.

---

## Runtime Boundary

All actions require human approval.

WPilot does not possess autonomous authority over WordPress environments.

---

## Success Criteria

WPilot succeeds if it allows the owner to safely operate, inspect, modify, validate, and recover WordPress websites through MARS workflows.
