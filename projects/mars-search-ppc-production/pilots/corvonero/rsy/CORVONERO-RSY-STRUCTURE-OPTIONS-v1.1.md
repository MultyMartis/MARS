## Can different CorvoNero services share one campaign via groups?

**Planning answer: YES**, unless cabinet/moderation later shows a specific block.

Help: many groups with different targeting in one ЕПК; category example with group CPA adjustments; more groups → more learning data; same-domain ads do not compete on billed price.

CorvoNero: one business, one domain, five related 1C services, close conversion goals (request / call). That matches the official group model better than five day-one campaigns.

LOCAL vs REMOTE is the **stronger** split (different geo + visit promise), not “программист vs сопровождение”.
