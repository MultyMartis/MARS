# CORVONERO — RSY brand and moderation cautions v0.3

- BRAND_COLOR_STATUS: SAFE UNKNOWN
- LOGO_STATUS: NOT_PROVIDED / OPTIONAL_LATER
- LOGO_PLACEHOLDER_ONLY is allowed only as a layout placeholder, not as an invented final logo.
- Suggested neutral style: dark graphite / muted blue / clean light background / business technology style.
- Do not invent an exact brandbook.
- Do not request or use unavailable brand assets.
- Do not show logos of 1С, Yandex, Честный знак, ЦРПТ, clients, or government marks.
- Do not imply official partnership, certification, government endorsement, or exclusive status.
- Do not show fake certificates, fake compliance badges, fake UI screenshots, or fake documents.
- Do not use frightening cyberattack imagery, legal-drama style, medical style, crypto or gaming aesthetics.
- Keep the tone trustworthy, calm, commercially useful, and clearly B2B.
