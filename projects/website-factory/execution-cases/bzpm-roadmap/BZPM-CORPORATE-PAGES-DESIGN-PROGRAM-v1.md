## Corporate Pages Design Order (single sequence)

| Order | Page | ID | Rationale |
|-------|------|-----|-----------|
| **1** | About Company | M9.13 | Trust anchor for all corp pages; establishes hero/media, OEM trust row, cert promo, geography, and baseline internal-page rhythm. IA notes existing `/about` CSS scaffold on TEST — lowest rework risk for pattern lock-in. |
| **2** | Payment | M9.15 | Locks B2B commerce process visuals (step timeline, payment matrix, document checklist, entity trust row) reused by Delivery, Custom, Dealers. |
| **3** | Delivery | M9.14 | Completes transaction journey pair with Payment; shipment-point cards and regional coverage become shared proof patterns for Dealers strip and Warranty RMA summary. |
| **4** | Warranty | M9.17 | Service/claim process diagram and document pack reuse process + document components; critical catalog trust gap (PLP «Гарантия» link). |
| **5** | Dealers | M9.16 | Composes trust strips linking to About/Payment/Delivery/Warranty — must not be designed before those summaries exist. Partner matrix + application form inherit corp form system. |
| **6** | Custom Manufacturing | M9.18 | Longest page; parameter matrix, TZ checklist, file-upload form, and process timeline extend — not invent — patterns from steps 1–5. Highest unique asset dependency. |

**Why not Dealers or Warranty first?** IA flags them as commercial gaps, but design-wise they are **composition pages** that deep-link to other owners. Designing them before Payment/Delivery/Warranty risks duplicate trust UI or inconsistent summary strips.

**Why not Custom earlier?** Custom has the most page-specific blocks (parameter matrix, material grades, file upload) and the highest operator asset gap; deferring it maximizes shared-component reuse.

### M9.13 — About Company (`/about`)

#### 1. Purpose

Primary owner of **entity narrative** — who ЗПМ is, factory identity, production footprint, OEM trust. Anchors «кто вы?» from catalog and Commercial Trust.

#### 2. Audience

- Владелец бизнеса — trust before first deal  
- Снабженец / закупщик — vendor legitimacy
