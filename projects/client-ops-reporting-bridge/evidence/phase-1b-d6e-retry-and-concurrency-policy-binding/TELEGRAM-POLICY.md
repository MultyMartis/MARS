# TELEGRAM-POLICY

**Tokens:** `D6E_TELEGRAM_SUCCESS_PENDING_FAILS_CLOSED` · `D6E_TELEGRAM_FAILED_NO_AUTO_RETRY` · `D6E_TELEGRAM_UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_RECONCILIATION`

| Telegram | Ledger | Decision |
|----------|--------|----------|
| SUCCESS | PENDING | `UNSAFE_TO_RETRY` + no-send guard |
| UNKNOWN | PENDING | `RECONCILE_BEFORE_RETRY` |
| DEFINITE_FAILURE | FAILED | `FINAL_FAILURE` |
| SUCCESS (historical evidence) | PENDING | `UNSAFE_TO_RETRY` (`HISTORICAL_PENDING_BLIND_RETRY_PROHIBITED`) |

D6E never calls Telegram API.
