# MARS Website Factory - Commercial Density Governance

**Status:** **documented** - Website Factory commercial density governance and human-supervised landing-page pressure methodology only.  
**Not:** conversion optimizer, automatic scoring model, copy generator, SaaS UI framework, or universal density rule.

**Core principle:** commercial landing pages need operational pressure, not sterile polish.

**Related layers:** [content-density-governance.md](content-density-governance.md), [commercial-landing-pressure-model.md](commercial-landing-pressure-model.md), [strategic-intent-governance.md](strategic-intent-governance.md), [cta-philosophy-governance.md](cta-philosophy-governance.md), [design-system-intent-governance.md](design-system-intent-governance.md), [beautification-drift-governance.md](beautification-drift-governance.md).

---

## 1. Purpose

Commercial Density Governance protects landing pages from becoming visually clean but commercially weak.

It covers:

- commercial density;
- operational pressure;
- anti-sterile UI;
- industrial landing rhythm;
- conversion pressure;
- proof density;
- CTA rhythm;
- anti-SaaS contamination.

---

## 2. Canonical Definition

**Commercial density** is the amount and rhythm of offer, proof, operational credibility, urgency, and action pressure a page needs to feel trustworthy and conversion-capable.

It is not the same as content overload. Healthy commercial density has readable hierarchy, enough proof, clear CTA rhythm, and operational seriousness.

---

## 3. Density Dimensions

| Dimension | Healthy read | Drift read |
|-----------|--------------|------------|
| **Offer pressure** | The offer is clear and repeated at useful decision points. | The page feels vague, generic, or decorative. |
| **Proof density** | Proof appears before or near high-friction conversion asks. | Proof is too thin, too late, or wallpaper-like. |
| **CTA rhythm** | CTA pressure builds without shouting. | CTA is buried, spammed, or visually diluted. |
| **Operational pressure** | Page communicates real-world capability and seriousness. | Page feels like a SaaS template detached from operations. |
| **Industrial rhythm** | Density, weight, and pacing match the business category. | Landing rhythm becomes sterile, soft, or over-minimal. |

---

## 4. Canonical Rules

- Preserve commercial pressure when simplifying UI.
- Do not equate whitespace with trust.
- Do not reduce proof density to make a layout “cleaner” without strategic authority.
- Keep CTA rhythm tied to conversion hierarchy, not decorative symmetry.
- Distinguish healthy density from overload: density must remain readable.
- Record commercial density findings when polish, redesign, or reconstruction weakens conversion pressure.

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it is drift |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **Sterile UI drift** | Page becomes clean but commercially underpowered. |
| **SaaSification pressure** | Industrial/commercial landing feels like generic SaaS marketing. |
| **Proof thinning** | Trust evidence is removed or softened for visual neatness. |
| **CTA politeness drift** | Calls to action become too quiet for the page goal. |
| **Operational pressure loss** | Real business capability is replaced by abstract premium aesthetics. |
| **Density panic** | Useful commercial information is removed because the page “feels busy.” |

---

## 6. Drift Patterns

- **Commercial density collapse** - conversion/proof/action pressure falls below page intent.
- **Clean UI drift** - visual simplicity erodes commercial usefulness.
- **Anti-SaaS contamination failure** - industrial landing language is replaced by generic SaaS smoothness.
- **Proof density erosion** - trust rhythm no longer supports CTA asks.

---

## 7. Triumph V3 Lesson

Triumph V3 showed clean UI drift and SaaSification pressure: reconstruction can make a landing page prettier while weakening industrial commercial force.

The governance lesson is to preserve commercial density and operational pressure. It is not a mandate to overload pages.

---

## 8. SAFE UNKNOWN

Record **SAFE UNKNOWN** when:

| Situation | Why it is unknown |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Commercial intent is not documented | Cannot judge required density or CTA pressure. |
| Proof authority is missing | Cannot decide whether proof density is real or fabricated. |
| Source density conflicts with stakeholder preference | Requires HITL or strategy authority. |
| Industry tone is unclear | Cannot calibrate industrial vs SaaS-like rhythm. |
| CTA hierarchy is unresolved | Cannot evaluate conversion pressure. |

**Action:** tie density review to strategy, proof authority, and conversion hierarchy before reducing or increasing pressure.
