# MARS Website Factory - Contextual Footer Governance

**Status:** **documented** - Website Factory footer role and sizing methodology only.  
**Not:** footer generator, universal footer height rule, CMS navigation model, ecommerce IA engine, or automatic legal-content system.

**Core principle:** footer height depends on page role and context. Footer size is not an absolute quality signal.

**Related layers:** [canonical-vertical-cadence-system.md](canonical-vertical-cadence-system.md), [commercial-landing-pressure-model.md](commercial-landing-pressure-model.md), [strategic-intent-governance.md](strategic-intent-governance.md), [content-density-governance.md](content-density-governance.md), [production-readiness-governance.md](production-readiness-governance.md).  
**Forge findings category:** `FOOTER CONTEXT FINDINGS`.

---

## 1. Purpose

This layer prevents footer-context drift: treating every footer as if it must be tall, portal-like, SEO-heavy, or equal across page types.

It governs:

- contextual footer sizing;
- footer-role classification;
- landing footer compactness;
- portal / ecommerce footer expansion;
- final closure rhythm;
- footer contextual misuse;
- footer-height absolutism drift.

---

## 2. Footer Role Classification

| Footer role | Typical context | Healthy sizing |
|-------------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Landing closure footer** | Single conversion landing, service page, campaign page | Compact, trust-supporting, contact/legal/navigation only as needed. |
| **Operational contact footer** | Local service page with phone/messenger/contact importance | Medium density; contact and action remain prominent. |
| **Corporate footer** | Multi-page company site | Expanded navigation and legal/support groups are acceptable. |
| **Portal footer** | Content portal, directory, SaaS app marketing with many routes | Larger IA footprint may be required. |
| **Ecommerce footer** | Catalog/checkout ecosystem | Expanded service, payment, delivery, account and legal groups may be appropriate. |

The footer role must be classified before judging height.

---

## 3. Canonical Rules

- Footer height depends on page role/context.
- Landing footer may remain compact when the page already carries strong conversion closure.
- Portal/ecommerce footer may expand when navigation, support, policy, account, and catalog responsibilities require it.
- Do not expand a landing footer only because a generic website template expects more columns.
- Do not shrink a portal/ecommerce footer if it must carry real IA and support obligations.
- Footer terminal rhythm must close the page without suffocation or dead whitespace.
- Footer content must not invent legal, company, support, or navigation facts missing from source authority.

---

## 4. Footer Drift Lessons Captured

| Drift | Governance lesson |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Footer contextual misuse** | Footer role was judged without page type and conversion context. |
| **Footer-height absolutism drift** | Tall/short footer was treated as universally correct. |
| **Section-stack feeling** | Footer became another isolated block instead of page closure. |
| **Loss of commercial narrative continuity** | Footer disconnected from final CTA and proof flow. |

Required V4 lesson labels captured: `footer contextual misuse`, `footer-height absolutism drift`.

---

## 5. Landing Footer Guidance

For a commercial landing:

- keep the footer subordinate to final conversion;
- preserve phone/contact trust;
- avoid turning the footer into a portal map unless the source requires it;
- keep legal placeholders honest when verified data is unavailable;
- preserve final closure cadence.

Compact does not mean cramped. Compact means role-appropriate.

---

## 6. Forge Use

Record `FOOTER CONTEXT FINDINGS` when:

- footer height is judged without role classification;
- footer expands into portal/ecommerce behavior on a landing;
- footer is too compressed for contact/legal/trust scan;
- footer breaks commercial momentum after final CTA;
- footer content invents unsupported IA or legal details.

Footer context findings may accompany `CADENCE FINDINGS`, `LANDING PRESSURE FINDINGS`, `CONTENT DENSITY FINDINGS`, or `PRODUCTION READINESS FINDINGS`.

---

## 7. SAFE UNKNOWN

Record **SAFE UNKNOWN** when:

| Situation | Why it is unknown |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Site type is unclear | Cannot classify footer role. |
| Legal/company data is missing | Cannot verify footer legal content. |
| Navigation IA is incomplete | Cannot decide whether footer should expand. |
| Final CTA destination is unknown | Cannot judge closure continuity. |
| Source shows only partial footer | Cannot claim exact footer sizing. |

**Action:** classify footer role, list known footer obligations, and avoid absolute footer-height claims.
