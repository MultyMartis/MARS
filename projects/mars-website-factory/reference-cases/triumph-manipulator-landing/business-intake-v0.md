## 6. Constraints

- No **fake** addresses, reviews, awards, or “24/7 everywhere” claims without ops coverage.
- **Industrial tone** must not slide into **unsafe DIY** encouragement (manipulator work is professional-only).
- **SEO:** no doorway pages; no “near you” without real service area definition.

## 7. Commercial risks

| Risk | Mitigation direction |
|------|----------------------|
