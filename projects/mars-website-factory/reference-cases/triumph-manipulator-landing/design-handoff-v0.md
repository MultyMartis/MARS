# Design handoff — Triumph Manipulator Landing (v0)

**Contract SoT:** [Design Handoff Contract v0](../../design-handoff-contract-v0.md)  
**Input blueprint:** [page-blueprint-v0.md](page-blueprint-v0.md)

---

## 1. Visual direction (summary)

- **Industrial commercial** — confident, non-playful; high contrast; restrained photography of real equipment where available.
- **Trust-first** — badges readable at mobile width; no “startup gradient” cliché unless brand demands (**SAFE UNKNOWN** brand tokens).

---

## 2. Hierarchy

- **H1** visually dominant; one primary button style across hero, sticky, final.
- **Section rhythm:** alternate **light/dark** or **surface/tint** bands for scan anchors (exact tokens **SAFE UNKNOWN**).

---

## 3. Spacing

- Generous **vertical rhythm** between **process_steps** and **cases** to avoid “wall of proof.”
- Form section breathing room — error messages must not clip submit on mobile.

---

## 4. Typography

- **Headline:** sturdy grotesk or industrial sans (**no** final font files in this pack).
- **Body:** high readability, **≥16px** base on mobile intent.
- **FAQ:** question line stronger than answer for scan.

---

## 5. Visual trust

- **trust_block:** monochrome or duotone badge treatment for consistency.
- **geo_trust:** map style **minimal** — clarity over decoration; **no** fake pins.

---

## 6. Industrial tone

- Imagery: real yard / street context preferred; avoid cartoon cranes.
- Iconography: simple line icons for **process_steps** if photos thin.

---

## 7. Mobile priorities

- Hero: CTA visible without scroll in common **375px** height (allowing dynamic toolbars — QA note).
- **sticky_cta:** does not cover form inputs or keyboard.
