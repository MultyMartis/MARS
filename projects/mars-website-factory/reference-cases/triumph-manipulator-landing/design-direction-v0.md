# Design direction — Triumph Manipulator Landing (v0)

**Explicit:** This is **directional** guidance for exploration — **not** final design, **not** signed-off mockups.

---

## 1. Style direction

- **“Grounded industrial”** — concrete greys, safety yellow **only** as accent (if brand permits), deep blue or charcoal primary **TBD** with brand.
- Photography-led hero **if** assets exist; otherwise typography-led hero with subtle texture.

---

## 2. Tone

- **Direct, calm, competent** — short sentences; avoid hype.
- **B2B/B2C overlap:** one neutral voice; FAQ handles split scenarios.

---

## 3. Visual references (classes, not URLs)

- Commercial equipment rental sites with **clear** service hierarchy (reference class only).
- **Avoid:** consumer e-commerce flash patterns, gamified icons.

---

## 4. Trust posture

- **Show, don’t claim:** machine in context, crew PPE, site cones — **when** photo rights exist.
- **Numbers:** only with footnote or “representative” labeling if not statistically audited.

---

## 5. Interaction style

- **FAQ:** accordion with one-open vs multi-open — **decision deferred** to UX; default one-open for clarity.
- **Form:** inline validation; no blocking modal on first field focus.

---

## 6. Motion philosophy

- **Subtle** — 150–250ms fades on section enter **optional**; **no** auto-play video with sound.
- **Respect** `prefers-reduced-motion` — mandatory in frontend handoff.

---
