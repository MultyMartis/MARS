# Marketing strategy — Triumph Manipulator Landing (v0)

**Not:** performance guarantees, A/B test results, or post-launch metrics.

---

## 1. Positioning

**Core idea:** **Dependable commercial manipulator operations** — safety-first crew, correct equipment class for the job, transparent **next step** (survey / quote), industrial tone without hostility.

**Differentiation axes (message pillars — evidence-bound):**

1. **Operational reliability** — show up window, communication, site discipline (copy only; **SAFE UNKNOWN** for real KPIs).
2. **Right tool for load** — educate lightly on capacity / reach (no spec fiction).
3. **Risk reduction** — insurance, method statements, **where allowed** — legal review gate.

---

## 2. CTA logic

| Level | Action | Rules |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Primary** | Quote / callback request | One label family sitewide on URL |
| **Secondary** | `tel:` / messenger | Visually and semantically secondary; repeat not as competing primary |
| **Deferred** | Download brochure | **Optional** — only if asset exists |

**Sticky mobile:** After first scroll, **same primary intent** as [Block Registry v0](../../block-registry-v0.md) `sticky_cta` guidance — not a second offer.

---

## 3. Trust strategy

**Order of proof (recommended):**

1. Light trust in **hero** (one line, no logo soup).
2. **`trust_block`** — certifications / membership / **verifiable** badges only.
3. **`cases`** — anonymized or permissioned project snapshots; no stock photo of unrelated cranes.
4. **`reviews`** — **omit** until verifiable widget or legal-approved quotes.

---

## 4. Urgency policy

- **Default:** **no** countdown timers or “only 2 slots left” unless tied to **provable** live capacity (ops feed — **SAFE UNKNOWN**).
- Acceptable: “We schedule same-week visits when capacity allows” — **conditional** HITL if interpreted as promise.

---

## 5. Commercial hierarchy

1. **Clarify service fit** (what manipulator work is / isn’t).
2. **Reduce fear** (damage, downtime, safety).
3. **Make next step obvious** (primary CTA).
4. **FAQ** handles price anxiety without fake precision.

---

## 6. Conversion friction reduction

- Short **`lead_form`** — name, contact, city/district, brief job description, preferred time — fields adjustable after CRM decision (**SAFE UNKNOWN**).
- Reassurance microcopy adjacent to submit — consent/privacy link mandatory if GDPR-like regime applies (**SAFE UNKNOWN** jurisdiction).

---

## 7. What we are not claiming

- No “#1 in region” without third-party proof.
- No guaranteed job duration without estimator sign-off.
- No **automatic** lead scoring or “AI will book you” narrative.
