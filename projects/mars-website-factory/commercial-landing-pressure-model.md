# MARS Website Factory - Commercial Landing Pressure Model

**Status:** **documented** - Website Factory commercial landing pressure model for human-supervised conversion rhythm review.  
**Not:** CRO engine, scoring model, analytics system, copy optimizer, urgency generator, or universal landing-page formula.

**Core principle:** landing pages need momentum continuity: trust, proof, CTA, urgency, and readability should pace each other.

**Related layers:** [commercial-density-governance.md](commercial-density-governance.md), [content-density-governance.md](content-density-governance.md), [strategic-intent-governance.md](strategic-intent-governance.md), [cta-philosophy-governance.md](cta-philosophy-governance.md), [section-language-governance.md](section-language-governance.md), [atmosphere-continuity-governance.md](atmosphere-continuity-governance.md).  
**Forge findings category:** `LANDING PRESSURE FINDINGS`.

---

## 1. Purpose

This model gives Forge a bounded vocabulary for commercial landing momentum without pretending to optimize conversion automatically.

It covers:

- conversion pacing;
- operational trust rhythm;
- CTA pressure rhythm;
- proof pacing;
- urgency density;
- commercial readability;
- pressure survivability;
- landing momentum continuity.

---

## 2. Pressure Layers

| Layer | Healthy pressure | Drift pressure |
|-------|------------------|----------------|
| **Conversion pacing** | User sees action opportunities after enough context and proof. | CTA appears too early, too late, too often, or too weakly. |
| **Operational trust rhythm** | Capability, process, proof, and reliability surface before high-friction asks. | Page asks for action before trust is earned. |
| **CTA pressure rhythm** | Primary action remains recognizable across the journey. | CTA labels, weight, or placement fragment. |
| **Proof pacing** | Evidence arrives near claims and decisions. | Proof is bunched, decorative, delayed, or absent. |
| **Urgency density** | Urgency is restrained, specific, and credible. | Fake urgency, pressure spam, or no urgency where market context needs it. |
| **Commercial readability** | Pressure remains scan-friendly and actionable. | Pressure becomes noisy, sterile, or buried. |

---

## 3. Momentum Model

A landing page has momentum when:

1. the first screen establishes offer, audience, and primary action;
2. early proof reduces skepticism before deeper claims;
3. mid-page sections build operational trust and specificity;
4. CTAs repeat with consistent meaning and increasing confidence;
5. urgency or scarcity is evidence-backed and restrained;
6. final conversion feels like a logical next step, not a sudden demand.

---

## 4. Canonical Rules

- Do not evaluate CTA visibility without proof and trust pacing.
- Do not add urgency without evidence or commercial authority.
- Do not reduce CTA pressure for aesthetic symmetry when conversion intent is high.
- Do not treat commercial readability as low density; readable pressure may still be dense.
- Check landing pressure after first-screen decomposition, because header/hero/CTA confusion can distort momentum.
- Record landing pressure findings when conversion pacing, trust rhythm, proof pacing, or CTA rhythm changes.

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it is drift |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **CTA island** | The CTA has no surrounding proof, trust, or context. |
| **Proof dump** | Evidence appears as a wall instead of paced support. |
| **Urgency theater** | Scarcity or urgency is invented or generic. |
| **Momentum reset** | Each section restarts the pitch instead of advancing it. |
| **Conversion politeness** | CTAs become too subdued for the landing goal. |
| **Pressure spam** | Every section shouts, reducing trust and readability. |

---

## 6. Drift Patterns

- **Landing momentum discontinuity** - page pressure resets or collapses between sections.
- **CTA pressure rhythm drift** - actions lose consistent meaning or visual priority.
- **Operational trust rhythm drift** - trust evidence no longer supports conversion asks.
- **Proof pacing drift** - evidence is not located near claims or friction points.

---

## 7. Triumph V3 Lesson

Triumph V3 showed that first-screen ambiguity can damage landing momentum: if header, hero, background, and conversion layer are merged, CTA pressure and proof rhythm become accidental.

The governance lesson is pressure pacing. It is not a conversion performance claim.

---

## 8. SAFE UNKNOWN

Record **SAFE UNKNOWN** when:

| Situation | Why it is unknown |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Conversion goal is missing | Cannot judge pressure rhythm. |
| Proof sources are unclear | Cannot validate proof pacing or urgency. |
| CTA destination is unknown | Cannot evaluate conversion continuity. |
| Stakeholder risk tolerance is unknown | Cannot calibrate urgency density. |
| Source only shows one screen | Cannot judge landing momentum continuity. |

**Action:** separate visible CTA quality from full landing pressure confidence.
