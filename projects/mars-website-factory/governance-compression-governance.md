# MARS Website Factory - Governance Compression & Operational Modes

**Status:** **documented** - Website Factory governance compression discipline and human-supervised operational-mode methodology only.  
**Not:** autonomous governance scaling AI, runtime governance orchestrator, universal operational mode system, automatic QA-depth selector, perfect deployability guarantee, or permission to disable necessary QA.

**Core principle:** frontend governance systems must preserve **operational deployability, scalable governance depth, mode-based survivability, governance compression integrity, and adaptive operational intensity**.  
They are not improved merely by "using fewer rules," "simplifying governance," "making reports shorter," or "disabling QA."

## 1. Positioning

Governance Compression & Operational Modes exists because Website Factory governance now covers prioritization, economics, cognitive survivability, adaptive governance, workflow discipline, meta-governance integrity, trust calibration, escalation governance, organizational memory, and many frontend intent layers.

That coverage is valuable only when it can be deployed at sustainable operational density.

| This layer governs | This layer does not govern |
|--------------------|----------------------------|
| Governance compression, operational modes, deployability scaling, mode transition integrity, compression readability, and governance portability | Universal operational modes, automated governance scaling, hidden runtime orchestration, or perfect deployability |
| Human-supervised discipline for choosing lite, standard, elevated, critical, freeze-validation, audit/reconstruction, or recovery/emergency operation | Disabling QA, flattening specialist layers, or treating compressed governance as lower quality by default |
| Drift vocabulary for deployment overload, one-mode governance, compression survivability failure, review-mode mismatch, deployment fatigue, and operational scaling collapse | Redesigning Triumph, creating an autonomous process selector, or replacing adjacent governance layers |
| Forge reporting discipline for `GOVERNANCE COMPRESSION FINDINGS` | A claim that all projects share the same governance density or mode thresholds |

The governance question is not "can the system contain strong methodology?"  
The governance question is: **can that methodology be deployed at the right operational intensity without collapsing under its own weight?**

## 2. Canonical Definition

**Governance compression** is the discipline of preserving essential governance signal, survivability, evidence boundaries, escalation triggers, and future readability while reducing operational density to a deployable form.

**Operational modes** are human-supervised intensity settings that determine how much governance depth, evidence, review, escalation, reporting, and continuity protection a task needs in its current context.

This layer protects:

- **Operational deployability** - governance can be used during real frontend work, not only in ideal critical reviews.
- **Scalable governance depth** - governance expands and contracts by risk, uncertainty, reversibility, freeze state, and delivery consequence.
- **Mode-based survivability** - each mode preserves enough evidence, escalation, and continuity for its risk level.
- **Compression integrity** - shortened or lighter governance does not erase material risks, proof boundaries, source authority, or unresolved unknowns.
- **Adaptive operational intensity** - routine work stays usable, while critical work can inherit deeper rigor.
- **Governance portability** - the method can be carried across sections, sessions, and projects without requiring private context or maximum-density reports.
- **Deployability survivability** - governance remains repeatable over long projects, handoffs, compressed context, and operator fatigue.

A governance system may contain strong methodology, preserve excellent survivability, provide extensive QA, and maintain operational rigor while still being too heavy for deployment, exhausting routine usage, or becoming practical only in critical scenarios.

## 5. Governance Compression Method

Before compressing governance, identify:

| Question | Compression meaning |
|----------|---------------------|
| What is the current operational mode? | Lite, operational-standard, elevated-review, critical, freeze-validation, audit/reconstruction, or recovery/emergency. |
| What must survive compression? | Source authority, critical findings, QA evidence, escalation triggers, freeze state, mode decision, and SAFE UNKNOWN. |
| What may be shortened? | Repeated background, duplicate findings, low-risk detail, adjacent-layer explanations, or already-linked methodology. |
| What may not be compressed away? | Material risk, unresolved contradiction, human-owned decisions, missing evidence, source ambiguity, or delivery blockers. |
| What would make the mode too heavy? | Mandatory full-depth reporting, duplicated checklists, permanent critical-mode inheritance, or operational density collapse. |
| What would make the mode too light? | Hidden unknowns, absent evidence, under-protected freeze/delivery risk, or missing escalation. |

**Rule:** compression is valid only when a future operator can still understand the mode, evidence, risk, escalation posture, and next safe action.

## 6. Anti-Patterns

Forbidden drift:

| Anti-pattern | Why it is forbidden |
|--------------|---------------------|
| **One-mode governance** | Treats routine fixes, elevated review, freeze validation, and recovery as the same operational condition. |
| **Permanent critical-mode operation** | Exhausts operators and makes critical rigor unusable when it is truly needed. |
| **Deployment-hostile governance** | Methodology is strong but too dense to deploy in real sessions. |
| **Unusable governance density** | Checklists and reports become technically complete but operationally unreadable. |
| **Endless operational rigor** | Every concern inherits maximum ceremony regardless of current risk. |
| **Survivability obsession everywhere** | Continuity protection expands until routine execution collapses. |
| **Compression without integrity** | Reports become shorter by hiding evidence, risk, or unresolved decisions. |
| **Operational rigidity** | Governance refuses to shift modes when task context changes. |
| **Review inflation inheritance** | Elevated or critical review depth becomes the default for later low-risk work. |
| **"Maximum governance always safer"** | Mistakes process density for deployability, confidence, or operational quality. |

Use [compression-drift-taxonomy.md](compression-drift-taxonomy.md) for full drift classification.

## 8. Triumph V2 Lessons Captured

Triumph V2 exposed reusable Website Factory governance-compression lessons:

- A V2 rebuild can require deep source, visual, responsive, QA, escalation, workflow, continuity, and memory governance while still needing compressed operational reporting to remain deployable.
- Full critical-mode rigor is valuable for source/freeze/delivery risks, but it can exhaust routine section work if inherited everywhere.
- Missing mobile source, source authority ambiguity, freeze uncertainty, and recovery state should escalate operational mode rather than force universal maximum governance.
- Long reports can preserve evidence but still reduce operational readability if they do not compress around mode, risk, and next action.
- Governance minimalism, economics, cognitive-load, and adaptive governance reduce parts of the problem; this layer adds explicit mode survivability and compression integrity.
- A section can be visually stable and procedurally reviewed while still failing deployability because governance density makes the path impractical to repeat.

These are Website Factory governance lessons, not Triumph redesign instructions.

## 9. SAFE UNKNOWN

Record **SAFE UNKNOWN** when:

| Situation | Why it is unknown |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Operational mode cannot be justified | Cannot prove whether lite, standard, elevated, critical, freeze, audit, or recovery depth fits. |
| Compression may hide material risk | Cannot safely shorten without losing proof boundary, escalation trigger, or unresolved unknown. |
| Governance density may be undeployable | Cannot prove the method can be applied sustainably in real sessions. |
| Critical-mode inheritance is unclear | Cannot tell whether high rigor remains necessary or has become drift. |
| Mode transition is ambiguous | Cannot prove why governance scaled up, scaled down, froze, audited, or recovered. |
| Portability is unproven | Cannot claim another operator or project can reuse the compressed governance path. |
| Deployability vs survivability balance is unresolved | Cannot decide whether to preserve more detail or compress for usability. |

**Action:** state the missing mode/compression evidence, identify what must survive compression, classify the provisional mode, and choose: keep density, compress, scale up, scale down, freeze-validate, audit/reconstruct, recover, HITL required, or SAFE UNKNOWN.
