## Core thesis

**SEO ≠ only keywords.** v0 treats SEO intent as **query demand alignment**, **honest on-page substance**, **internal link ethics**, and **schema truthfulness** — coordinated with **commercial_intent** and **trust_strategy**.

---

## Intent dimensions

| Dimension | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Acquisition intent** | What **new** visits this URL should earn (category, problem-led, brand discovery). |
| **Commercial intent** | Degree to which SERP expectations include **transaction** or **comparison** — must match on-page offer. |
| **Local intent** | Geo-modified or “near me” demand; pairs with **local_trust** surfaces. |
| **Informational intent** | Teach/explain demand; page must deliver depth, not thin bridge. |
| **Entity intent** | Disambiguate organization/product/person for search and **citation** surfaces. |
| **AI visibility intent** | Supply **checkable** facts for retrieval/citation contexts — **not** control of third-party assistants ([page-blueprint-contract-v0.md](page-blueprint-contract-v0.md) AI visibility example). |

---

## Friction and readiness (conceptual)

| Concept | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Conversion friction** | SEO-driven traffic may arrive **information-first**; aggressive CTA without proof raises **Conversion QA** flags. |
| **Trust friction** | High YMYL queries need stronger **expertise_trust** / **compliance_trust** before commercial modules dominate. |
| **CTA readiness** | User’s implied task stage vs CTA offered; mismatch → blueprint revision. |
| **Page maturity** | Rough lifecycle: `hypothesis` → `draft` → `substantive` → `approved_for_indexing` (labels **narrative only**). |
| **Escalation triggers** | Doorway patterns, duplicate geo, fake FAQ schema, **AggregateRating** without eligible reviews. |
