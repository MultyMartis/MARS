**Status:** **documented** - Website Factory atmosphere continuity governance and human-supervised environmental rhythm methodology only.  
**Not:** mood generator, visual renderer, automatic theme engine, or universal dark/light cadence system.

**Core principle:** atmosphere is continuity, not isolated decoration.

## 1. Purpose

Atmosphere Continuity Governance protects the environmental rhythm of a landing page across sections, backgrounds, density changes, dark/light cadence, and emotional pressure.

It covers:

- atmosphere continuity;
- environmental rhythm;
- emotional pressure continuity;
- visual heaviness continuity;
- dark/light cadence;
- atmosphere survivability;
- environmental fragmentation drift.

---

## 2. Canonical Definition

**Atmosphere continuity** is the page-level consistency of mood, weight, visual pressure, and environment as users move from first screen to proof, offer, details, and conversion.

Atmosphere may change, but changes should read as pacing decisions, not accidental fragmentation.

---

## 3. Continuity Dimensions

| Dimension | Governance read |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Environmental rhythm** | Backgrounds, surfaces, and density transitions form a coherent journey. |
| **Emotional pressure** | Seriousness, urgency, trust, and industrial force do not randomly spike or disappear. |
| **Visual heaviness** | Heavy/dark/dense regions and lighter breathing regions are intentionally paced. |
| **Dark/light cadence** | Contrast changes support narrative rhythm rather than section accidents. |
| **Atmosphere survivability** | The mood survives responsive collapse, asset substitution, and scoped fixes. |

---

## 4. Canonical Rules

- Treat atmosphere as a cross-section concern when backgrounds, lighting, or density change.
- Do not let a single reconstructed section break page mood without reporting it.
- Do not flatten atmosphere into sterile UI polish.
- Preserve emotional pressure where the source or commercial intent requires it.
- Check mobile atmosphere: collapse can fragment mood as much as desktop section splits.
- Record atmosphere findings when environmental rhythm or visual heaviness changes materially.

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it is drift |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **Atmospheric fragmentation** | Sections feel like unrelated templates. |
| **Mood reset by accident** | Dark/light or heavy/light transitions occur without narrative reason. |
| **Sterile atmosphere washout** | Polish removes emotional pressure and industrial force. |
| **Hero-only atmosphere** | Mood exists in the hero and disappears immediately after. |
| **Responsive mood collapse** | Mobile stacking destroys atmosphere continuity. |
| **Background-as-mood simplification** | Atmosphere is reduced to one image or gradient. |

---

## 6. Drift Patterns

- **Environmental fragmentation drift** - page sections no longer share a coherent environment.
- **Emotional pressure discontinuity** - urgency, seriousness, or trust appears and disappears unpredictably.
- **Visual heaviness drift** - weight distribution stops matching source or business intent.
- **Dark/light cadence drift** - contrast changes become arbitrary.

---

## 7. Triumph V3 Lesson

Triumph V3 exposed atmospheric fragmentation risk: first-screen and downstream sections can each look acceptable while the page loses environmental continuity.

The governance lesson is to review atmosphere as a survivable page rhythm. It is not a mandate for one continuous background.

---

## 8. SAFE UNKNOWN

Record **SAFE UNKNOWN** when:

| Situation | Why it is unknown |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Source does not show adjacent sections | Cannot judge atmosphere continuity. |
| Background ownership is unclear | Cannot tell whether atmosphere is shell, hero, or section-owned. |
| Mobile source is missing | Cannot prove mood survives collapse. |
| Asset substitution changes mood | Requires asset lifecycle and source authority review. |
| Commercial tone is unclear | Cannot calibrate emotional pressure. |

**Action:** preserve known atmosphere, disclose unknown continuity boundaries, and avoid claiming full atmospheric parity.
