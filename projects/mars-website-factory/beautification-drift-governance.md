# MARS Website Factory - Beautification Drift Governance

**Status:** **documented** - Website Factory beautification drift governance and human-supervised source-intent protection only.  
**Not:** aesthetic scoring system, design police, automatic modernization blocker, or universal visual taste law.

**Core principle:** making a page prettier can still make it less correct.

**Related layers:** [design-system-intent-governance.md](design-system-intent-governance.md), [commercial-density-governance.md](commercial-density-governance.md), [section-language-governance.md](section-language-governance.md), [atmosphere-continuity-governance.md](atmosphere-continuity-governance.md), [source-interpretation-governance.md](source-interpretation-governance.md), [reconstruction-fidelity-model.md](reconstruction-fidelity-model.md).  
**Forge findings category:** `BEAUTIFICATION DRIFT FINDINGS`.

---

## 1. Purpose

Beautification Drift Governance protects source intent, commercial force, industrial tone, and reconstruction fidelity from aesthetic overreach.

It covers:

- beautification drift;
- SaaSification;
- fake premiumization;
- aesthetic overreach;
- visual sterilization;
- unnecessary modernization;
- decorative drift;
- source-intent erosion through beautification.

---

## 2. Canonical Definition

**Beautification drift** happens when a change improves perceived polish while eroding source intent, business tone, commercial density, atmosphere, hierarchy, or operational credibility.

It is not a ban on quality improvement. It is a boundary against taste replacing authority.

---

## 3. Drift Taxonomy

| Drift type | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **SaaSification** | Industrial, operational, or commercial identity becomes generic SaaS UI. |
| **Fake premiumization** | More whitespace, softness, gradients, or smoothness are treated as higher quality without source support. |
| **Aesthetic overreach** | Local design taste changes structure, hierarchy, copy pressure, or atmosphere. |
| **Visual sterilization** | Useful messiness, proof density, or operational pressure is removed for neatness. |
| **Unnecessary modernization** | Old-but-intentional language is replaced by trendy conventions. |
| **Decorative drift** | Ornament, motion, icons, or effects distract from source and conversion purpose. |

---

## 4. Canonical Rules

- Do not improve visual polish by weakening source intent.
- Do not treat “modern” as a substitute for authority.
- Do not soften industrial or operational pages into generic SaaS aesthetics without strategy approval.
- Do not remove density, proof, heaviness, or friction cues solely because they look less clean.
- Record beautification findings when a change looks better but may be less faithful, less commercial, or less operationally credible.

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it is drift |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **Pretty but wrong** | Visual appeal hides source, hierarchy, or business mismatch. |
| **Clean UI worship** | Neatness overrides conversion pressure and proof density. |
| **Premium by blur/gradient** | Decorative effects replace real trust or operational seriousness. |
| **Modernization by taste** | Current trends overwrite project identity. |
| **Softening the category** | Heavy industrial/commercial tone becomes polite SaaS smoothness. |
| **Decoration as reconstruction** | Added visual flourishes are mistaken for source fidelity. |

---

## 6. Drift Patterns

- **Source-intent erosion through beautification** - source meaning changes under polish.
- **Clean UI drift** - operational pressure and proof density are washed out.
- **SaaSification pressure** - page tone migrates toward generic SaaS conventions.
- **Decorative authority drift** - decoration starts dictating hierarchy or structure.

---

## 7. Triumph V3 Lesson

Triumph V3 showed that “better-looking” reconstruction can still damage the landing page if it sterilizes atmosphere, commercial density, or industrial credibility.

The governance lesson is to review beautification as a drift vector. It is not an instruction to preserve poor execution.

---

## 8. SAFE UNKNOWN

Record **SAFE UNKNOWN** when:

| Situation | Why it is unknown |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Source design quality is unclear | Cannot tell improvement from drift. |
| Stakeholder intent conflicts with source | Requires HITL or strategy authority. |
| Category tone is undocumented | Cannot calibrate industrial vs SaaS-like aesthetics. |
| Polish changes proof or CTA pressure | Cannot judge without commercial-density review. |
| Modernization affects identity | Requires source lineage and intent review. |

**Action:** separate execution correction from aesthetic replacement, then seek authority for material visual reinterpretation.
