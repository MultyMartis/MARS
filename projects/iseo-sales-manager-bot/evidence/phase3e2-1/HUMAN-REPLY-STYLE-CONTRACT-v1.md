# HUMAN REPLY STYLE CONTRACT v1

**Versions:** `first_reply_version=sm-reply-v2.1` · `human_reply_style_version=sm-human-v1.0` · parser remains `sm-parser-v3.3` · card `sm-msg-v2.4` unless structure changes.

## Purpose

Produce drafts Оля can send with minimal editing — natural attentive-employee Russian, not parser narration.

## Principles (summary)

1. Speak to the customer, not about the parser.
2. Acknowledge the concrete request.
3. Known-information suppression is **silent**.
4. Ask only useful next questions (≤3 groups).
5. Prefer natural sentences over system labels.
6. No canned «мы учли ваш комментарий».
7. No statements explaining missing/present fields.
8. Do not mechanically repeat the customer.
9. Not a diagnostic report.
10. Do not over-formalize simple requests.
11. Professional warmth.
12. No excessive emojis in customer draft.
13. No fake personalization.
14. No unsupported promises / dates / auto-contact claims.
15. Easy to edit.
