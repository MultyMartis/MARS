# HomeGateway v4.ai — light theme tactical environment study v0.1

**Статус:** **DRAFT** · **PLANNING** · Lane B  
**Назначение:** **светлая тема как тактическая среда** — operational, premium, layered — без CRM / enterprise admin / sterile SaaS.

**Не является:** light theme token freeze, WCAG audit report, implementation CSS.

**Связанные:** [color-behavior-and-accent-philosophy-v0.1.md](color-behavior-and-accent-philosophy-v0.1.md) · [theme-system-draft-v0.1.md](theme-system-draft-v0.1.md) · [background-and-environment-philosophy-v0.1.md](background-and-environment-philosophy-v0.1.md)

---

## VERY IMPORTANT — light theme problem statement

Most light UIs drift to:

| Drift | Symptom |
|-------|---------|
| **CRM feeling** | Pipeline columns, pastel cards, sales vibes |
| **Enterprise admin** | Gray tables, breadcrumb density, clerk mode |
| **Sterile SaaS** | Flat white + Inter + blue links only |

HG light theme must be: **tactical daylight** — same cockpit identity, different illumination.

---

## Light theme tactical goals

| Goal | Means |
|------|-------|
| **Operational** | Instruments readable; signal ladder intact |
| **Premium** | Layered whites; material borders — not flat |
| **Tactical** | Peripheral rail calm; desaturated signals |
| **Layered** | 2–3 environment planes — not single #FFF |

---

## WITHOUT (forbidden light atmosphere)

- Pure flat white void
- Warm beige consumer app
- Colorful widget headers
- Heavy shadows mimicking Material marketing
- Loss of spatial tri-focus
- Neon accents «because dark is gone»

---

## Explore

| Direction | Guideline |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Cold neutrals** | Blue-gray family — not warm gray SaaS |
| **Layered whites** | Off-white steps; subtle cool gradients |
| **Soft tactical light** | Top-down wash; corner softness |
| **Architectural brightness** | Geometry at 2–5% on light base |
| **Desaturated signals** | Badges readable — not fluorescent |

Dark remains **primary immersive**; light is **alternate operational clarity** ([color-behavior-and-accent-philosophy-v0.1.md](color-behavior-and-accent-philosophy-v0.1.md)).
