## Decision

| Field | Value |

| **Outcome** | **PASS WITH NOTES** |

| **Explicitly not passed by this decision** | Generator implementation; HandoffContract code; Identity Continuity Record encoding; production `snapshot_id` algorithm code; section population; persistence |

---

## Rationale

1. **R2 continuity preserved** — `acquisition_id` survival, `site_ref` → `site_id` transform, and `snapshot_id` absence on evidence documented with traceability chain matching R2.6.
2. **`snapshot_id` policy complete** — who/when/where/reference rules formalized; immutability and mock/production boundary aligned with R1.8B G-05.
3. **Transform matrix authoritative** — field-by-field survives/transforms/created/forbidden classification sufficient for R3.3 section rules and R3.5 HandoffContract.
4. **Drift prevention extended** — ID-R3-01–15 cover evidence/snapshot namespace separation, bulk_root aliasing, mock id boundary, and continuity record scope.
5. **R3.1 model sufficient** — `SnapshotIdentity` requires no structural change; future items scoped to R3.5/R3.6 only.

**PASS WITH NOTES** (not bare **PASS**): no runtime identity generator; production date anchor and sequence counter storage remain **SAFE UNKNOWN**; Identity Continuity Record physical encoding deferred to R3.5; mock pipeline `acquisition_id` convergence still open until R3.5.

**FAIL** would apply if contract allowed `snapshot_id` on evidence, omitted `acquisition_id` continuity, or assigned quality/publish ownership to identity layer — **none apply**.
