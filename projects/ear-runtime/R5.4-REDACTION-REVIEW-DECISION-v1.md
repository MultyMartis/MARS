## Rationale

1. **Seven categories chartered** — credentials, secrets, pii, internal_paths, unsafe_publication, quarantine_leakage, other registered in `CANONICAL_REDACTION_CATEGORY_REGISTRY` (SC-R5.4-01 scope extended per mission spec).
2. **Three review purposes covered** — secret detection (`secrets`), credential exposure (`credentials`), unsafe publication (`unsafe_publication`) — plus complementary categories for PII, paths, quarantine leakage (SC-R5.4-01).
3. **Fail-closed stated** — `BLOCKED` status and `NOT_PUBLISHABLE` recommendation documented; enforcement deferred R5.7 (SC-R5.4-02).
4. **R3 boundary clean** — VB-R3-12 documented; R3 copy-avoidance ≠ R5 redaction enforcement (SC-R5.4-03).
5. **Scan depth SAFE UNKNOWN** — operator policy noted (SC-R5.4-04).
6. **Ownership explicit** — R5 owns publication safety review; excludes quarantine management, Publish, secret storage, IR.
7. **Alignment confirmed** — no overlap with Quality, Possession, or Readiness categories; distinct from `ValidationStatus` and `ValidationRecommendation`.
8. **Forbidden scope absent** — no scanners, regex libraries, detection, validation rules, engine, or Publish in module.

**PASS WITH NOTES** (not bare **PASS**): `RedactionReview` → `ValidationResult` integration deferred R5.5/R5.7; `RedactionStatus` → `ValidationSeverity` mapping **SAFE UNKNOWN** until R5.7; scan depth operator policy **SAFE UNKNOWN**.

**FAIL** would apply if model included scanners, stored sensitive values in findings, omitted R3/R5 boundary, or modified R2/R3 contracts — **none apply**.
