## Rationale

1. **Handoff contract implemented** — `handoff_contract.py` provides eligibility, identity continuity, and safe-unknown constants per R2.6/R3.2/R3.4.
2. **Identity continuity preserved** — `acquisition_id` survives; `site_ref` → `site_id`; mock `snapshot_id` created at R3 boundary only.
3. **Honesty discipline** — L1/L2 sections empty with corresponding safe-unknown entries; no fabricated payloads; `package_quality_level: 0`.
4. **R3 validation boundary respected** — Structural checks only; no R5 quality or publish ownership.
5. **CLI demonstrable** — `--contract-snapshot` returns snapshot_id, acquisition_id, site_id, safe_unknown_count, validation PASS.

**PASS WITH NOTES** (not bare **PASS**): no quarantine bulk expansion; production snapshot_id SAFE UNKNOWN; R1.6 mock snapshot path preserved; Identity Continuity Record sidecar encoding deferred.

**FAIL** would apply if generator wrote to disk, certified quality, or mutated evidence identity — **none apply**.
