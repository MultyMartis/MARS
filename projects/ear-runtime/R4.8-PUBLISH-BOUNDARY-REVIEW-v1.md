## Purpose

Perform a **full architectural boundary audit** of R4 — EAR Publish Layer — before R4.9 Readiness Review. Verify that R4 engineering artefacts (R4 Charter through R4.7 Publish Engine architecture) did **not** absorb responsibilities from **R2** (Evidence), **R3** (Snapshot Assembly), or **R5** (EAR Validate), and that no hidden ownership conflicts exist with consumer programs or Store lifecycle.

**Explicit exclusions:** Publish Engine code, `ear_publish_engine.py`, CLI, Store adapter, persistence, architecture redesign.
