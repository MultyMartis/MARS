---

## Rationale

1. **Handoff rules integrated** — all HO-ALLOW-01–10 mapped to concrete sections; HO-FORBID-01–15 reviewed with R3 applicability; eight ambiguities documented with resolutions or SAFE UNKNOWN delegation.
2. **Complete section matrix** — every normative OpenCart section (`metadata/` through `acquisition-log/`) has allowed inputs, forbidden inputs, expected result, and fallback behavior.
3. **Quality levels aligned** — L0–L3 classification matrix matches EAR-OPENCART-QUALITY-MAPPING-v1 and EAR-OPENCART-SNAPSHOT-SPEC-v1; no invented levels.
4. **Assembly rules sufficient** — AR-R3-01–25 cover identity continuity, honesty, quality boundary, Validate/Publish independence, and L1 vs L2 scope.
5. **Ownership clean** — no collisions between R2/R3/R5/R4 on evidence shape, assembly, quality, or publish.
6. **R3.1 model sufficient** — all sections populatable; future enrichment scoped to R3.5 parsers and optional L2+ fields.

**PASS WITH NOTES** (not bare **PASS**): no assembly code; safe-unknown topic taxonomy deferred to R3.4; manifest parser and `approved_at` policy remain **SAFE UNKNOWN**; `metadata.snapshot_id` echo helper deferred to R3.5.

**FAIL** would apply if mapping allowed quality fabrication, evidence/snapshot merge, silent completeness, or missing sections in the matrix — **none apply**.
