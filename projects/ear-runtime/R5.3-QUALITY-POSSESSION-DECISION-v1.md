## Rationale

1. **Four levels chartered** — L0–L3 registered in `CANONICAL_QUALITY_POSSESSION_REGISTRY` aligned with EAR-OPENCART-QUALITY-MAPPING (SC-R5.3-01).
2. **Mandatory disambiguation** — R3 `package_quality_level: 0` documented as uncertified placeholder; R5 certified 0–3 as assessed claim via `claim_stage` constants (SC-R5.3-02; Q-INV-R5-01).
3. **Downgrade/inflation policy stated** — downgrade allowed; inflation forbidden — conceptual only, algorithms deferred R5.7 (SC-R5.3-03).
4. **R5 sole certifier** — only R5 assigns `certified_quality_level` on Validate pass; R3 enforces candidate 0; R4 freezes published claim (SC-R5.3-04).
5. **R5.2 alignment confirmed** — Quality category owns level assignment; Possession category owns section adequacy; no ownership conflict.
6. **Forbidden scope absent** — no scoring, percentages, thresholds, engine, Publish, or R5-V-* rules in module.

**PASS WITH NOTES** (not bare **PASS**): `CANDIDATE_PACKAGE_QUALITY_LEVEL` duplicates `handoff_contract.py` constant (values identical — dedup optional R5.7); downgrade/max-level derivation algorithms **SAFE UNKNOWN** until R5.7; `target_certify_level` aggregate field deferred R5.5.

**FAIL** would apply if model included certification algorithms, omitted R3/R5 disambiguation, or modified R2/R3 contracts — **none apply**.
