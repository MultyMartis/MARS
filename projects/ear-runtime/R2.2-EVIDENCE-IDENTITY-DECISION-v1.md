---

## Rationale

1. **Normative alignment** — [EAR-EVIDENCE-PACKAGE-v1.md](../../shared/external-access-runtime/EAR-EVIDENCE-PACKAGE-v1.md), [R2-CHARTER-v1.md](R2-CHARTER-v1.md), and [R2-IMPLEMENTATION-CHARTER-v1.md](R2-IMPLEMENTATION-CHARTER-v1.md) agree on required identity: `acquisition_id`, `site_ref`, `connector_class`.
2. **Model alignment** — R2.1 `EvidenceIdentity` implements exactly that tuple; excludes `evidence_id` and `package_quality_level` per charter.
3. **Traceability** — [R1-CONTRACT-MAPPING-v1.md](R1-CONTRACT-MAPPING-v1.md) documents origins: runtime `acquisition_id`, mapped `site_ref` / `connector_class` from R1.2 JSON.
4. **Boundaries** — Evidence identity ≠ snapshot identity (`snapshot_id` at R3) documented with architecture evidence.
5. **SAFE UNKNOWN discipline** — `evidence_id`, hybrid merge identity extensions, and 1:N `acquisition_id` → `snapshot_id` policy remain unresolved by design — no invention required for R2.2 pass.

**PASS WITH NOTES** (not bare **PASS**): identity **binding and validation are not implemented** yet; R1.6 mock pipeline still uses legacy field names; mock persist `build_mock_acquisition_id()` is separate from evidence assembly — must converge at R2.7.

**FAIL** would apply if `EvidenceIdentity` added forbidden fields, omitted required contract fields, or charters contradicted the model — **none apply**.
