| Field | Value |

| **Explicitly not passed by this decision** | HandoffContract code module; HandoffRecord serialization; generator; snapshot builder; per-section OpenCart mapping implementation; quarantine persist; live acquisition |

---

## Rationale

1. **Authoritative boundary exists** — [R2-IMPLEMENTATION-CHARTER-v1.md](R2-IMPLEMENTATION-CHARTER-v1.md) § Evidence → Snapshot Boundary and E-06 `HandoffContract` are expanded into concrete inputs, outputs, and invariants without contradicting EAR-EVIDENCE-PACKAGE-v1, EAR-SNAPSHOT-CONTRACT-v1, or EAR-SNAPSHOT-LIFECYCLE-v1.
2. **Separation enforced** — evidence ≠ snapshot; evidence survives handoff; snapshot identity ≠ evidence identity; R2 pass ≠ R3 pass ≠ R5 pass ≠ Publish — mapped to HO-INV-* and carries R2.4 VAL-INV-* and R2.5 Q-INV-* without inventing new policy.
3. **Identity continuity aligned** — R2.2 traceability chain extended through handoff; drift prevention ID-DRIFT-01–ID-DRIFT-06; no `evidence_id` introduction.
4. **Quarantine handoff integrated** — R2.5 notes N-R2.5-01 satisfied: quarantine path H-IN-08 documented; consumer exposure forbidden (HO-FORBID-07, HO-INV-15).
5. **R2.4 recommendation satisfied** — R-R2.4-08: R2 pass **necessary not sufficient** for R3/R5 (HO-INV-05, HO-INV-06).
6. **No implementation scope creep** — review only; no generator, validator, persistence, snapshot assembly, or network.

**PASS WITH NOTES** (not bare **PASS**): handoff **not implemented in code** (expected at this milestone); per-section OpenCart field mapping detail **deferred to R3 charter**; `HandoffRecord` format **SAFE UNKNOWN**; R5 vs R3 ordering **SAFE UNKNOWN**; hybrid multi-package handoff **Future** R2.8.

**FAIL** would apply if handoff permitted evidence/snapshot merge, evidence deletion on handoff, quality fabrication from evidence, consumer quarantine exposure, or identity conflation (`snapshot_id` on evidence) — **none apply**.
