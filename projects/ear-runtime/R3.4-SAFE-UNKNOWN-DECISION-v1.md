## Rationale

1. **Authoritative propagation contract** — consolidates scattered seeds from R3 Implementation Charter, R3.3 fallbacks, R2.6 handoff, and EAR-OPENCART-SNAPSHOT-SPEC into one normative document.
2. **Taxonomy complete** — ten categories cover missing evidence, partial/failed acquisition, scope, unverified claims, deferred L2, and contract gaps; SU-CAT-08 reserved for R5 only.
3. **Propagation matrix actionable** — connector, scope, artifact, section, and capability sources map to canonical topics with exact behavior.
4. **Entry semantics locked** — `topic`, `reason`, `impact` required; `unblock_hint` optional; forbidden and future fields documented; `SnapshotSafeUnknownEntry` assessed **sufficient**.
5. **Rules complete for L1 scope** — SU-R3-01–20 plus SU-FORBID-01–05 prevent silent omission, fabrication, and quality inflation.
6. **Ownership clean** — gap detection split R2/R3; representation R3; quality interpretation R5; publish R5+R4; consumer disclosure R4 — no collisions.

**PASS WITH NOTES** (not bare **PASS**): no propagation code; `category` not in R3.1 model (derivable from topic/reason); `unblock` vs `unblock_hint` persist alias deferred to R3.5; structured warning→artifact links **SAFE UNKNOWN** until R2.8.

**FAIL** would apply if contract allowed silent gaps, quality inflation, fabricated replacements, or insufficient entry model — **none apply**.
