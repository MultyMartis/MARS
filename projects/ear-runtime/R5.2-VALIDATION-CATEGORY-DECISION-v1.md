---

## Rationale

1. **Seven categories chartered** — Identity, Structure, Possession, Quality, Redaction, Readiness, Consistency registered in `CANONICAL_VALIDATION_CATEGORY_REGISTRY` (SC-R5.2-01).
2. **Single R5 owner per category** — `ValidationCategoryOwnership.owner == "R5"` for all seven (SC-R5.2-02).
3. **Complementary R2/R3 checks documented** — ownership matrix and overlap prevention table; no duplication as certification (SC-R5.2-03).
4. **Terminology preserved** — categories are ownership contracts, not validators; distinct from R3 assembly eligibility and R2 structural gate (SC-R5.2-04; N-R5-07).
5. **R5.1 alignment confirmed** — `ValidationCategoryRef.category` values match registry `category_id` constants; no model conflict on `ValidationFinding`, `ValidationSummary`, or `ValidationResult`.
6. **Forbidden scope absent** — no scoring, weights, engine, Publish, or R5-V-* rules in module.

**PASS WITH NOTES** (not bare **PASS**): category constants duplicated in `validation_result_models.py` (values identical — dedup optional at R5.7); per-category summary refs on aggregate deferred R5.5/R5.7; category dispatch order **SAFE UNKNOWN**.

**FAIL** would apply if categories included validation logic, omitted ownership exclusions, or absorbed R3 assembly / R4 Publish — **none apply**.
