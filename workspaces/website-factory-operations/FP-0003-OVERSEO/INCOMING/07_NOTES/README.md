# 07_NOTES — Operator Notes Intake

**Factory Project:** FP-0003 — OVERSEO  
**Purpose:** Capture coordination notes, Telegram excerpts, and operator/client observations.

---

## Accepts

- Telegram excerpts relevant to scope or design intent
- Meeting notes and call summaries
- Coordinator ↔ operator handoff notes
- Olga thoughts on homepage direction, content, or constraints
- Open questions and follow-up lists
- Intake triage notes
- Links to external discussion threads *(sanitized)*

---

## Does not accept

- Authoritative Factory declarations *(use passport / PROJECT-STATUS after formal decisions)*
- Approved design targets
- Secrets or credentials

---

## Intake discipline

- Notes are **source evidence** — not approval.
- Design phase must synthesize notes into structured brief; notes alone do not authorize frontend.
