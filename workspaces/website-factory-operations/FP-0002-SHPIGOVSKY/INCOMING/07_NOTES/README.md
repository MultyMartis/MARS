# 07_NOTES — Operator Notes Intake

**Purpose:** Capture coordination notes, meeting summaries, and operator observations.

---

## Accepts

- Meeting notes and call summaries
- Coordinator ↔ operator handoff notes
- Open questions and follow-up lists
- Intake triage notes
- Links to external discussion threads *(sanitized)*

---

## Does not accept

- Authoritative Factory declarations *(use Playbook 04 after onboarding)*
- ADR decisions *(use [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md))*
