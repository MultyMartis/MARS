How engagement works

Ordered steps from first contact to delivery — structure extracted from Triumph V6 order-steps--process flow; aligned with VF_PROCESS_STEP_TIMELINE. Neutral placeholders only.

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Share contact details and the window when your team is available to coordinate.

  2. 2

    Describe the scope

    Outline load type, access constraints, locations, and any timing requirements upfront.

  3. 3

    Confirm terms

    Align on capacity fit, cost estimate, and dispatch timing before work begins on site.

  4. 4

    Execute on site

    Crew arrives per agreed parameters — work proceeds against the confirmed scope and schedule.
